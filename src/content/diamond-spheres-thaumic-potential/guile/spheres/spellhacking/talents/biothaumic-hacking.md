**Prerequisites:** Spellhacking sphere.

You can use hack magic on a creature itself rather than a magic item or magical effect. When doing so, the (hack) talent can interact with supernatural and extraordinary effects, as well as qualities of the creature that are inherent to their race (although its effects may vary, up to GM discretion), such as hacking a tiefling’s fire resistance with Repurpose Resilience, or a dragon’s breath weapon with Blast Tuning.

When determining the DC of the hack attempt, it is equal to 11 + the creature’s Hit Dice.
