**Prerequisite:** Spellhacking sphere.

**Benefit:** Whenever you use hack magic as a standard action or longer, you can hack two targets instead of one–both targets must still be in range. If you apply the same (hack) talent to both targets and the talent allows you to redistribute a bonus (or other statistic) that the target grants or possesses, you can distribute the bonuses between both hacked targets. Any bonuses redistributed this way must still be valid to the new target, and they must be the same type of item (such as a weapon or shield).
For example, you can use Shift Enchantment to remove a +1 enhancement bonus from a +2 longsword and grant it to a +3 greatsword.

If you possess the Repurpose Activated Item talent and apply it to both targets when hacking this way, you can use either object’s associated school of magic or magic sphere when choosing the new function granted to the hacked items.
