**Target:** A magical item, see text.

You allow the item to self-perpetuate in some aspect, depending on the target.

**Magical Rebound:** If the hacked item is a weapon that can be thrown, it can return back to its wielder. Whenever the hacked item is thrown, it teleports back to the wielder’s hand after it resolves its attack (or similar action). If the wielder does not have a free hand, it drops in front of the ground of the wielder. This is a teleportation effect.

**Munitions Preservation:** If the hacked item is a ranged weapon that requires ammunition, it can supply itself magically. The target object can magically manifest basic ammunition when it is reloaded, and treats its attacks as magical. Alternatively, if the hacked weapon is used to fire a piece of magical ammunition, it does not break on a missed attack, and teleports to the weapon 1 round after (usually in a quiver or similar container dedicated to storing the weapon’s ammunition).

At 6 ranks in this sphere’s associated skill, magical ammunition that would normally be destroyed after delivering its damage instead can last one additional successful attack, plus an additional attack at 12 ranks in the associated skill and every 6 ranks thereafter. Once such a piece of magical ammunition is used once, the magical wear and tear is obvious and becomes worthless.
