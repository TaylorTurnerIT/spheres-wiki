**Prerequisites:** Subterfuge sphere.

You can spend a standard action and touch a creature to have your worn equipment instantly change to what the touched creature is wearing (or specific equipment if you wish, although you must be aware of said equipment in this case), although this does not retain any of the magical properties the equipment may have possessed. The equipment otherwise lasts for as long as they remain on you.

You can also choose to reveal this as a plan, with the assumption being that you find time to be able to be in contact with the creature.
