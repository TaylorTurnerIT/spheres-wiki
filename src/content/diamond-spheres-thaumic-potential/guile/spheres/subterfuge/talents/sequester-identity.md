**Prerequisites:** Associated ranks 5, Subterfuge sphere (Mimic Style).

When you use Mimic Style, you can spend 1 use of skill leverage to attempt to mimic the touched creature even moreso. The creature must succeed at a Will saving throw or have their entire identity become a unique (disguise) talent you have access to. Regardless of the saving throw result, the creature is unaware of the attempt, but increases the skill leverage cost by 1 on any subsequent attempts made during the next 24 hours. You can only have a single sequestered identity at one time, but it otherwise lasts indefinitely until you sequester another identity.
The disguise is a flawless imitation of the touched creature, –including their facial features, vocal cues and mannerisms, and identifying features– granting a +10 bonus to Disguise checks to impersonate the touched creature, and you take no penalties to the check due to the creature being a different race, age, size, or gender.
Due to its supernatural nature, this disguise does not require using a disguise kit to apply (although it is a supernatural polymorph effect).

You can also choose to steal the creature’s features for your own when you use this talent, with a failed save resulting in the touched creature’s features, voice, and mannerisms becoming completely unremarkable. This lasts for 1 hour per associated skill rank, after which you lose access to the disguise. A successful save results in the creature being aware of the attempt, which is likely to make them hostile.
When using this function of Sequester Identity and you possess at least 10 ranks in this sphere’s associated skill, you can spend an additional use of skill leverage to have the touched creature, on a failed save, become transformed into a helpless, inanimate piece of clothing for the duration. The target can still perceive its surroundings while transformed and take purely mental actions (or be targeted by spells that affect their mind), but it cannot speak or move. This specific function cannot be used against hostile creatures.

You can no longer use this talent’s unique (disguise) option, but any creature that dons this piece of clothing is transformed as the (disguise) option, and reverts to its natural form when it is removed. If the creature or clothing suffers any amount of damage, this effect ends immediately.
