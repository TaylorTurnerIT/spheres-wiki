---
id: essence-empowerment
name: Essence Empowerment
---
You may use the essence that flows within your body to empower your magical effects. You may invest non-temporary essence in this boon. Whenever you use a sphere effect, you may burn essence invested in this boon to gain a competence bonus to your caster level for that sphere effect equal to the number of points of essence burned. If the sphere effect has a duration longer than instantaneous, you cannot recover the burned essence until the sphere effect's duration has expired.
