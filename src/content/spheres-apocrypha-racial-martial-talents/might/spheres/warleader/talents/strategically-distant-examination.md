---
id: "strategically-distant-examination"
name: "Strategically Distant Examination"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
---
When you are benefiting from cover or concealment against at least one opponent and you are not within the threatened area of any opponent, increase morale bonuses granted by your tactics and shouts by 1 for every 10 ranks in Diplomacy you possess (minimum 1) until the start of your next turn.
