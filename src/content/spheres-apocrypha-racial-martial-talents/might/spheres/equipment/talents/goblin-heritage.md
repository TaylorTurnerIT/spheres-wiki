You gain proficiency with the the brutal bola, dogslicer, garrote, horsechopper, light flail, machete, sap, shortbow, spiked chain, swordbreaker dagger and torch. In addition, when you wield a torch as a weapon it is considered a club that deals an additional 1d4 fire damage on a successful hit if the torch is lit.
