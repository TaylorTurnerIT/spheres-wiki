**Prerequisites:** Destruction sphere (Energy Strike [strike])

You may only use this blast type in conjunction with the energy strike blast shape. The blast deals damage of one or more types that the attack corresponding with energy strike deals, chosen when the attack is made. Add the damage of the attack and the destructive blast together before applying DR and energy resistance. This blast cannot deal ability damage.

This blast ignores spell resistance, spell turning, and can penetrate a globe of invulnerability, antimagic field, or other forms of antimagic, and is not treated as a spell or magical for the purpose of bypassing damage reduction, damaging incorporeal creatures, or for creatures or classes that gain a bonus to saving throws against magic unless the corresponding weapon is considered any of those things.

Magnifying blast counts as its own blast type group for the purpose of abilities which select a specific blast type group.
