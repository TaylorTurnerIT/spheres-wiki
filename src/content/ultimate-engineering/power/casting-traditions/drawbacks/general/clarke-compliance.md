*Your magic is created or assisted through an external advanced mechanism, and as such is subject to many of the same limitations.*

Your magic sphere effects are treated as both magical and mechanical for effects, whichever is more disadvantageous (such as for being countered, disabled, disrupted, resisted, or suppressed by abilities and effects that would interact with a magic or mechanical effect). Treat the gizmo level of your magic sphere effects as being equal to your magic skill bonus for the purposes of effects that depend on gizmo level.

Creatures in contact with an active Tinker sphere gizmo (or other reasonably technological or "invented" item, subject to GM discretion) receive a +2 circumstance bonus on saving throws against your magic sphere effects.
