---
name: "Demolishing Chemistry"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
---

Alchemical formulae that would only deal half damage to objects due to dealing energy damage or being ranged attacks instead deal full damage, and deal an additional 2 damage per rank in Craft (alchemy) to unattended objects.

If the formulae destroys a wall or piece of terrain, it can affect creatures or objects on the other side, although damage dealt to the barrier is subtracted from any damage done to the creatures or objects.
