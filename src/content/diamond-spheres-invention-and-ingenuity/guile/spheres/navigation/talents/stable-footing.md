  - "pathing"
---

Allies within the area of your *pathing* reduce any forced movement they are subject to from physical sources (such as a bull rush combat maneuver) by 5 feet + 5 feet per 10 associated ranks.

Such allies can spend an immediate action to double this forced movement reduction–if this would reduce the forced movement to 0 feet, the ally can move 5 feet as part of this action (which does not provoke attacks of opportunity).
