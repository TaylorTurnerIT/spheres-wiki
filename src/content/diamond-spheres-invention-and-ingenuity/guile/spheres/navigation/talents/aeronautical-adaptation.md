You may select the following acclimation benefits:

- **Cyclone-Sturdy:** While a creature is flying within an area of Wind of severity level 2 or higher, they gain a +5-foot circumstance bonus to their fly speed, +5 feet per category above severity level 2. Additionally, as a free action usable once per round, they can turn up to 180 degrees without spending any movement with a successful DC 25 Fly check.
If you possess the (aerial) package, this fly speed bonus doubles.
- **Tempest-tost:** Acclimated targets treat themselves as being one size category larger for the purposes of wind effects, and halve any penalty on Perception checks caused by rain or high winds. Also, acclimated targets gain a +2 competence bonus to saving throws against effects that cause the deafened condition, that have the electricity or sonic descriptor, or that deal electricity damage.
If you possess the (aerial) package, acclimated targets also gain electricity resistance equal to the (aerial) package’s associated ranks.

**Associated Feats:** Storm-Lashed (*Adventurer’s Guide*), Wind Rider (*Blood of the Ancients*), Wingover (*Pathfinder RPG Bestiary*).
