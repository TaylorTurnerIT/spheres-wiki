When you use dredge, you can choose one of the following options to apply to the area:

**Rigid Terrain:** Creatures within the dredged area suffer a -1 penalty to AC and Reflex saves and are unable to benefit from dodge bonuses. This penalty increases by 1 for every 6 associated ranks you possess.

**Yielding Terrain:** Creatures within the dredged area gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC and Reflex saves and retain any dodge bonus to AC whenever they would lose their Dexterity to AC. This bonus increases by 1 for every 6 associated ranks you possess.
