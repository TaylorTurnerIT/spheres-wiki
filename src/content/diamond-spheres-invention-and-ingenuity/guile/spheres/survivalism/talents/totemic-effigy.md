**Prerequisites:** Survivalism sphere ((dredge) and (harvest) package).

**Benefit:** Whenever you dredge an area as a standard action or longer, you can utilize a talisman as part of it, expending it in the process. Creatures within the dredged area gain the benefits of the talisman as if they were wearing it–this does not count towards the amount of talisman benefits a creature can benefit from. Effects that are dependent on perceiving a creature wearing the talisman instead function as long as such a creature is within the dredged area.
