If you possess the (dredge) package, you can dredge in such a way that it wears down those within the area. Any creature that moves through the dredged area suffers 1d6 nonlethal damage for every 5 feet of movement spent entering or moving through the area (this excludes forced movement). A successful Fortitude save made at the end of the total movement halves this damage.

Additionally, if a (ground) talent applied to the dredged area would normally deal lethal damage, you can choose for it to deal nonlethal damage instead.

If you possess the (harvest) package, you can harvest in such a way that it is near harmless, allowing you to harvest component’s from living creatures–generally requiring them to be unconscious or willing. A specific component harvested this way cannot be harvested from the same creature for 1 week after this time.

A component integral to the function of the creature may be difficult or impossible to harvest harmlessly, up to GMs discretion (for example, harvesting blood may be uncomfortable for an animal and may require a successful Handle Animal check, whereas harvesting a creature’s heart would be impossible).
