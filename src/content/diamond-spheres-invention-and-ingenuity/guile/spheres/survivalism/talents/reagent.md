**Prerequisites:** Associated skill 5 ranks

**Refinement DC:** 25

You harvest the parts of a creature that are highly magical, such as a unicorn’s horn or pixie dust. A refined reagent can be expended as part of casting a spell or magical sphere effect to improve it, allowing the user to choose one of the following benefits to add to the effect:

- The effect’s range increases by 10 feet (as long as it has a range greater than touch), or its area by 5 feet. These increase by 5 feet for every 5 associated ranks you possess.
- The user rolls any concentration checks made as part of creating or maintaining the effect twice and takes the highest result.
- The effect gains a +1 alchemical bonus to its MSB and MSD, +1 per 10 associated ranks. The user can expend a reagent when attempting a dispel check made as part of hack magic to confer this bonus to the attempt.

**Potency:** The refined reagent gains two additional uses before it becomes expended.

**Talisman:** A reagent talisman grants its wearer a +2 alchemical bonus to concentration checks and a +1 alchemical bonus to their MSB and MSD. Additionally, once per hour the wearer can grant themselves a +1 alchemical bonus to their caster level as part of casting a spell or magical sphere effect.

**Potency:** The bonuses granted by the reagent talisman increases by 1.
