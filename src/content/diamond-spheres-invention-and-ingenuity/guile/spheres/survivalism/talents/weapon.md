**Refinement DC:** 20.

You can refine the jaws, claws, or similar natural weapon from a creature, which can be setup to be worn or even supplement pre-existing weapons. A refined weapon grants the wearer a bite, claw, or gore attack, depending on the refined component (although other natural attacks may be able to be refined, up to GMs discretion). This is a primary attack that deals 1d6 damage.
Alternatively, a refined weapon can supplement a natural weapon of a kind a creature already possesses, increasing the damage die of the natural weapon by one step (this is not a size increase).

**Potency:** The granted natural attack deals 1d8 damage, and the penalty associated with making a secondary natural attack with the refined weapon is reduced to -2 (instead of -5).

**Talisman:** A weapon talisman’s functions as armor spikes and the wearer is proficient with these armor spikes. A creature that suffers damage from these armor spikes also suffers 1 bleed damage. This bleed damage stacks itself (maximum up to your operative modifier) and with other sources.

These armor spikes are treated as both manufactured and natural weapons for the purposes of spells and effects that would affect them. A creature that hits the wearer with an unarmed attack or natural weapon suffers damage as though they were struck by the armor spikes.

**Potency:** The armor spikes weapon damage die increases to 1d8, and the bleed damage increases to 3.
