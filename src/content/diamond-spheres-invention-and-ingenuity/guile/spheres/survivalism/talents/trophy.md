**Refinement DC:** 15 + the creature’s CR.

You can harvest the most iconic part of the creature (generally the head), functioning as a harvested trophy (*Ultimate Wilderness* © 2017, Paizo Inc), although it is considered to be preserved as part of the time spent to harvest it. Unlike a normal component, it possesses a market value as normal for a trophy.

A refined trophy can be used to outwit a creature by revealing it to them, as long as the CR of the creature that the component originates from is at least 3 higher than that of the outwitted creature, or 1 higher if the outwitted creature is trained in the Knowledge skill required to recall information about the creature. Once a trophy has been used to outwit a creature this way, it cannot be used on the same creature again.

**Talisman:** A trophy talisman’s wearer can assess the proficiency of creature’s of the component’s associated creature type as part of the same action to recall information about such creatures, and they can do so without having to observe such a creature for at least 3 rounds. Additionally, the wearer gains a +3 insight bonus to checks made to assess the proficiency of creature’s of the component’s associated creature type.
