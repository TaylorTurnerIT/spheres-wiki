**Refinement DC:** 15

You can refine the hide or plumage of a creature, which functions as a disguise kit with a number of uses equal to your operative modifier (minimum 1). When a use is expended from the refined pattern, the Disguise check attempt suffers no penalty when attempting to Disguise different materials, add or cover fur, feathers, or scales, or disguise as a substantially different body type. Additionally, the penalty is halved when attempting to disguise as a different size category or the number or arrangement of limbs.

The refined pattern can also be used to disguise as a creature of the component’s associated creature type, although doing so incurs a -6 penalty if it is a different creature type than the disguised creature. A creature trained in the skill used to identify the creature you have disguised as can be used in place of a Perception check when attempting to foil the disguise.

**Potency:** The refined pattern gains twice as many uses, and when used, removes the penalty associated with attempting to disguise as a different size category, the number or arrangement of limbs, or as a creature of the component’s associated creature type. Additionally, a creature wearing a disguise created using a refined pattern can use your associated skill ranks instead of their Bluff ranks when attempting to create a diversion.

**Talisman:** The pattern talisman’s wearer naturally draws attention to themselves, gaining a +3 circumstance bonus to checks that make creatures distracted or impressed towards them (such as an Intimidate check to pose a menace, a Perform check to entertain, or any impressive display of skill), which increases by 1 for every 5 ranks in the associated skill you possess.
