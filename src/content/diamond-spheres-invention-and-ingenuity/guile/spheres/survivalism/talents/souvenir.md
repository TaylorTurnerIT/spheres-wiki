**Refinement DC:** 15.

You can refine an easily recognizable part of a creature, like the tooth of a dragon.

A refined souvenir acts as a beacon of inspiration for those that can see it, granting a +2 morale bonus to ability checks and skill checks attempted against or in regard to creatures of the component’s associated creature type to all creatures that can see the souvenir. These bonuses increase by 1 for every 5 associated ranks you possess.

**Potency:** Creatures that can see the souvenir also gain a +1 morale bonus to all saving throws against any ability’s originating from creatures of the component’s associated creature type, +1 per 7 associated ranks you possess.

**Talisman:** A souvenir talisman’s wearer gains a +1 competence bonus to weapon attack and damage rolls made against creature’s of the component’s associated creature type, +1 per 7 associated ranks you possess.
