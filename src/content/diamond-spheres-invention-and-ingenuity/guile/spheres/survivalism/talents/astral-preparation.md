**Prerequisites:** Survivalism sphere, associated skill 5 ranks.

If you possess the (dredge) package, your dredged areas are considered force effects, allowing their effects to be subject to incorporeal creatures.

If you possess the (harvest) package, you can choose to take twice as long when harvesting a component from a creature to gain a component that the creature did not originally possess (but not components that have already been harvested). You can only harvest a single specific component from a creature this way (meaning you cannot harvest two hearts from an ooze), unless you could normally do so (such as the Efficient Preparation talent).

You can also harvest components from creatures that do not leave remains, and instead do so where the creature died.

**Special:** If you possess the Humane Survivalist talent, this allows you to harmlessly harvest components that would normally be innately fatal to the creature (such as their heart), as well as allowing you to harvest components harmlessly from non-corporeal creatures.
