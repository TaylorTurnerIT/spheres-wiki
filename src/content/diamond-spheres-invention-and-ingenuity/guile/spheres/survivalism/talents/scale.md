**Refinement DC:** 20

You refine the scales (or similarly hardy plates) of a creature, which are usually then woven into armor or clothing, granting a +1 natural armor bonus to AC, +1 per 5 ranks you possess in the associated skill.

**Potency:** The scales have been so refined that they become partially reflective. The natural armor bonus increases by +1, and the wearer has a 20% chance to cause any ray attacks that target them to be deflected, as if it had missed. Once such an attack has been successfully deflected, it loses this reflective quality until it is specially polished for 10 minutes.

**Talisman:** The wearer of a scale talisman gains a +1 enhancement bonus to any natural armor that they possess. A creature without natural armor has an effective natural armor bonus of +0.

**Potency:** The bonus granted by the scale talisman increases by 1 + 1 per 5 associated skill ranks you possess.
