**Prerequisites:** Survivalism sphere, associated 5 ranks.

If you have the (dredge) package, whenever you successfully damage a creature with a melee attack (or a ranged attack if you have the Expansive Preparation talent), you can dredge a square occupied by the damaged creature as a free action–if the attack was a critical hit or killed the creature, you dredge a 10-foot radius centered on the damaged creature. These dredged squares last for a number of rounds equal to your operative modifier (minimum 1).

If you have the (harvest) package, whenever you kill a creature, you can harvest it as a free action (as if you had taken 1 minute to do so), and refine one such component as an immediate action. You can also harvest a corpse as a move action.

If you have the Humane Survivalist talent, you can harvest a component harmlessly from a creature as a standard action or as part of knocking a creature unconscious–if the creature is conscious and unwilling, you can do so with a successful steal combat maneuver or a DC 20 Sleight of Hand check (as if it were a small object). After this action, you can refine such a component as an immediate action.
