**Refinement DC**: 15

You refine the fur or coat of a creature by attaching it to your armor or clothing, or as a separate piece entirely. Depending on the creature that the coat comes from, it can either be conspicuous or inconspicuous–this is generally up to GM discretion, but a creature that is trained in Stealth usually possesses an inconspicuous coat. If the coat is considered both conspicuous and inconspicuous, choose one when you refine it.

A refined coat grants its wearer a +2 circumstance bonus to Stealth checks (if inconspicuous) or Diplomacy, Handle Animal, and Perform checks (if conspicuous), increasing by +1 per 5 associated ranks you possess. This bonus to Stealth checks is doubled in terrain that the creature would naturally inhabit.

**Potency:** The wearer of an inconspicuous refined coat gains concealment (a 20% miss chance) from creatures more than 5 feet away from them, but only if they move no more than half their base speed–if they exceed half their base speed, they lose this concealment until the start of their next turn.

Whenever the wearer of a conspicuous refined coat would be subject to a visual effect, there is a 20% chance that the effect fails, as if they did not observe it. This only applies once per round per effect, and is foiled if the wearer purposefully observes the effect.

**Talisman:** The coat talisman’s wearer gains increased spryness, gaining a +1 alchemical bonus to Reflex saving throws, and a +2 alchemical bonus to Dexterity-based ability checks and skill checks. These bonuses increase by +1 per 6 associated ranks you possess.
