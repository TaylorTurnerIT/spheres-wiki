---
id: sleet-step
name: "Sleet Step"
system: power
type: talent
sphere: weather
tier: basic
tags: ["mantle", "snow"]
---

When a creature bearing your mantle is in an area of Cold and Precipitation of severity 2 or higher, they can move across ice and snow without penalty, and do not need to attempt Acrobatics checks to run or charge on ice. Additionally, such creatures bearing your mantle gain a +10-foot circumstance bonus to their land speed on ice and snow, +5-feet for each category above light snow.

Additionally, whenever such a creature moves, they can choose to leave behind a flurry of snow in every square they move out of (or squares of their choice), causing such areas to become difficult terrain for a number of rounds equal to the current Cold severity of the area–within an area of Cold and Precipitation of severity 4 or higher, this difficult terrain is treated as heavy snow (costing 4 squares of movement to enter).
