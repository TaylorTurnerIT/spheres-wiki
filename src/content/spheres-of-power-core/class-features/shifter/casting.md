Combine spheres and talents to create magical effects.

- **Caster Level:** Mid-Caster (3/4 level).
- **Bonus Talents:** 2 bonus talents + casting tradition when first gaining feature.
- **Spell Pool:** Class level + casting ability modifier (min 1). Replenish after 8 hours rest.
- **Talent Progression:** 3/4 talents per level.
