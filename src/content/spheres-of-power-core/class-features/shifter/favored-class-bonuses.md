- **Aasimar:** +1/2 bonus to all Handle Animal checks.
- **Elf:** +1/3 to natural armor bonus while using Alteration form (other than Blank).
- **Half-orc:** Gain +1/6 of a bestial trait.
- **Human:** +1/2 bonus on Diplomacy and Intimidate checks to change a creature’s attitude.
- **Orc:** Choose one natural attack from bestial traits. Increase die size by 1 step every 6 times gained.
- **Skinwalker:** Gain 1/6th of an Alteration sphere talent.
