Suffer no harm from hot/cold environments (-50 to 140°F). Equipment protected. No protection from fire/cold damage or lack of air.

- **Flight:** Acclimated to high altitudes (treat zone as 1 lower; 2 lower at level 10).
- **Home in Water:** Immune to ocean pressure.
