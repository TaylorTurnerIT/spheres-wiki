---
name: "Misleading Magic"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "utility"
---

You can modify a magical effect as you produce it in cosmetic and misleading ways. Spells, magic items you activate, and the effects of magic spheres are all subject to this talent. Observers can realize the true effect regardless of how you modified it with this talent by succeeding at a single Perception or Spellcraft check against DC 15 + your ranks in the associated skill. You make one of the following changes, plus another change per 5 ranks you have in the associated skill.

- You can alter the apparent method of casting. You can make an item’s effect seem to come from you, or make your own magic seem to come from an item you have. You must still take any actions required by the effect, but that action can be misleading (such as making a verbal component seem to be a command word or vice versa). You can also imitate the magical signature of another kind of spellcaster (such as divine or psychic or a specific casting tradition, or a specific spellcaster whose signature you know).
- You can make purely cosmetic alterations to the spell to make it resemble another spell of the same school or using different talents from the same sphere, provided both have similar targets or areas. The GM might rule that some effects are too different to be disguised as each other. Doing so makes it harder to identify the spell or effect with Knowledge (arcana) or Spellcraft, increasing the DC by 5. Anyone who fails the check by 4 or less mistakenly identifies the spell or effect as the one you made it resemble. If the effect is disguised by multiple means, the one with the highest DC overrides the effects of other means of disguise.

**Associated Feat:** Stylized Spell (Pathfinder Roleplaying Game: Ultimate Intrigue).
