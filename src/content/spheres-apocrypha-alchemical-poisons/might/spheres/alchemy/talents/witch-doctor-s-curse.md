**Prerequisites:** Craft (alchemy) 5 ranks, Alchemy sphere ((poison) package, Food Poisoning, Potent Poison).

You may expend martial focus when creating a poison to create a curse poison. A curse poison is a magical effect and a curse: It is permanent and can only be cured by effects that specifically cure curses such as the Break Enchantment talent of the Life sphere or the remove curse spell.

**Author's Note:** This talent increases the duration of a (toxin)'s negative effects, not any effect of a (toxin).
