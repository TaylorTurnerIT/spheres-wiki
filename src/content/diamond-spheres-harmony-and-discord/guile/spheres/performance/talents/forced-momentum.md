---
name: "Forced Momentum"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "dance"
---

Whenever a dancer attempts a bull rush combat maneuver, the dancer may use your ranks in this package's associated skill instead of their base attack bonus when calculating their CMB.

Whenever an opponent would enter a dancer's threatened area (and they move at least 10 feet when doing so) or miss a melee attack against a dancer while within the dancer’s natural reach, the dancer can attempt a bull rush combat maneuver against them as an immediate action; this attempt can be made against creatures up to two sizes larger than the dancer. An opponent that is bull rushed while moving may choose to continue their movement, spending any remaining movement as normal.

If the creature charged or moved more than 20 feet, the dancer gains a +2 circumstance bonus to the attempt, +1 per 5 ranks in the associated skill.
