---
name: "Rhythmic Refrain"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "instrumental"
  - "lyric"
---

Allies affected by this instrumental or lyric gain a +1 morale bonus to Reflex saves, and a morale bonus to Dexterity-based skill checks and any checks made when taking 10 or 20 equal to half the ranks you possess in the package’s associated skill. The bonus to Reflex saves increases by +1 for every 10 ranks in the package’s associated skill you possess.

When used as an inspiring lyric, it can be expended as part of attempting a Reflex saving throw that reduces the damage on a successful save to grant themselves the evasion ability against the triggering effect–if the creature already possesses evasion, they instead gain the benefits of the improved evasion ability.
