**Prerequisites:** Performance sphere (Combat Juggling), Perform (any) ranks 5.

You increase the number of objects you can juggle by an amount equal to your operative ability modifier.

Additionally, you can juggle creatures up to one size category smaller than you. A creature three size categories smaller than you is treated as one object, a creature two size categories smaller than you is treated as two objects, and a creature one size category smaller than you is treated as three objects. If the creature is unwilling, it requires a successful grapple maneuver made as a standard action, although you can substitute your Perform ranks in a package’s associated skill for your base attack bonus while doing so.

While you are juggling a creature, any single-target attacks that target you or a juggled creature have a 50% chance to target you (if you were not the original target) or another juggled creature (chosen randomly) instead of its original target. Regardless of whether or not a juggled creature is willing, they lose their Dexterity bonus to AC and are considered to be grappled.
