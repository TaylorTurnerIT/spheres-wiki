**Prerequisites:** Performance sphere ((act) package), Perform (act) ranks 10.

You act out a strange and unassuming tale of an individual coming to terms with their mortality, reality, and the community they struggle to connect with. The simple and mundane shifts to the eclectic and bizarre, and eventually… reality becomes a malleable clay within their fingertips.

**Uninitiated (approach):** Your uneventful life has led to you lacking a certain resolve. You suffer 1d4 points of Wisdom burn damage. If you do not (or cannot) suffer this damage, you cannot benefit from this act.

**Glimpse Beyond the Veil (scene, 1 swift action):** You receive a peek behind the curtain of reality for a moment, straining your psyche. You gain immunity to the confused and dumbfounded conditions, in addition to fear effects. Allies within the act’s area may choose to suffer the burn from this act’s approach to gain the benefits of this act’s scenes.

**Transcendent Gaze (scene, 1 standard action):** Your dreams are plagued with esoteric ramblings, and you awaken not quite the same. Allies benefitting from this act gain a glare attack that deals 1d6 untyped damage per 2 associated ranks you possess and 1d2 Wisdom burn damage; a successful Will save halves both sources of damage. This functions as a gaze attack with a range of 60 feet, except that it can only affect a single creature each round.

**Embrace True Reality (finale, 1 standard action):** Your psyche has shattered completely, as you achieve full enlightenment. For the next minute, if an ally benefitting from this act would reduce a creature’s Wisdom score to 0 using their glare attack, the creature must succeed a Will saving throw or become enslaved by the ally permanently, as dominate monster. A creature does not heal ability damage to its Wisdom while enslaved by you, but is brought back to a Wisdom of 1 and can only be released by break enchantment or a similar effect. There can only be a number of creature’s enslaved at one time between all allies benefitting from this act equal to your operative ability modifier.

Alternatively, an ally benefitting from this act can choose to suffer an amount of Wisdom drain equal to half their Wisdom score and dismiss the finale’s effects to gain intense glimpses of the future, gaining a cryptic answer to a single question (as per the divination spell). This ability cannot be used if it would reduce the ally’s Wisdom score to 1 or lower, or if they are immune to ability drain.
