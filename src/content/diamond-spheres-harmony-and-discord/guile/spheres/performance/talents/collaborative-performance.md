---
name: "Collaborative Performance"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
---

If you possess the (act) package, an ally within the act’s area can spend the associated action of a finale to activate it. When the effects of an act’s scenes would end (such as by activating the act’s finale), an ally within the act’s area can spend an immediate action to allow the effects of one of the act’s scenes to linger for 1 round after it would normally end.

If you possess the (dance) package, whenever a dancer moves or makes a 5-foot step, they can move into the space occupied by another dancer of their size or smaller and switch places with them. Both dancers must be willing and be able to move and occupy the spaces they are placed within; this movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity, and does not count towards the other dancer’s movement on their next turn.

If you possess the (lyric) package, you can expend an inspiring lyric you have placed within your lyric range, using your own action to do so, and can choose to confer its benefits to any ally within your lyric range.

If you possess the (instrumental) package, an ally (except yourself) that is trained in Perform (keyboard, oratory, percussion, string, sing, or wind) can spend the same action required to maintain your instrumental either imitating the sound of your instrument or providing backing using their own instrument, removing the requirement for you to maintain the instrumental on your next turn. If the ally’s Hit Dice is lower than your ranks in this package’s associated skill, the instrumental’s effective ranks is lowered to that of the ally’s Hit Dice.
