Enemies affected by this instrumental or lyric suffer a -3 penalty to initiative checks (or reduces their current initiative count by 3, although this does not grant additional actions if they have already acted), and to attack rolls made as part of an attack of opportunity. This penalty increases by 1 for every 5 ranks in this package’s associated Perform skill you possess. If a creature’s initiative would be reduced to 0 by this effect, that creature becomes uncertain for the effect’s duration (Will negates).

When used as an instrumental, affected enemies suffer a penalty to saving throws against effects that would reduce their state of awareness or cause the asleep, exhausted, fascinated, or fatigued condition equal to half the penalty granted by the instrumental. Additionally, each subsequent failed saving throw made against the instrumental decreases the creature’s initiative count by an additional 2.

When used as a discouraging lyric, the target becomes fatigued and suffers a -10-foot penalty to all of their speeds (minimum 5 feet) for the duration. At the start of each subsequent turn, the target either reduces their initiative count by an additional 4 or becomes exhausted for the lyric’s duration (Will negates). If the lyric is expended, the affected creature has their initiative count reduced to 0, and must succeed at a Will saving throw or become dumbfounded for 1 round–a successful save still renders the creature shocked for 1 round.
