name: "Virtuoso’s Challenge"
tier: "advanced"
tags: []
---

**Prerequisites:** Performance sphere, associated ranks 5.

You can use a Performance sphere ability as a standard action (or longer) to start a virtuoso’s challenge, choosing a creature within the sphere ability’s range to declare as your target. The creature must succeed at a Will saving throw or become overwhelmed with the need to perform the same kind of performance that you used to start this ability. This ability has different effect depending on the Performance sphere ability used:

If used as an act, the creature must spend an action each round completing a scene from the act, or if there are no scenes remaining within the act, they must spend a swift action each round performing.

If used as a dance, the creature must spend their move action each turn to move at least half their speed for each round they are affected. You can also choose to spend your standard action and/or swift action on your turn to dance, causing the creature to lose those actions on their following turn. While affected, the creature is considered a dancer for determining how many dancers are within the dance.

If used as a lyric or instrumental, the creature must spend a standard action each round singing or playing an instrument. In the case of a lyric they can use abilities that rely on auditory components as part of doing so, whereas for an instrumental they can use abilities that rely on the use of the instrument.

The creature can attempt a Will saving throw at the end of each turn they are affected to break free from this effect. If the creature suffers damage while under this effect, they receive an immediate saving throw against this effect with a +4 bonus. This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect.
