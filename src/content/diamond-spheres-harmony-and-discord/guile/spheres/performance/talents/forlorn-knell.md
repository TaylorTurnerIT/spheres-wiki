Enemies affected by this instrumental or lyric suffer a -1 penalty to saving throws and a -2 penalty to the first damage roll they make in a turn. These penalties increase by 1 for every 10/5 ranks in the package’s associated skill you possess respectively.

When used as an instrumental, if it is maintained on the subsequent turn from starting it, all affected enemies halve any temporary hit points they would gain (minimum 1).

When used as a discouraging lyric, the target cannot reroll any d20 rolls they attempt (even if the reroll is granted from a different source, beneficial or harmful), and has all morale bonuses they are benefitting from suppressed for as long as the lyric persists–the duration of the bonus (if any) elapses as normal.

The lyric can be expended as a free action usable outside of their turn whenever the affected creature successfully confirms a critical hit to instead treat the attack as a normal hit, or by allowing an attack made against them to automatically succeed (this is chosen before the attack roll is made, although the roll is still attempted to determine if the attack threatens a critical hit).
