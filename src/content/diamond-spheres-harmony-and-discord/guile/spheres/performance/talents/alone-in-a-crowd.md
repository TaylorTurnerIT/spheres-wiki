Usually depicted through the allegory of a wolf pack, you weave a story of the destructively thin line of self-sufficiency and hyper independence.

**Lone Wolf (approach):** All allies within the act’s area that have no ally within 10 feet of them (and are not receiving benefits from aid another) gain a +1 morale bonus to weapon attack and damage rolls against creatures that are not threatened by another ally, increasing by 1 for every 10 ranks in the associated skill you possess.

Alternatively, at any point, an ally benefitting from this act’s approach can choose to make themselves the ‘lone wolf’ for the duration of this act, causing all other allies within the act’s area to only be able to benefit from this act’s approach, regardless of any other completed scenes.

**Friendly Rivalry (scene, 1 standard action):** As long as an ally is benefitting from this act’s approach, the associated morale bonus granted increases by 1 for as long as they perceive another ally and they are also benefiting from this act’s approach.

If there is a lone wolf within the act, the morale bonus granted increases by 1 for the lone wolf.

**Learn From Their Mistakes (scene, 1 swift action):** Whenever an ally within the act’s area fails a saving throw or attack roll by 5 or more, the next ally that attempts the same kind of roll within the next round can roll the attempt twice, taking the highest result. If the ally fails the attempt by 4 or less, the next ally instead gains a +2 morale bonus to the next roll they attempt within the next round.

If there is a lone wolf within the act, they treat any failed attack or saving throw attempted by an ally to have failed by 5 or more for determining their own benefits.

**Culmination (finale, 1 immediate action):** If the act has no lone wolf, all allies within the act’s area gain a +2 morale bonus to AC, as well as to all saving throws and attack and damage rolls for 3 rounds.

If the act has a lone wolf, they gain a +4 morale bonus to the above statistics for 3 rounds for as long as they are threatening an enemy and have no allies within 10 feet of them (and are not receiving any aid another benefits).
