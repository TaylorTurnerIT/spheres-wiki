Attempt a Bluff check against the creature’s demoralize DC. If successful, the target gains a +3 morale bonus to saving throws made against disorientation effects and the impressed condition, and a +1 morale bonus to  Charisma-based skill checks (except for Use Magic Device) and checks made as an impressive display of skill for 10 minutes per rank you possess in the associated skill. For every 5 ranks you possess in the associated skill, these bonuses increase by 1.

If a creature impressed by the original target witnesses the target fail a significant challenge, the target is only granted an additional saving throw against the impressed condition (using your Bluster save DC if no other DC is applicable). If the creature witnesses subsequent failures by the target, they gain a cumulative +2 bonus to the new save.

If your Bluff check fails by 4 or less, its duration is based in 1 minute intervals instead. If the creature fails one of the skill checks improved by this quip by 5 or more during its duration, they must succeed at a Will saving throw or lose all of its benefits and become uncertain for the remaining duration.
