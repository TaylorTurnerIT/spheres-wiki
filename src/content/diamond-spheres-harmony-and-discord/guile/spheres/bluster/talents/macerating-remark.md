name: "Macerating Remark"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "quip"
---

You relentlessly hound the target’s flaws, diminishing their will to fight. If the target fails at its Will save, it suffers 1d6 nonlethal damage, plus 1d6 per 3 ranks you possess in the associated skill you possess. If you invoke the target’s motivation as part of using this quip, the damage die increases to d8s. You can outwit the target as a cost as part of using this quip to add your ranks in this sphere’s associated skill to the damage dealt, as well as halving the damage on a successful save (instead of negating).

Additionally, for 1 round, +1 round per 4 ranks in the associated skill, after you use this quip (regardless of the saving throw result), you can spend an immediate action whenever you succeed an attack roll against the target of the quip or a saving throw against an effect originating from the target of the quip to deal +1d12 nonlethal damage to them–this is multiplied on a critical hit (as long as this ability triggered from an attack roll that resulted in a critical hit). This damage is considered to be from a quip.

You can outwit a creature as a cost as part of using any other quip as a standard action (or longer) to also apply the effects of this quip as part of it.
