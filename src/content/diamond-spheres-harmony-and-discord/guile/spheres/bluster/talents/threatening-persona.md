You adopt this approach as a swift action; when you adopt this approach, choose either braggadocious or menacing. You can change this choice by adopting this approach again.

**Braggadocious:** You use your Bluff instead of Intimidate when attempting to demoralize or pose a menace. If you use Bluff to demoralize, you take no skill check penalty for being smaller than your target, and the demoralized creature cannot make attacks of opportunity against you, although the creature reduces the duration of the shaken condition caused by the demoralization by 1 round for each attack of opportunity it would be denied this way. Additionally, when using Bluff to pose a menace, creatures that are distracted by you cannot make attacks of opportunity against creatures other than you.

**Menacing:** You use your Intimidate instead of Bluff when attempting to feint or create a diversion. If you use Intimidate to feint, you do not suffer a penalty to the attempt due to the creature being nonhumanoid, and halves the penalty if the target is of animal Intelligence (normally reducing it to a -4 penalty). If you successfully use Intimidate to feint or create a diversion, the affected creature(s) must succeed at a Will save or immediately be forced to take a 5-foot step and be unable to take a 5-foot step on their following turn (although they may choose where to take this movement, it will usually be away from you), unless doing so would put them in a harmful square.

**Associated Feats:** Empty Threats (*Pathfinder Player Companion: Blood of the Beast*), Taunt (*Advanced Player’s Guide*), Visceral Threat (*Monster Codex*).
