**Prerequisites:** (dredge) package.

Choose either harmful or beneficial dredge options; once chosen, this cannot be changed. You may only use dredge options of the chosen kind. A ‘harmful’ dredge option is something that applies a penalty or similar impediment (such as difficult terrain or a pit), whereas a ‘beneficial’ dredge option is one that applies a benefit or removes impediments (such as the Cleared Terrain option).

If you chose harmful dredge, you gain the Meticulous Hunter talent as a bonus talent, whereas if you chose beneficial dredge, you gain the Complex Preparation talent as a bonus talent.
