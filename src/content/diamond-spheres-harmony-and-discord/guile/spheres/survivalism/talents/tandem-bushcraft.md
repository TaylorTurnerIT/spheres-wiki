If you possess the (dredge) package, your allies gain a +5 bonus to saving throws against your dredged areas–if the chosen option does not grant a save, they avoid any other penalties or detriments associated with the area (such as ignoring difficult terrain, but not simply ignoring a pit created through the Pit talent).

If the ally has unlocked skill leverage with Survival, or they share a rapport with you, they instead automatically succeed saving throws made against your dredged areas.

If you possess the (harvest) package, when you attempt to grant first aid to a helpless or willing creature, instead of stabilizing or stopping bleed, you can instead attempt to remove a poison from the creature with a successful Heal or associated skill check; the DC of which is equal to the poison's save DC. You can only attempt to draw out poison with this talent within the first 2 rounds of when the target was poisoned, plus an additional round for every 5 ranks you possess in the associated skill. If the check is successful, the creature no longer suffers any additional effects from the poison. You do not risk poisoning yourself when using this talent.
