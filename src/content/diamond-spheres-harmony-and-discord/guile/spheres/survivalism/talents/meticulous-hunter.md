If you possess the (dredge) package, you can spend an additional 5 minutes when dredging to also camouflage the dredged area, changing the DC to perceive the dredged effect to 10 + your modifier in the associated skill. A creature that fails this check suffers a -2 penalty to any saving throws associated with the dredged area.

If a (ground) option already allows you to camouflage the dredged area (such as the Pit talent), the penalty to saving throws associated with the area increases by 2.

If you possess the (harvest) package, you can harvest all components you have the appropriate (harvest) talent for in 1 minute, instead of just one component. Additionally, you can spend 1 use of skill leverage when you gain the results of a refine attempt, allowing you to add the listed potency effect of the component regardless of the skill check.
