Whenever a creature attempts a Knowledge or Lore check, you can use the aid another action to improve the check as a free action usable outside of your turn–you must be aware of the ally’s check to attempt to aid it, and doing so requires them to be able to perceive you.

If a creature aided this way shares a *rapport* with you and your aid another check is successful, you also learn any information the aided creature learns from their successful check (as if you had also succeeded on that check); if this check would give additional pieces of useful information when exceeding the check’s DC, both you and the aided creature receive 1 additional piece of useful information. Regardless of the results of the check, you cannot attempt to recall information on the same topic.

**Associated Feat:** Collective Recollection (*Pathfinder Society Primer*).
