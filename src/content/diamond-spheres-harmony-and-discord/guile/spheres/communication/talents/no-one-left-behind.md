Creatures that share your *rapport* that would be encumbered due to carrying another creature within the *rapport* ignore the speed penalty for doing so (as long as the carried creature is no more than one size category larger than the creature carrying them).

Additionally, creatures within the *rapport* gain a +10-foot morale bonus to any movement made as part of the withdraw or run actions, and can sheathe a weapon or stow an item as part of taking the action.

Whenever such a creature takes the withdraw action and they have at least one free hand, they can pick up one unattended object or an unconscious ally of their size or smaller at any point during their movement without provoking attacks of opportunity (although any additional movement performed on their turn still provokes attacks of opportunities as normal).

**Associated Feat:** Cut Your Losses (*Pathfinder Society Primer*)
