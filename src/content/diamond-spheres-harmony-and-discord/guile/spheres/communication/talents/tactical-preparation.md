When you move during your turn, you may use the aid another action at any point during your movement, spending actions to do so as normal.

Whenever you use aid another to improve a creature’s attack roll, the aided creature also gains a circumstance bonus to the damage roll equal to half the granted aid another bonus (minimum 1).

Whenever you use aid another to improve a creature’s AC but the applicable attack still deals damage to the creature, they reduce the damage dealt by an amount equal to half the aid another bonus–this reduction can only apply once per round.

If the aided creature shares your *rapport*, this damage bonus/reduction increases by 2.
