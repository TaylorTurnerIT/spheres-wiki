Whenever you improve an ally’s AC using aid another, the bonus granted increases to +3. This increases by 1 per 10 ranks in the associated skill you possess.

If you improve an ally’s AC using aid another, the aided ally also gains one of the following benefits against that opponent for the aid another effect’s duration:

- The ally does not provoke attacks of opportunity from the opponent for standing up from prone, moving out of their threatened area, picking up an item, or drinking a potion.
- The ally is not considered flanked or flat-footed against the opponent.

**Associated Feat:** Got Your Back (*Melee Tactics Toolbox*)
