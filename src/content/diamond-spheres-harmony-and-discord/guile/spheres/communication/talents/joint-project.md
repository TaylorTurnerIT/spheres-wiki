**Prerequisites:** Communication sphere.

Creatures that share your *rapport* can assist one another in crafting mundane and magical items, and such creatures that use the aid another action to improve each other’s Craft check increase the bonus to +3, +1 per 10 ranks in the associated skill you possess. Such creature’s can fulfill the prerequisites for crafting the item on behalf of one another, and the assistance doubles the gp value of items that can be crafted each day.

At 5 ranks in the associated skill, creatures that share your *rapport* share one another’s item creation feats.

**Associated Feat:** Cooperative Crafting (*Advanced Player’s Guide*).
