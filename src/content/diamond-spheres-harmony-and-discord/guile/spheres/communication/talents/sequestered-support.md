**Prerequisites:** Associated 5 ranks, Communication sphere (Recall Support).

Whenever you use the Recall Support talent, it no longer has a limit on the types of rolls it can benefit (as long as it could normally benefit from aid another). If the aid attempt requires an opponent to be relevant to, the aided creature chooses the opponent when they ponder your words.

You can spend 1 use of skill leverage when using the Recall Support talent to have part of your cognitive ability ‘stored’ within the creature for the next day, allowing them to ponder your words a number of times per day equal to 1/2 your ranks in the sphere’s associated skill.
