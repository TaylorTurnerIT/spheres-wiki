name: "Briber"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "utility"
---

Whenever you bribe a creature, you treat their level as being 2 lower for determining how many gold pieces they expect (see bribes). You can spend 1 use of skill leverage when attempting to bribe a creature to treat their level as being 1 additional lower, plus 1 per 5 ranks in the sphere’s associated skill you possess.

You gain a +2 bonus to Diplomacy checks made to bribe someone and to Diplomacy and Charisma checks made to bargain with a conjured creature (such as with planar ally or planar binding), increasing by 1 per 5 ranks in the sphere’s associated skill you possess.

Additionally, the first time a specific creature refuses a bribe that you offer, their attitude towards you does not worsen (even if the attempt would normally offend them). Plus, a creature that you fail to bribe only reports you to the authorities (or their equivalent) if you fail the attempt by 5 or more.

**Associated Feat:** Persuasive Bribery (*Ultimate Intrigue*).
