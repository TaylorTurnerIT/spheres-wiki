**Prerequisites:** Knowledge (arcana) 6 ranks, Perception 6 ranks, Scout sphere (Heightened Awareness, Magehunter’s Vision).

After spending three rounds using Heightened Awareness to examine a magic aura, you may track the creature or object that created the magic aura. You gain a circumstance bonus to follow tracks based upon the strength of the examined aura (dim +1, faint +2, moderate +4, strong +8, overwhelming +16). The bonus lasts until you have traveled a number of miles or made a number of Survival checks equal to half your ranks in Perception, or until you use Heightened Awareness to examine another magic aura.
