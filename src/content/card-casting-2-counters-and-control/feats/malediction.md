**Prerequisites:** Fate sphere, Protection sphere (Punishment)

**Benefit:** When you use the Punishment succor, you may target the attacker with a word in place of dealing the damage from Punishment. The spell point cost of such a word is reduced by 1, to a minimum of 0. In addition, when using the Punishment succor, you may end a word or motif effect which you have spent at least one spell point on in place of ending an aegis.
