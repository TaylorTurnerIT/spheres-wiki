**Prerequisites:** Cascade Spell, Pawstpone

**Benefit:** When you use Cascade Spell, you may delay the effects of specific magic sphere effects as time motes, each set to activate automatically at the start of one of your turns, the order determined when you initially create the time motes (but no two effects on the same turn). If you do, you may ignore the targeting restrictions of Cascade Spell.
