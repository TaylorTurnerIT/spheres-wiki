summary: "Double an area dispel's radius by limiting it to one magic sphere or school."
---
**Prerequisites:** Improved Counterspell

**Benefit:** Whenever you use an area dispel with Improved Counterspell or Greater Counterspell, you may choose to only dispel effects from a single magic sphere or school of magic. If you do, the radius of the area dispel is doubled.
