**Prerequisites:** Improved Counterspell

**Benefit:** Whenever you successfully counterspell 2 or more effects created by a single creature as part of a single counterspell attempt, you may divide the dispelled effects into two groups of any size. The caster selects which of the two groups are successfully dispelled and which remain active. If you do this, you are refunded one spell point used in the counterspell (to a minimum of 1 point).

**Special:** If you have the Improved Negation talent you may use that talent to meet the prerequisites of this feat in place of Improved Counterspell, and you may use the effects of this feat with effects counterspelled using Improved Negation.
