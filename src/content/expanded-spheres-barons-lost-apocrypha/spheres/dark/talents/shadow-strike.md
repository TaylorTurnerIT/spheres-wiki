As a standard action, you may make a single weapon attack in conjunction with using a (shadow) effect. This casting cannot be reduced below a standard action, and does not provoke attacks of opportunity, unless as usual if making a ranged attack. If the target is struck by the attack, it is also affected by the (shadow). If using a scatter weapon, the (shadow) only applies to a single target, chosen at the time of attack. If you possess the Clinging Darkness talent, you may affect the target with a (darkness) or (blot) effect in place of a (shadow).
