---
id: environmental-presence
name: "Environmental Presence"
system: power
type: talent
sphere: nature
tier: basic
tags: ["mass"]
---

When using a geomancing or spirit effect which targets a creature or object, you may spend an additional spell point to affect an additional 1 target per 2 caster levels (minimum 1) at the same time. Each target must be within range and must be affected by the same geomancing or spirit effect.

If you would gain an ability which would grant you the mass talent associated with the nature sphere, you must choose between gaining this talent or the second instance of the Grant Spirit talent (provided you already have the first instance of Grant Spirit and thus qualify for the second).
