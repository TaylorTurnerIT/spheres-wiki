**Prerequisites:** Warp Sphere (Teleport Object, Unwilling Teleport)

You may use Teleport Object to teleport objects into the free hands of or onto the persons of unwilling creatures. Creatures having objects teleported onto themselves are allowed a Reflex save to avoid donning or grabbing the item, in which case it appears in a square adjacent to them. As part of planting an object on a creature, you may make a Sleight of Hand check opposed by the creature's Perception check. You may use your caster level + your casting ability modifier in place of your Sleight of Hand modifier for this action. On a success, the creature is not aware of the object planted on them.
