**Prerequisites:** Time sphere (shuffle time)

You create a sphere with a radius up to 10 feet + 5 feet per 2 caster levels. This effect remains centered around you as you move. You may spend 1 spell point to allow this effect to continue for 1 round per caster level without concentration.

If you possess the Ranged Time talent, you may choose to center this effect on a square within your Time sphere range, and if you do, can include yourself.

At the start of each combat round, each creature in the area rerolls their initiative, acting on this new initiative counts on any future combat rounds (including the round that these dice were rolled).

If you possess the Fate sphere and the Declare Fate talent, you may spend 2 additional spell points to cause all creatures in the area to be affected by that talent for any turn they begin in the area. When the creature declares its actions for the next turn, it rolls the following dice for each action that it declared, subtracting the total amount from its next initiative roll. If an action would fall under multiple categories, treat it as the action that uses the largest die.

| Action Declared | Die |
|---|---|
| Swift Action | d4 |
| Move Action | d6 |
| Standard Action | d8 |
| Full-Round Action | d10 |
