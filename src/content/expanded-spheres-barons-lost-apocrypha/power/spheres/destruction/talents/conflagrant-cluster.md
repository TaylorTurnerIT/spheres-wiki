**Prerequisites:** Destruction sphere, caster level 5th

Whenever you would roll the listed amount and size of dice using a destructive blast, you may roll the corresponding dice on Table: Conflagrant Cluster Variant Dice. You do not need to replace all of the dice used in the destructive blast with their alternatives and may make multiple substitutions as part of a single destructive blast (for example, when making a destructive blast that deals 16d6 damage, you may have it deal 4d24+1d14+2d6 damage). Effects that modify a destructive blast based on the number of dice rolled use the new number of dice rolled (so a focused blast using the above layout of dice would only deal +7 damage).
You may take this talent a second time, in which case you may select from an additional variety of Alternative Dice
