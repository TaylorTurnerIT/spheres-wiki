**Prerequisite:** Spellhacking sphere.

**Benefit:** You can take a special move action to prepare yourself to hack magic using your spellhacking instrument to alter a magical effect as it activates. Unlike using the ready action, this feat allows you to react to your own action and doesn’t alter your initiative count. This can also be prepared to hack a magic item or trap as it activates, as long as you are aware of it and the item's caster level or trap's challenge rating is less than the number of ranks in the Spellhacking sphere’s associated skill you possess.

You can hack the effect as part of its casting or activation if you cast the effect or produce the effect from an item you wear or wield. If you target another creature's effect or an item or trap, you may hack the effect as an immediate action (after spending the move action to ready yourself).
