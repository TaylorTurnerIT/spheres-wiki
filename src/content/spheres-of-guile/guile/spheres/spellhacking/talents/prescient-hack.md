---
name: "Prescient Hack"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "plan"
---

You can delay the application of your hack magic ability until you are ready to exploit it. You can reveal a plan to be a use of hack magic to alter a magic item or effect you could access when you last prepared plans. Revealing this plan is a full-round action, which can include drawing and activating the item you reveal to be hacked if the activation is normally a standard action or less, or else concentrating on or otherwise directing a magic effect you reveal to be hacked.
