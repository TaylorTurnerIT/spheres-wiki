Operatives of the Faction sphere cultivate networks of allies, resources, and influence to support their operations from the shadows.

**Associated Skill:** Diplomacy or Knowledge (local).

You gain the following benefits when you gain the Faction sphere:

You gain 5 ranks in the chosen associated skill, plus 5 ranks per additional talent spent in the Faction sphere (maximum ranks equal to your Hit Dice). If you already have ranks in the associated skill you may immediately retrain them.

**Skill Leverage:** You unlock skill leverage with Diplomacy and Knowledge (local).

**Package:** When you gain the Faction sphere, you gain one of the following packages with its included abilities.

You also acquire the Requisition ability.
