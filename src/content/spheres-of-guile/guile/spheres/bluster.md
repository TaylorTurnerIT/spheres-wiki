Operatives of the Bluster sphere use their words as weapons, wielding threats, intimidation, and psychological warfare to control others without lifting a finger.

**Associated Skill:** Intimidate.

You gain the following benefits when you gain the Bluster sphere:

You gain 5 ranks in Intimidate, plus 5 ranks per additional talent spent in the Bluster sphere (maximum ranks equal to your Hit Dice). If you already have ranks in Intimidate you may immediately retrain them.

**Skill Leverage:** You unlock skill leverage with the Intimidate skill.

You also acquire the Flaunt Secrets and Quip abilities.
