Operatives of the Herbalism sphere harness the power of plants, fungi, and natural remedies to heal allies and hinder foes.

**Associated Skill:** Craft (alchemy) or Profession (herbalist).

You gain the following benefits when you gain the Herbalism sphere:

You gain 5 ranks in the chosen associated skill, plus 5 ranks per additional talent spent in the Herbalism sphere (maximum ranks equal to your Hit Dice). If you already have ranks in the associated skill you may immediately retrain them.

**Skill Leverage:** You unlock skill leverage with Craft (alchemy) and Heal.
