Operatives of the Survivalism sphere are masters of living off the land, finding resources in any environment and turning nature's bounty to their advantage.

**Associated Skill:** Survival, plus one depending on your chosen package.

You gain the following benefits when you gain the Survivalism sphere:

You gain 5 ranks in the chosen associated skill, plus 5 ranks per additional talent spent in the Survivalism sphere (maximum ranks equal to your Hit Dice). If you already have ranks in the associated skill you may immediately retrain them.

**Skill Leverage:** You unlock skill leverage with Survival and one additional skill depending on your package.

**Package:** When you gain the Survivalism sphere, you gain one of the following packages with its included abilities.

You also acquire the Canny Survivor ability.
