Operatives of the Body Control sphere master their physical form, learning to push past normal limits of endurance, flexibility, and control.

**Associated Skill:** Acrobatics or Escape Artist.

You gain the following benefits when you gain the Body Control sphere:

You gain 5 ranks in the chosen associated skill, plus 5 ranks per additional talent spent in the Body Control sphere (maximum ranks equal to your Hit Dice). If you already have ranks in the associated skill you may immediately retrain them.

**Skill Leverage:** You unlock skill leverage with the Acrobatics and Escape Artist skills.

You also acquire the Meditate and Slip Past abilities.
