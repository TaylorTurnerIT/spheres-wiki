id: "conversational-decorum"
name: "Conversational Decorum"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "approach"
  - "utility"
---

You may adopt this approach as a standard action. While in this approach, you are aware of any action that could cause a creature’s attitude towards you to shift negatively. You acquire this information prior to performing such an action. In addition, you become aware of any changes in a creature’s attitude towards you or another creature you can perceive due to your own or another creature’s actions, gaining this insight after such an action is made.

You can spend 1 use of skill leverage when you or any ally within your rapport take such an action to instead have the action be something you or they simply thought of, but did not actually do.
