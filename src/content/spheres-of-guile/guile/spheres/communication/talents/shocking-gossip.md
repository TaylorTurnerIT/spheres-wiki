---
id: "shocking-gossip"
name: "Shocking Gossip"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "utility"
---

If you successfully alter a target creature’s attitude toward a third party using the Diplomacy skill and either spend 1 use of skill leverage or invoke the target’s motivation, the target must succeed at a Will saving throw or gain one of the following conditions: shocked (if you worsened their attitude) or impressed (if you improved their attitude, in which case the target is impressed with the subject of your gossip). Either condition lasts for 10 minutes. If the target's Hit Dice are lower than your ranks in the associated skill, the effect lasts for 1d4 rounds even on a successful save.

In addition, you can spend 1 use of skill leverage to use the Diplomacy skill to alter a creature’s attitude toward a third creature as a standard action, even in combat.
