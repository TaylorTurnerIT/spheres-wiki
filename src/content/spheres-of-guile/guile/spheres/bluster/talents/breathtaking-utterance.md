**Prerequisites:** Associated skill 10 ranks, Bluster sphere.

Your statements can leave those who hear it so bewildered that they struggle to breathe. Whenever you give a creature the dumbfounded or shocked condition, you may outwit the target as a cost to also force them to succeed at a Will saving throw or be unable to breathe (and forced to hold their breath) until they no longer have a disorientation condition, and each attempt to reduce or end the condition with a Will save reduces the remaining amount of time they can hold their breath by half. A creature holding their breath in this way cannot speak or use verbal components for spells. If this causes the creature to fall unconscious due to suffocation, the creature stabilizes and loses the disorientation condition unless you also spend 2 uses of skill leverage as part of the action to quip.
