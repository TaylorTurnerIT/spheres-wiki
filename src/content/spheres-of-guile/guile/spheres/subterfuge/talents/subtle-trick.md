name: "Subtle Trick"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
---

You do not automatically break Stealth when you attempt a dirty trick combat maneuver (Pathfinder Roleplaying Game: Advanced Player’s Guide). Instead, you attempt a Sleight of Hand check and observers notice you if their opposed Perception check exceeds your skill check result. If the effect doesn’t apply to the target’s current activity (such as if it is sitting still watching a performance when you entangle it), the duration of the trick doesn’t begin to elapse until it becomes relevant (such as when an entangled creature moves or attempts a penalized skill check).

Creatures who were already in combat realize you are the source of the trick as soon as you complete your action. In addition, a successful check denies the target their Dexterity bonus to CMD against the maneuver.
