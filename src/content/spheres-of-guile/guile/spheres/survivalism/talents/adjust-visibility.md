When you use dredge, you can choose one of the following options to apply to the area:

**Clangorous Terrain:** Creatures within the dredged area suffer a penalty to Stealth checks equal to your ranks in the associated skill and have their square revealed as if they had been successfully pinpointed—if such a creature is invisible, attacks made against them only suffer from concealment instead of total concealment. These effects persist 1 round after leaving the dredged area. The noise caused by being within the dredged area also causes any sleeping creatures within 30 feet to become conscious.

**Softened Terrain:** Creatures within the dredged area do not apply their armor check penalty to Stealth checks, and any Perception checks made against creatures within the area suffer a -2 penalty, increasing by 1 per 4 ranks in the associated skill. This penalty is doubled against Perception checks that rely solely on sound. Additionally, creatures with tremorsense can only sense creatures within the area at half the usual distance; if the creature within the area moves at half speed or slower, creatures with tremorsense cannot detect them at all. These effects persist 1 round after leaving the dredged area.
