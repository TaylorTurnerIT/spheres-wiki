Operatives of the Spellhacking sphere specialize in interfering with and manipulating magical effects, turning enemy magic against them.

**Associated Skill:** Spellcraft or Use Magic Device.

You gain the following benefits when you gain the Spellhacking sphere:

You gain 5 ranks in the chosen associated skill, plus 5 ranks per additional talent spent in the Spellhacking sphere (maximum ranks equal to your Hit Dice). If you already have ranks in the associated skill you may immediately retrain them.

**Skill Leverage:** You unlock skill leverage with Spellcraft and Use Magic Device.

You also acquire the Control Mishap and Hack Magic abilities.
