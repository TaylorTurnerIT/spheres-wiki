**Prerequisites:** Disable Device 5 ranks, Infiltration sphere.

Your (sabotage) talent effects have no maximum duration and require two successful opposed Craft or Disable Device checks to remove rather than 1. A successful Perception check opposed by your Disable Device check is required after the first successful Craft or Disable Device check to discover that the first check did not remove the (sabotage).
