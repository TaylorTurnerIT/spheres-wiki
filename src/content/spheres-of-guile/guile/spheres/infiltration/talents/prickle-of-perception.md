**Prerequisites:** Perception 7 ranks or Stealth 7 ranks, Infiltration sphere.

As a swift action, you can adopt this approach to notice when creatures have spotted you. You get a prickling sensation when a creature has a higher state of awareness toward you than you have toward it (see states of awareness). This indeterminate sense lets you know approximately how many creatures within 120 feet have the edge in awareness over you (one or two, a few, several, many, a crowd, or a horde). This talent does not alert you to creatures you could not perceive even if you rolled a 20 on your Perception check.
