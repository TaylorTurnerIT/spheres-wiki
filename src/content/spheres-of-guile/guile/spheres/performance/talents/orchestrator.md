name: "Orchestrator"
tier: "basic"

  - "utility"
---

Whenever you teach a creature a Perform skill, you reduce the time to do so to 10 minutes, and may choose a single Performance sphere [utility] talent you possess. The creature uses their own ranks in the skill used to teach when determining the effects of the talent, or half your number of ranks in the skill, whichever is higher, but is still affected by any sphere-specific drawbacks you may possess. A specific creature can only remember a single taught talent at one time; teaching another requires them to forget the previous one.
