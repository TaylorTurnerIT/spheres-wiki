When using overland travel, you and allies traveling with you travel an additional distance in the same period up to 1/4 of your normal overland travel speed. For every 5 ranks in an associated skill you possess, this improves by an additional +1/4 of your normal overland travel speed. Additionally, for the purposes of determining overland speed, you and your allies treat trackless terrain as being a trail, and any terrain with a road or trail as being a highway.

Additionally, traveling does not increase the time it takes for you to use certain Survival skill uses (such as when getting along in the wild, fortifying against severe weather, obscuring tracks, etc.). If you have the (urban) package, you can use an (urban) package associated skill, Sleight of Hand, or Stealth instead of Survival to get along in the wild, forage, or obscure tracks in an urban setting.

**Associated Feats:** Live off the Land (*Ultimate Wilderness*), Urban Forager (*Pathfinder Companion: Taldor, Echoes of Glory*)
