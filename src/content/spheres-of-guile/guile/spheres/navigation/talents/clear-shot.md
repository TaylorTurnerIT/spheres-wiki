---
id: "clear-shot"
name: "Clear Shot"
tier: "base"
tags:
  - "pathing"
---

Allies ignore any soft cover, partial cover, or partial concealment provided by objects or effects that are inside the area of your *pathing* but do not target individual creatures. Allies treat standard cover from inside the area as partial cover. In addition, their total penalties on Perception checks for observing targets within the area of your *pathing* are reduced by 2, plus an additional 1 for every 5 ranks you have in one of your associated skills. If you have at least 10 ranks in an associated skill, allies also treat total concealment as partial concealment.
