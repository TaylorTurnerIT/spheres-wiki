*You are adept at preparing a large group for the environments ahead.*

You can grant the benefits of your acclimation to a number of creatures or vehicles equal to the ranks you possess in a package’s associated skill of your choice (chosen when using this ability) + your operative ability modifier. As a standard action, you may change an acclimation affecting a touched creature to a different acclimation you could apply.

Additionally, you can have up to two instances of the pathing ability active at one time. Alternatively, you can use pathing as a full-round action to apply two (pathing) talents to the area (each talent is treated as their own instance of the pathing ability).
