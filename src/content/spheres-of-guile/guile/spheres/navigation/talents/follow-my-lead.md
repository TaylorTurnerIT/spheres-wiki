You can help your allies in their movement, directing their actions with your expertise. While you maintain this approach, allies within 60 feet of you may use the higher value between your ranks in one of your associated skills and their normal modifier to that skill. For every 4 ranks in the corresponding skill you possess, these bonuses increase by 1. The area of this effect increases by 5 feet for every 2 ranks you possess in an associated skill, to a maximum of 110 feet. You do not gain any bonuses from this approach.
