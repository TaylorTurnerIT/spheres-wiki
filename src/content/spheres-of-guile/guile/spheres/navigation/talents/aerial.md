You gain the following acclimation:

- **Weatherproofing:** Acclimated targets may treat the severity of weather effects as being 1 step lower, +1 step per 7 ranks you possess in this package’s associated skill. Additionally, such targets improve the maneuverability on any fly speed they possess by one step (maximum good maneuverability), and are always considered to have moved at least half their speed for the purpose of determining if they need to make Fly checks to remain flying. Acclimated air-current vehicles reduce the DC of any piloting checks attempted to perform actions on them by 1 + 1 per 4 ranks in this package’s associated skill.

**Associated Feat:** Careful Flyer (*Ultimate Intrigue*)

**Associated Skill:** Fly or Profession (pilot).
