You gain the following acclimation:

- **Conditioning:**  Acclimated targets gain a +2 competence bonus to Constitution checks, and to saving throws against environmental hazards and weather effects, as well as effects that cause fatigue or nonlethal damage. They also reduce all nonlethal damage as well as damage dealt due to environmental hazards (such as falling, pressure, extreme temperatures, etc.) by 3. These bonuses (and damage reduction) increase by +1 per 5 ranks you possess in this package’s associated skill.
Lastly, such creatures do not become fatigued due to sleeping in armor or from suffering nonlethal damage from extreme temperatures, and do not take a penalty to Constitution checks for wearing armor in hot climates.

**Associated Feat:** Endurance

**Associated Skill:** Climb, Profession (guide), Profession (driver), or Survival.
