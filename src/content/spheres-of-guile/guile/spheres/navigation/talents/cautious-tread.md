Allies within your *pathing* gain a +1 insight bonus to AC and Reflex saving throws against hazards and traps. This improves by 1 for every 4 ranks you possess in an associated skill. You and any allies moving through your *pathing* receive a check to notice traps whenever they pass within 10 feet of them, whether or not they are actively looking.
