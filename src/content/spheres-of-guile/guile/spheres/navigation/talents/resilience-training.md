You may select the following acclimation benefits:

- **Heavy Load:** Acclimated targets gain a +2 competence bonus on Strength checks and to their Strength score for the purpose of determining their carrying capacity. For every 5 ranks you possess in one of your associated skills, these bonuses increase by 1.
- **Tireless Strider:** Acclimated targets do not need to attempt Acrobatics checks to run or charge on slippery or unstable surfaces, can move at full speed when using Acrobatics to balance on narrow surfaces, and do not become flat-footed or lose their Dexterity bonus to AC when doing so. Lastly, an acclimated target only needs to spend 2 squares of movement when moving through a square that would usually cost more (such as deep bog or snow).
