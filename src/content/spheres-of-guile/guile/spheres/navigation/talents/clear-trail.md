---
name: "Clear Trail"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "pathing"
---

Allies within your *pathing* may take 5-foot steps in difficult terrain and reduce the penalty for moving through difficult terrain by 50%; for example, if moving through snow normally costs you 2 squares of movement (1 square plus a 1 square penalty) per square traveled, this effect would reduce this cost to 1.5 squares per square traveled. Additionally, allies within your *pathing* also gain the following benefits against the effects of hazardous terrain (such as entangle, a Living Crystal Blast, most effects from the *dredge* ability, etc.): DR 2/–, +2 bonus to ability checks and skill checks, and a +2 competence bonus to saving throws. These benefits increase by +1 per 4 associated ranks you possess.

If you possess at least 5 ranks in one of your associated skills, allies within your *pathing* ignore penalties for moving through nonmagical difficult terrain. If you possess at least 15 ranks in one of your associated skills, allies within your *pathing* ignore penalties for moving through all difficult terrain.
