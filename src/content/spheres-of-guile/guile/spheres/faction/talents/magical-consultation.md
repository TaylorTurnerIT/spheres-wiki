---
name: "Magical Consultation"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "supply"
---

Your faction briefs you on how to use any magic item you requisition. If you need the Use Magic Device skill to activate an item provided or identified by your faction, you can use your Appraise modifier in place of your Use Magic Device modifier for those checks. In addition, you gain the following requisition option.

- **Identification:** You learn the function of any magic item as if with a Spellcraft result of 20 + your ranks in the associated skill. If your game features advanced technology, the faction can identify its function using any appropriate skill check with the same result.
