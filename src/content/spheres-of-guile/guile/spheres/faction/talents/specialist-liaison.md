For every noncombatant retainer profession you have, choose a new set of skills and skill talents for one. When you requisition from this profession, you choose which of your defined versions you get. If you gain another retainer profession, you can define another combination of skills and skill talents for it. You can take this talent multiple times, defining a new combination for each profession you have each time.
