---
name: Harvest Meat
featureId: courser-ventures
tags: []
requires: "Survivalism sphere (harvest) package"
---

The courser gains the Meat Survivalism talent as a bonus talent. Any meat that the courser refines does not count towards their maximum number of components they can have refined at one time.

Additionally, if the courser adds refined meat to a meal created with their forage class feature, it only takes 30 minutes to prepare, as well as increasing the number of creatures they can feed with the meal by twice the number of refined meat spent.
