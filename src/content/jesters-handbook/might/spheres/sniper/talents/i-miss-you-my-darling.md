---
name: "I Miss You My Darling"
tier: "advanced"
tags: []
---

**Prerequisites:** Stealth ranks 10, Scout sphere (Track The Scene), Sniper sphere.

Whenever you miss your target when performing a deadly shot, you may expend martial focus as a free action. If you do, you designate that target as your prey. While following the tracks of your prey, you may take 10 on Survival skill checks while moving at normal speed, without penalty. In addition, you receive a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls made against your prey, and automatically confirm the first critical threat you make against your prey. This effect ends when you confirm a critical threat against your prey, or 24 hours pass, whichever comes first. A creature cannot be designated as your prey more than once every 24 hours.

You may not have more than one creature designated as your prey at any time, and may dismiss this effect at any time as a free action.
