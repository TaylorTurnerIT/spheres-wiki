**Craft DC:** 10

You create a flask of pleasant thought distillate that functions as a splash weapon you can use as a ranged touch attack with a range increment of 10 feet. Anyone standing in the square of impact or adjacent squares must attempt Will saves. Creatures who fail their saving throw have their disposition toward you increased by 1 step to a maximum of friendly for 1 minute per rank in Craft (alchemy). This is a mind-affecting compulsion effect.

You can increase the Craft DC for this weapon in increments of 10; each time you do so, the radius increases by 5 feet. You can separately increase the Craft DC for this weapon by an increment of 20 to increase its potency; when you do so, any creature who fails its Will save against this effect instead have their disposition towards you increase to friendly for the duration.
