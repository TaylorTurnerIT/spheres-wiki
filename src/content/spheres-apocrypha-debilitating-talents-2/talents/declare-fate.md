**Prerequisites:** Fate sphere, caster level 15th.

You may spend two spell points to place a word on a creature to peer into its near future. Each round, at the end of the creature's turn, the creature must succeed at a Will saving throw or be forced to declare all actions that it will take on its next turn and be unable to change them. If a declared action would become impossible to perform (such as casting a spell on a creature that is no longer within range), that action is lost. You are aware of what actions an affected creature intends to take.

This effect lasts as long as you concentrate, but you may always spend a spell point to allow the effect to endure for 1 minute per caster level without concentration.

**Author's Note:** This does not affect attacks of opportunity, which are normally free actions.
