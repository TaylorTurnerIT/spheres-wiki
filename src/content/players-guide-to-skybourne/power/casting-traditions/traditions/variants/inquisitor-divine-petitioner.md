There are those who are directly empowered by their deity but exist outside the general priesthood; inquisitors, who are trusted by their deity to act in ways a usual divine petitioner cannot. These casters are trusted with direct divine power and do not need to pray for their magic nor hold their liege's holy symbol, but as a countermeasure, often possess a more limited measure of their deity's power.
