**Prerequisites:** Warp sphere (Pouncing Teleport, Segmented Warp), caster level 5th.

When you use Segmented Warp in combination with Pouncing Teleport to make a full attack or attack action after a teleport, you may spend an additional spell point to teleport again after any attack you make this turn, dividing your remaining teleport range into increments between each attack and after your last attack. You must teleport at least 5 feet each time you teleport. The total distance covered by all teleports cannot be longer than the maximum range of your teleport ability.
