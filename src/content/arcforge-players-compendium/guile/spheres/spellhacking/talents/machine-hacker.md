id: "machine-hacker"
name: "Machine Hacker"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
summary: "Use control mishap and hack magic on technological items with Craft (mechanical)."
---

You may use control mishap on a technological item whenever it would trigger a glitch and may use hack magic on technological items as if they were magic items. You may also coax a mishap out of a technological item in the same manner as you could a magical item. When attempting checks as part of such actions, you use Craft (mechanical) in place of your normal associated skill.
