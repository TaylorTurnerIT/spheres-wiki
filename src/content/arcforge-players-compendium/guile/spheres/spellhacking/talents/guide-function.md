---
name: "Guide Function"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "hack"
---

**Target:** one magic item

You configure the item to be more likely to succeed or fail on the next action taken using it. When the item would be used to make an attack roll or magic skill check, the user rolls their d20 twice and you choose the result. This hack then ends. For every 5 ranks you possess in your associated skill, this hack affects one additional roll.
