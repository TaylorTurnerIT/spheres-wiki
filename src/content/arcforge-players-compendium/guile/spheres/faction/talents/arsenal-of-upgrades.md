**Prerequisite:** Faction sphere.

Your item requisition can be for any cybertech or graft available in your faction’s area. You must still take time to install any cybertech or grafts on your body, though as part of the requisition the faction can supply a doctor with a Heal modifier equal to the primary skill modifier of a retainer corresponding to your associated skill ranks. This doctor will install any cybernetics or grafts that you requisition with this talent but will not remain after they are installed.
