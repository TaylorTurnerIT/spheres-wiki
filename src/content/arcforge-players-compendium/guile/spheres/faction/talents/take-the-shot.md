**Prerequisites:** Faction sphere (Armed Retainer (retainer), Logistical Genius  (plan)).

By spending 3 authorizations as a plan, you may reveal the existence of a single warden retainer within long range who has a weapon drawn and ready to attack. You may allow the retainer to make a single standard action as part of revealing this plan. If the location of the warden is exceptionally difficult to reach, you must attempt a check with your associated skill to determine if your warden was able to be placed properly. The DC for this check is set by the GM, generally either DC 10 + the highest Perception modifier among guardians in the area or DC 10 + twice the highest challenge rating among all guardians of the area.
