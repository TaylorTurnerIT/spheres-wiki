---
id: "harrying-fire"
name: "Harrying Fire"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
summary: "With martial focus, missing a deadly shot by 10 or less inflicts a -2 penalty to the target's AC or attack rolls for 1 round."
---

As long as you have martial focus, whenever you miss a target with your deadly shot, if you miss by 10 or less, the target takes a -2 penalty to AC or attack rolls for 1 round, chosen when you make the attack.
