---
id: "make-it-hurt"
name: "Make It Hurt"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "shout"
summary: "Expend martial focus to grant allies in an area a 19-20 critical threat range and a bonus on critical confirmation rolls."
---

You may expend your martial focus to use this shout, causing all allies in the area to threaten a critical hit on a 19 or 20 on their attack rolls (or using the normal critical threat range of their weapon, whichever is better). Allies affected by this shout gain a morale bonus on critical hit confirmation rolls equal to half your ranks in Diplomacy.
