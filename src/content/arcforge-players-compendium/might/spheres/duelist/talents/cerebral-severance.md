---
id: "cerebral-severance"
name: "Cerebral Severance"
tier: "advanced"
tags:
  - "bleed"

---

**Prerequisite:** Duelist sphere.

Whenever you deal bleed damage to a creature, you may leave fluids leaking in their skull. The target cannot use telepathy to send or receive messages so long as it is bleeding. Abilities based on telepathic connection or other linking of minds (such as a tactician’s collective or a symbiat’s mind link) do not function while the creature is bleeding.
