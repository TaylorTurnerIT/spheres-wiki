---
id: "cruise-control"
name: "Cruise Control"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
summary: "Reduce the action cost of driving or crew actions by one step while maintaining martial focus."
---

As long as you maintain martial focus, you reduce the action cost required to perform a driving action (if driving a vehicle) or crew action (if aboard an airship or similar vehicle) by one step (full-round action > standard > move > swift > free), though you can never reduce the action cost required to utilize an immediate action.
