name: "Companion Aethership"
tier: "advanced"

**Prerequisites:** Athletics sphere ((fly) package or Ace Pilot).

You gain a companion aethership as the aethertech pilot cavalier class feature. Your effective cavalier level for this companion is the highest between your base attack bonus, Fly ranks, or Profession (pilot) ranks - 3 (minimum 1).

Should you abandon your aethership or your aethership is destroyed, you may gain a new aethership after 1 week. If you are in the environment where you could easily acquire an aethership, you may instead gain that new companion in 24 hours.

Abilities that grant talents temporarily can not be used to gain this talent, such as taking the Extra Combat Talent feat with martial flexibility or gaining it from an armiger’sSoM customized weapon.

You may take this talent a second time. If you do, your effective aethertech pilot level when determining the effects of this talent becomes equal to the highest between your base attack bonus, ranks in Fly, or ranks in Profession (pilot).

**Special:** If you already possess a companion aethership, or gain one at a later time from another source, you gain the benefits of this talent as if taken twice. Your effective aethertech pilot level from this talent stacks with those from other sources, to a maximum of your character level.

If you select a gunship as your companion aethership, you may cause your gunship to only possess a space speed and no atmosphere speed. If you do, you may select this talent as a utility talent.
