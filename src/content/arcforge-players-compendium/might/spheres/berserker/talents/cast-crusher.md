name: "Cast-Crusher"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "exertion"
summary: "Inflict a scaling caster level penalty on creatures damaged by your brutal strike for as long as they are battered."
---

Creatures damaged by your brutal strike take a -1 penalty on caster level for as long as the creature is battered. This penalty increases by -1 at base attack bonus +4 and every 4 points of base attack bonus thereafter. This penalty does not stack with other uses of the same talent.
