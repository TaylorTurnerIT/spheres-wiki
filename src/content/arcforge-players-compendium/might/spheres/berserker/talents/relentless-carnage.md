**Prerequisites:** Berserker sphere (Advancing Carnage x2), base attack bonus +10.

When you use Advancing Carnage, additional targets do not need to be adjacent to each other and the maximum number of additional attacks made with this ability increases to your base attack bonus (minimum 1).
