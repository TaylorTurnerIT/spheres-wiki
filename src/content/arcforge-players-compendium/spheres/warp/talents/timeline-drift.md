**Prerequisites:** Warp sphere (Distant Teleport, Unseeing Teleport), caster level 10th.

You may spend 2 additional spell points to teleport to your present time in an alternate timeline. You must be aware of the specific alternate timeline you are trying to reach, as well as the location (within your normal teleport range) you would like to appear (if you do not know a specific location to appear on that timeline you may still use this ability, but you run the risk of appearing anywhere within your teleport range in the new timeline). Even if you do have a specific location in mind, you always appear 5 to 500 miles (5 × d100) from your intended destination.
