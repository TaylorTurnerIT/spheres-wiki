---
id: pullback
name: "Pullback"
system: power
type: talent
sphere: warp
tier: basic
tags: ["space"]
---

You may cause a creature or object's location in space and time to become sticky. At the end of each turn, you may have the target be teleported to the location it began the turn at (even if the target is teleported or sent to another plane). The affected creature or a creature in possession of the affected object is allowed a Will saving throw to avoid the target being teleported back to its original location. This effect lasts as long as you concentrate, but you may spend a spell point to allow it to remain for 1 round per caster level without concentration.

If you have the Dimensional Aegis feat, you may apply Pullback to a creature as a (succor) effect. If you do, it has a duration of 1 round.
