name: "Technological Suppression"
system: power
type: talent
sphere: protection
tier: basic
tags: ["aegis", "ward"]
---

You may spend a spell point to create a ward that represses technological functions within its area of effect. Attempt one magic skill check against the gizmo penetration DC against each technological item, robot, creature with cybertech components, and other effect created through technological means within the area. Technological items have a gizmo penetration DC of their craft DC -4, and creatures have a gizmo penetration DC of 10 + their CR. If your check exceeds the gizmo penetration DC of an item, any of the item's ongoing effects that use charges immediately end. If your check exceeds the gizmo penetration DC of a creature, that creature must succeed at a Fortitude save each round they remain in the ward. Failure indicates that robots and other technological constructs are helpless for 1 round, while partially technological creatures like androids or cyborgs are staggered for 1 round.

Any time a creature in the area would spend charges from a technological item, they must succeed at a gizmo penetration check against your MSD (using the item's craft DC -15 or the source's CR in place of a gizmo penetration check if the item or creature is not a gizmo). On a failure, no charges are spent and the action has no effect. On a success, the item functions normally.

You may also spend 2 spell points instead of 1 to create an aegis that grants the target protection from technological effects. Whenever the target would be affected by an effect from a technological item or technological construct which imposes a condition or damage type other than bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, the source of the effect must attempt a gizmo penetration check against your MSD (using the item's craft DC -15 or the source's CR in place of a gizmo penetration check if the item or creature is not a gizmo). On a failure, any conditions or damage besides bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage from the effect do not take effect.
