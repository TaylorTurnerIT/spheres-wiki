**Prerequisites:** Time sphere (Witness Across Time* (advanced)), caster level 10th.

Any of your talents and feats which allow you to send and receive information or use non-teleportation abilities across planes now also function across alternate timelines and separations in time. For example, a character with the Greater Communication Mind sphere talent can send messages to an ally they are aware of who is currently in an alternate timeline or even in an alternate time period in that timeline (such as 100 years in the past). You must be aware of a specific alternate timeline's existence (such as having traveled from it, knowing that somebody else has traveled to it, or knowing that a portal exists to it) in order to target that timeline or anything in it. An alternate "stripe" of time resulting from an inconsequential form of time travel (such as using a Shadowbore or teleportation effect for superluminal travel) does not qualify as an alternate timeline.
