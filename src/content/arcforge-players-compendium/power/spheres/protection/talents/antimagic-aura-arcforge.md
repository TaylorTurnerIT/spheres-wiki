**Prerequisites:** Protection sphere (Spell Ward (aegis, ward) or Technological Suppression (aegis, ward)), caster level 10th.

When creating a Spell Ward, you may spend an additional spell point to improve the ward’s abilities: The ward stops all magic and summoned creatures without the need for a magic skill check. If this ward is pitted against a summoned creature that has spell resistance, you must still attempt a magic skill check against the creature’s spell resistance to banish it.

When creating a Technological Suppression ward, you may spend an additional spell point to improve the ward’s abilities: The ward stops all technological abilities and uses of charges without the need for a magic skill check or allowance for a gizmo penetration check. If this ward is pitted against a construct or cyborg that has spell resistance, you must still attempt a magic skill check against the creature’s spell resistance to affect it.
