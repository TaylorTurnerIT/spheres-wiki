**Prerequisites:** Protection sphere (Spell Selectivity (advanced); Spell Ward (aegis, ward) or Technological Suppression (aegis, ward)), caster level 10th.

When you use Spell Selectivity to affect one or more specific spheres or schools of magic (rather than nullify all except a chosen number of spheres), you may select individual talents or feats which require casting in place of spheres. If a specific talent is affected, all talents and feats which require that talent as a prerequisite are also affected

When you use Spell Selectivity to affect one or more specific categories of technological item or creature (rather than nullify all except a chosen number of technological sources), you may select individual gizmos, items, or creatures (such as arc rifles, plasma grenades, or gearsman robots) in place of item or creature types.
