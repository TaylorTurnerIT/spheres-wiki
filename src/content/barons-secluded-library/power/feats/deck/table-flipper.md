**Prerequisites:** casting class feature, does not possess the Card Casting drawback

**Benefits:** You gain several abilities related to interfering with the abilities of other characters with the Card Casting drawback, as per the Control Caster feat. The save DC of these abilities is equal to the sphere DC of one of the ability’s associated spheres.
