**Prerequisites:** Card Casting (Bleeding Hand)

**Benefits:** Whenever you would be targeted by an attack roll, you may discard a card from your hand to gain a +2 luck bonus to your AC against the attack roll. If you possess the Handblood’s Advantage feat, you may discard a card when you are forced to make a saving throw to gain a +2 bonus to the saving throw based on the type of card discarded.

*Physical:* Fortitude saving throw
*Mental:* Reflex saving throw
*Spiritual:* Will saving throw

**Special:** You may select this feat a second time, in which case you may discard a second card to increase the AC or saving throw bonus from +2 to +4.
