**Prerequisites:** Card Casting (Bleeding Hand)

**Benefit:** Each card in your deck gains a category from among Physical, Mental, or Spiritual, chosen when you create the card. Whenever you discard a card as part of performing an action, you gain a benefit based on the category of the card discarded.

*Physical:* You gain a +2 luck bonus a single die roll as part of the action that adds your Strength or Constitution modifier
*Mental:* You gain a +2 luck bonus a single die roll as part of the action that adds your Dexterity or Intelligence modifier
*Spiritual:* You gain a +2 luck bonus a single die roll as part of the action that adds your Wisdom or Charisma modifier
