If this is this character's first level in any class, the masterwork chronicler may select a trade tradition of their choice, and have an adroit trade rank. The masterwork chronicler gains a number of skill ranks at each level equal to 6 + their Intelligence modifier.

This replaces the skald's normal class skills.
