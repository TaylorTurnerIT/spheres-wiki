**Prerequisites:** Dream Travel grand secret.

If you have the dream travel grand secret, whenever you merge your dreamspace with the dream of another, you may transfer any items in your possession to a willing creature who you contacted in the dream, causing those items to appear around the dreamer when they wake up.
