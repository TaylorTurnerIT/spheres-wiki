name: "Appeal to Normalcy"
type: "talent"
sourceBook: "barons-secluded-library"
sphere: "bluster"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "utility"
---

Whenever you try to convince a non-spellcaster that a magical phenomenon or creature is not magical, you treat the lie as two steps more believable. In addition, non-spellcasters are always treated as wanting to believe you when you try to convince them that something is not magic.
