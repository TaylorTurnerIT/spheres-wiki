**Prerequisites:** Craft (any 3) 10 ranks, Artifice Sphere (Underwater Basketweaving)

Choose a Craft skill you possess ranks in. When attempting a skill check using any other Craft skill or as part of crafting an item (such as using Heal to create a construct or using another skill as part of a dynamic magic item creation challenge), you may choose to use your ranks in the chosen Craft skill rather than your ranks in the used skill.
