name: "Underhanded Blades"
tier: "basic"
---

**Prerequisites:** Dual Wielding sphere

When making a dual attack, you may use d16s in place of d20s for each attack roll. If you do, you reduce any attack roll penalties from dual attack by 2 and threaten a critical hit on a 16 before your critical threat range is increased or doubled (so for example, a weapon which normally has an 18-20 critical threat range threatens a critical on a 14-16).
