**Prerequisites:** Form sphere (Hidden Storage [auxiliary]), Warp sphere (Extradimensional Storage x2).

**Benefit:** Your hidden storage is connected to your extradimensional storage, allowing you to store or withdraw items from your extradimensional storage using the actions it would normally take to store or withdraw them using hidden storage. You may affect targets within your hidden storage with any class features, talents or feats which would affect targets within your extradimensional storage.
