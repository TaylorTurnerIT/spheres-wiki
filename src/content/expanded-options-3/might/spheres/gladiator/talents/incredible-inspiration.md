**Prerequisites:** Intimidate ranks 10, Gladiator sphere (Inspire (boast)).

When using the Inspire (boast) talent, if the chosen ally succeeds the next saving throw they attempt within the next minute, and the effect allows for a reduced effect on a successful save, the ally instead avoids the effect entirely.
