**Prerequisites:** Sniper sphere, base attack bonus +5.

Whenever you perform a deadly shot special attack action, you may choose to make a called shot. If you do, you reduce the penalty associated with the called shot by 2 (minimum 0). **Associated Feat:** Improved Called Shot.

**Note:** This talent requires the called shot optional rule from Ultimate Combat.
