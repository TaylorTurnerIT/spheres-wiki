**Prerequisites:** Barroom sphere and Performance sphere.

**Benefit:** You can retrieve and drink an alcoholic beverage (or grab one within your natural reach) as part of using or maintaining a Performance sphere ability (except when it is done as a free action).

Additionally, you gain an additional benefit depending on which Performance sphere packages you possess:

- **Act:** You can add the following scene to your acts:
 - **Bottoms Up! (scene, 1 standard action):** When this scene is completed, all allies within the act's area may drink an alcoholic beverage or other consumable as an immediate action—an ally can retrieve or grab such a beverage that is within their natural reach as part of this action.
- **Dance:** While you are dancing, drunk creatures within your dance area automatically join your dance without needing to spend an action to begin dancing. If you are drunk when you begin a dance, your dance area increases by 10 feet. Additionally, whenever a creature maintains or joins your dance, they can retrieve and imbibe an alcoholic beverage or potion as part of the action to do so (although can only do this once per round).
- **Instrumental:** Whenever you activate a (smash) talent, you can either maintain your instrumental as a free action this round, or increase the area by 5 feet and its save DC (if any) by 1 for 1 round—multiple applications only increase the duration of this effect. 
- **Lyric:** Whenever you activate a (drunk) talent, you can reduce the action cost of your next lyric made this turn by 1 step (full-round > standard > move > swift > free).
