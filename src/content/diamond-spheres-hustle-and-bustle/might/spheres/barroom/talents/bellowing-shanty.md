---
name: "Bellowing Shanty"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "drunk"
---

As long as you have the drunk status, you gain an alchemical bonus to Perform (act, comedy, oratory, and sing) checks equal to 1/2 your base attack bonus.

You can expend your drunk status as a free action to increase the range and radius of any extraordinary or supernatural ability that relies on auditory components you use by 10 feet + 5 feet for every 5 points of base attack bonus you possess, as well as the save DC of such effects, by 1 until the end of your next turn.
