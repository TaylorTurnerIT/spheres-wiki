**Prerequisites:** Barroom sphere.

As long as you have the drunk status, you gain a +2 bonus to saving throws and AC against effects that affect you based on your alignment (such as holy smite, or an Enmity Fate sphere word), as well as to Use Magic Device checks to emulate an alignment. This bonus increases by +1 per 6 points of base attack bonus you possess. While drunk, you produce no alignment aura.

You can expend your drunk status as a free action to be treated as having no alignment for any harmful effect reliant on it for 1 round per 2 points of base attack bonus you possess.

**Associated Feat:** Muddled Morals (*Inner Sea Taverns*).
