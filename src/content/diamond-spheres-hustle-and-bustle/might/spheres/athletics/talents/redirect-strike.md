You can expend your martial focus as an immediate action whenever a creature misses a melee attack against you to redirect the attack to another creature within the attacker’s reach (excluding the original attacker), using their original attack roll result when determining if the attack hits.

If the attacker succeeds this second attack roll, you regain your martial focus.

You may not use this talent in conjunction with (deflect) talents or any other ability that would grant you an attack or combat maneuver due to a missed attack against you.

**Special**—If you have the Reactive Motion or Reflexive Twist talent, you can use this talent as a free action usable outside of your turn without expending martial focus if a creature misses you due to either talent.
