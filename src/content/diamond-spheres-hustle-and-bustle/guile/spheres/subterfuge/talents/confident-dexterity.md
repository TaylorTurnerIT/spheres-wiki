---
name: "Confident Dexterity"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "utility"
---

When you adopt the confident subtlety approach, you also gain a +2 bonus to Sleight of Hand checks made to conceal an object or to lift or plant an item on a creature, increasing by +1 per 4 associated ranks you possess.

Additionally, whenever you lift an item from a creature, you can plant an item on the creature or another creature within your natural reach as a free action (requiring a skill check as normal). If you outwit the creature as a cost, you can roll twice and take the highest result when doing so.
