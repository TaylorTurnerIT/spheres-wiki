**Prerequisites:** Performance sphere ((dance) package), associated skill 8 ranks.

You gain a +10-feet enhancement bonus to the speed of all of your movement modes (excluding land speed) while maintaining this dance; you must already possess such a movement mode to gain this benefit.

All dancers gain all alternate movement modes that you possess (burrow, fly, or swim) equal to your base speed with that movement mode—a fly speed granted this way has a maneuverability one step worse than your own. If such a dancer already possesses such an alternate movement mode, the dancer gains a +10-feet enhancement bonus to the movement mode's speed.

If a creature would move out of your dance area while burrowing, flying, or swimming, they retain its benefits for 1 round after.
