You can make Stealth checks in normal light at a -20 penalty, even when observed. This penalty is reduced to -10 while maintaining the light step approach.

Additionally, a creature suffering from the dazzled condition is not treated as observing you for the purposes of Stealth.

At 5 ranks in Stealth, you can also attempt Stealth checks in bright light with the above penalty.
Associated Feat: Hellcat Stealth (Pathfinder Chronicles — Cheliax: Empire of Devils).
