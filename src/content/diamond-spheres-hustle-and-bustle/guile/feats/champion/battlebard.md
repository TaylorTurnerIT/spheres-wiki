**Prerequisites:** Performance sphere, ability to maintain martial focus.

**Benefit:** You gain a benefit depending on which Performance sphere packages you possess:

- **Act:** You can add the following finale to your acts:
 - **Focusing Finale (finale, 1 free action):** All allies within the act's area that have contributed an action to this act regain their martial focus.
- **Dance:** Any dancer part of your dance that moves their full speed during their turn regains their martial focus.
- **Instrumental:** You can expend your martial focus as part of starting an instrumental—when you first start this instrumental and whenever you maintain it on subsequent rounds, you can restore the martial focus of 1 creature (excluding yourself) within the instrumental's area, as long as you maintain the instrumental as a move action or longer. Once this instrumental has restored the martial focus of a number of creatures equal to your operative modifier (minimum 2), you cannot do so again with the same instrumental.
- **Lyric:** A creature can expend your inspiring lyrics as a move action (or a swift action if you have the Flowing Theatrics talent) to regain their martial focus (in addition to the lyrics normal benefits). Whenever a creature expends your discouraging lyrics, they lose their martial focus.
