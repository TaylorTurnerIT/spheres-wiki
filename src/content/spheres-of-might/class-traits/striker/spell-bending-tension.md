When a willing ally within close range of the striker casts a spell, the striker can spend 3 tension as an immediate action to absorb and redirect the spell's energy. The striker chooses the spell's targets, measures the spell's range from her space, and makes any attack rolls for the spell as if she were the caster, though the original spellcaster attempts any caster level checks, rolls any damage, and is responsible for controlling and concentrating on any subsequent effects of the spell. In addition, the striker also applies one of the following three special effects to the spell:

- Increase the spell's saving throw DCs by 2.
- Grant the caster a +3 bonus to overcome spell resistance.
- If the spell deals damage, the striker can replace her unarmed strikes and natural attacks' damage with one damage type dealt by the spell until the end of her next turn.

*Source: LG*
