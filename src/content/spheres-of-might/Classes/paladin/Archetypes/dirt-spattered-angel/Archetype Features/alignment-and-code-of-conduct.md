A dirt spattered angel may be of any good alignment, and lose all class features except proficiencies if she ever willingly commits an evil act. In addition, she must act with honesty and integrity, never lying or cheating, and she may never willingly fail her duty by breaking an oath or promise, however small. Unlike other paladins, the dirt spattered angel has no restrictions on the tools she may bring to bear against her foes; a dirt spattered angel who uses poison may be frowned upon by some of her peers, but does not lose any of her class abilities.
