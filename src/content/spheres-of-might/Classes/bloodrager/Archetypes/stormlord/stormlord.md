Stormlords, despite the name, are not bound to any particular type of magic or born from any specific magical bloodline. These mighty warriors get their names because their presence ripples across the battlefield like a storm, the clangorous clash of their weapons blending with the explosive hiss of their spells so that each second of battle resounds with the pulsing fury of a mighty storm.

**Proficiencies:** Stor
