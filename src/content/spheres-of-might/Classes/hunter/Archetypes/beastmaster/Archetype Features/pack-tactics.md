---
id: pack-tactics
name: "Pack Tactics"
level: 3
replaces: ["hunter tactics"]
tags: ["cohort"]
---

At 3rd level, the beastmaster automatically grants her teamwork feats to 1 + her Charisma modifier (minimum 2 creatures) animal allies within close range (25 ft. +5 ft. per 2 class levels). They do not need to meet the prerequisites of the shared feats. The selected animal allies may be changed as a swift action.

This replaces hunter tactics.
