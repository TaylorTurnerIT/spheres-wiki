Covert agent, slippery mercenary, or deadly assassin, shinobi are experts in espionage, sabotage, infiltration, assassination, and guerilla warfare. Eschewing armor in favor of mobility, these deadly warriors learn to strike hard and fast before disappearing into the shadows.
