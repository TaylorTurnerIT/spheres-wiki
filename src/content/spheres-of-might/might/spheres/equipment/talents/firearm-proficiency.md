You gain proficiency with all firearms (except siege weapons) and the Gunsmithing feat.

<sup>**Wiki Note:** It is reasonable for any Martial Tradition that grants this proficiency to also grant a battered firearm. ([Source](http://www.giantitp.com/forums/showthread.php?561876-Drop-Dead-Studios-Ask-Me-Anything-3/page4&p=23200219#post23200219))</sup>
