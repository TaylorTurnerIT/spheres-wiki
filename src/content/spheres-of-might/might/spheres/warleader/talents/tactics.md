name: "Tactics"
tier: "base"
tags: []
---

Tactics are coordinated battle plans that require continuing direction from the practitioner to maintain. A creature must have line of sight to, and be able to see, the practitioner to benefit from a tactic. Beginning a tactic is a move action, and it can be maintained each round as a move or swift action. Tactics affect all allied creatures within a radius of 10 ft. + 5ft. per rank in Diplomacy you possess.
