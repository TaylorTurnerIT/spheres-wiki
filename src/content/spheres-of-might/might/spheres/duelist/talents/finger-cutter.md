---
id: "finger-cutter"
name: "Finger Cutter"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "disarm"

---

Whenever you succeed at a disarm attempt against a creature, they take 1 additional point of bleed damage, +1 additional point for every 3 points of base attack bonus you possess. This stacks with other bleed damage granted by the Duelist sphere.

In addition, you no longer provoke attacks of opportunity for attempting the disarm combat maneuver. **Associated Feat:** Improved Disarm.

**Author's Note:** Finger Cutter "stacks" with other sources of duelist bleed, but only with other Duelist sphere bleeds (such as blooded strike and Open Vein).
