**Prerequisites:** Duelist sphere, Vacuum Cut, base attack bonus +11.

Whenever you use the Vacuum Cut talent, you may spend a move action and your martial focus to increase the effect, targeting all creatures within a close range cone. Make a single attack roll and compare the result to each creature within the affected area; all creatures hit by the attack take damage as though you had hit them directly with a melee attack (including any bonus damage to which you would be entitled, such as from the Vital Strike feat). Only damage is dealt; no other effects granted by the attack action are applied.
