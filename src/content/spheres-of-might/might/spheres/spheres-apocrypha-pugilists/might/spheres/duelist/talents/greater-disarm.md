You gain a +1 competence bonus to your disarm combat maneuver checks, as well as to your CMD vs. that maneuver. This bonus increases by +1 for 4 points of base attack bonus you possess. This bonus does not stack with the bonus to this maneuver provided by the Improved or Greater Disarm feats. **Associated Feat:** Improved Disarm.
