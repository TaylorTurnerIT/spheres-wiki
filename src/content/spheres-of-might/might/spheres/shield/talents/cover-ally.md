---
id: "cover-ally"
name: "Cover Ally"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
---

As long as you have martial focus, you may use active defense to benefit an ally when an ally within your shield’s reach is targeted by an attack.

<sup>**Note:** This only provides the benefits of active defense, not the benefit of the base shield. ([Source](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs42esx?SoM-Cover-ally#4))</sup>
