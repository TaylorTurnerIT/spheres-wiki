---
name: "Ranged Impale"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
---

You may attempt to impale a creature with a thrown weapon, although doing so does not cause the impaled creature to move to a square adjacent to you. If you possess the Pinning Impale talent, you may use it with this talent. Ammunition and other weapons which would be destroyed upon successfully hitting a creature may not be used with this talent.
