---
name: "Remote Trigger"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
---

As an immediate action, when a creature comes within the area of effect of one of your traps, you may trigger a trap. Additionally, when a creature succeeds on a Reflex save to avoid triggering one of your snare traps, you may force them to reroll the save as an immediate action. You must be within close range (25 ft. plus 5 ft. per 2 ranks in Craft (traps)) of the trap to use either ability.

If you select the remote trigger talent a second time, you may trigger your trap from medium range of your trap (100 feet + 10 feet per rank in Craft (trap)), and without requiring a creature to enter the area of effect for your trap. Lastly you may set your trap to trigger on a timer after a set amount of time after it is placed (but no longer than its base duration). If the trap can activate multiple times, you can set the time for each activation individually.
