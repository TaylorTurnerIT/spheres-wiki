You can use your trained senses to read the movements of the wind and other factors in the environment to improve your accuracy with ranged attacks. As long as you have martial focus, you may spend a swift action to halve the penalties to ranged attacks inflicted by wind, rain, or other weather in regard to your next ranged attack, made before the end of your turn.
