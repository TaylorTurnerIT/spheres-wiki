name: "Iron Grip"
tier: "basic"
tags: []
---

You gain a +1 competence bonus to your grapple combat maneuver checks, as well as to your CMD vs. that maneuver. This bonus increases by +1 for every 4 points of base attack bonus you possess. This bonus does not stack with the bonus to this maneuver provided by the Improved or Greater Grapple feats. **Associated Feat:** Improved Grapple.
