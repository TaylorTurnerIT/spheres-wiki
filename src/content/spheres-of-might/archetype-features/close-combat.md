At 1st level, the brutal pummeler gains the Boxing and Open Hand combat spheres as bonus talents. If the brutal pummeler already possesses one of these base spheres, she may instead choose any one combat talent she qualifies for from either sphere.

This replaces the AC bonus class feature.
