At 20th level, the blood-soaked demon is cloaked in evil dark and powerful enough to give pause to any true demon or devil. The blood-soaked demon gains DR 5/-; this stacks with any other DR of the same type he possesses. In addition, the blood-soaked demon may use his touch of corruption ability as a swift action. Finally, the blood-soaked demon draws power from crushing the will to fight out of his enemies; whenever he defeats an opponent whose CR is equal to at least half his character level (rounded down, minimum 1/2), he regains 1 use of his touch of corruption ability; if the opponent also had the good subtype, his next use of touch of corruption automatically deals the maximum amount of damage, and he may apply 1 additional cruelty to its effects.

This replaces unholy champion.
