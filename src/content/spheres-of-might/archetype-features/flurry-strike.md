---

Starting at 1st level, whenever the street fighter uses the attack action to attack with an unarmed strike or a monk special weapon, he may make one additional attack with an unarmed strike or monk special weapon as a free action. The street fighter must decide whether or not to use this ability before making the first attack roll for her attack action. Attacks made using this ability apply the street fighter's full Strength bonus to the damage roll, regardless of whether the weapon is wielded with two hands or with an off-hand. This extra attack cannot be used with the extra attack granted by the Dual Wielding sphere.

This replaces flurry of blows.
