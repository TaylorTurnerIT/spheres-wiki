Suit pilots have one goal, and one goal only: Creating the best suit of powered armor they can.

---

+ ##993300|Favored Class Bonuses##

**Aasimar:** Increase the benefit of your danger sense class feature by +1/3.

**Created:** Gain +1/8th of a new invention.

**Dwarf:** Increase the range of your trap insight class feature by 1 foot.

**Elf:** For every 4 times this favored class bonus is taken, your inventions count as possessing one fewer improvements when calculating the penalties other creatures suffers when using them.

**Gnome:** Gain +1/8th of a new invention.

**Goblin:** Gain 1/3rd of an additional gadget each day which can only be used as a detonator.

**Halfling:** Add a +1/2 bonus to all Disable Device checks made to pick a lock or disarm or reset a trap.

**Hobgoblin:** When you deal fire, cold, electricity, or acid damage with an invention, your invention ignores 1 point of resistance for that particular element.

**Human:** Gain +1/6th of a technical insight.

**Kobold:** Gain 1/6th of a Trap sphere talent.

**Orc:** Gain +1 energy resistance to one kind of energy (+10 maximum per energy type).

**Tatulani:** Gain +1/6th of a technical insight.

**Tiefling:** Whenever you deal damage to a target within 30 ft. that is either flat-footed, that you are flanking, or that has lost its Dexterity bonus to ac, you deal an additional +1/2 damage to that target.

+ ##993300|Class Equipment##

++++ Gadget Guide [TS:WAT]
**Aura** moderate Creation; **CL** 8th
**Slot** none; **Price** 14,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This book contains detailed schematics for creating a specific invention. A creature with the inventions class feature can use these instructions to create an additional invention (that does not count against their normal limit) consisting of a base invention and one associated improvement, with the specific options chosen when this item is created. The creature following this guide must still meet any other prerequisites for creating the invention (including having any relevant insights), and cannot create any other inventions with the same base. For example, if a gadget guide teaches how to create an improved gloves invention with the webbing improvement, a creature following this guide cannot create any other glove inventions. A creature can only create an invention from one gadget guide at a time.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Apparatus, Creation sphere, creator must have the inventions class feature; **Cost** 7,000 gp

++++ Tinker's Manual [TS:WAT]
**Aura** strong Creation; **CL** 12th
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This heavy tome is sometimes used as a reference for higher education, particularly in areas with extensive schooling opportunities. Despite being several hundred pages thick, every page is needed to detail the complex interactions of certain chemicals and materials. A creature with the technical insights class feature can study this manual to gain the benefits of one technical insight, chosen when this manual is written. They retain this knowledge for 24 hours, but can read this manual as part of their morning preparations to refresh their knowledge of the insight. Any existing options dependent on this insight (such as an invention using the mechanical insight) fail immediately if a creature loses access to the insight those options depend on. A creature cannot benefit from more than one of these manuals at a time, and studying a different manual erases their knowledge of the previous one.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Apparatus, Creation sphere, creator must have the technical insights class feature, creator must know the technical insight added to this manual; **Cost** 7,500 gp

++++ Trapspotter [TS:WAT]
**Aura** weak Divination; **CL** 1st
**Slot** eyes; **Price** 2,000 gp; **Weight** 1/8 lbs.

This slender monocle hooks over one ear to stay in place without problems. As a standard action, a creature with the trapfinding ability can peer through this monocle to search for magical auras within 25 feet, and may continue looking for magical auras as long as they concentrate. This functions as the basic divine ability of the Divination sphere.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Marvelous Item, Divination sphere; **Cost** 1,000 gp
