Mechanical enhancements are hardwired into your brain. Improvement benefits from an exocortex cannot be applied to any character other than the technician.

**Prerequisites:** None

### ##993300|Improvements##

#### Combat Aptitude
The technician gains a combat feat of his choice as a bonus feat, chosen when this invention is created. This improvement may be selected once for every 6 technician levels the technician possesses, each time selecting a new combat feat.

#### Combat Tracking
As a move action, the technician can designate a foe for the exocortex to track. As long as that target is in sight, the exocortex feeds the technician telemetry, vulnerabilities, and combat tactics, allowing the technician to make attacks against that target and use combat sphere abilities on the target as if his base attack bonus from the technician levels were equal to his technician level. Designating another target causes the technician to immediately lose this bonus against the previous target.

This ability counts as the slayer's studied target ability for the purpose of feats and for meeting prerequisites.

#### Echolocators (Requires Electrical Insight)
The technician gains blindsense (Echosense) out to 20 feet. This improvement may be selected multiple times, each time increasing the blindsense range by 20 feet.

#### Memory Module (Requires Chemical Insight)
The technician gains Skill Focus as a bonus feat. By spending martial focus, the technician may reduce the time needed to use the skill selected with Skill Focus. If the skill use would normally take 2 rounds or longer, the recipient of the aid finishes in half the time. If the skill use would normally take 1 round or less, reduce the time it takes by one step along this list: 1 round, 1 full-round action, 1 standard action, 1 move action, 1 swift action, 1 immediate action (taken at the end of the turn).

#### Multitargeting (Requires Combat Tracking)
The technician may use combat tracking on up to three creatures at a time. This improvement may be selected a second time, in which case the technician may use combat tracking on any number of targets.

#### Sonar Pulse (Requires Echolocators)
The echosense from echolocators improves to blindsight (Echosight).
