The font of imbuement is built upon the values and meanings imposed upon inanimate objects, and tapping into it allows the conduit to construct narratives and metaphysical throughlines that bring new applications to the mundane.

++++ Font-Bound Esoterica: Sprite Manipulation
The conduit may use evoke dweomer with technological items as if they were magical items, treating technological items as having no school or sphere of magic. An exhausted technological item functions as if it has no charges.

When using this esoterica, the conduit creates a Technomancy Sphere sprite which can be augmented with the following talents: Concealed Sprite, Lingering Sprite, Sprite Sustenance.
If the item used is a technological item or a magic item with the Technomancy sphere in its construction requirements, they can augment the esoterica with the following options: any (program) talent, Rapid Transfer, Reactive Offload.

++++ Unravel Arcana: Protection Plan
At 7th level, the conduit can unravel arcana as a free action (usable outside of their turn) before a creature attempts a combat maneuver or MSB check against the conduit's equipment or active magic effects, forcing the attacker to roll twice and take the lower result.
The conduit can exhaust the attending item to use this ability when rolling on a glitch table for an item, allowing the conduit to roll twice and take the result of their choosing

++++ Evolve Dweomer: Reroute Power (Any technological item)
At 10th level, whenever the technological item would expend charges or deplete batteries, you may expend those charges or deplete batteries from any other technological items you are carrying or wearing. In addition, whenever you use evoke dweomer with the item, it gains 1 temporary charge or battery plus an additional charge or battery for every 4 conduit levels beyond 10th. These temporary charges or batteries last for 1 hour.

++++ Protoarcanum: Unfathomable Potential (Technomancy or Veilweaving sphere, or any technological item)
At 17th level, as a free action, the conduit can infuse their armaments with the dreams and visions of countless futurists and engineers, enabling them to truly operate without limitations. For 1 minute, whenever the conduit uses any technological or magical item in their possession which require charges, those charges are not spent. In this state, whenever the conduit depletes batteries or uses of unique ammunition for a technological item, those batteries and ammunition are not depleted and can be reused. If the conduit possess any receptacles they can invest essence in, each of these receptacles is considered to have the maximum possible amount of essence invested in them.

In addition, while in this state, the conduit gains a force field which grants 100 bonus hit points which replenish 10 hit points per turn. Any damage dealt to the conduit or their equipment is subtracted from these hit points first.

++++ Apogean Font
At 20th level, the conduit may craft any item in half the normal time and gains DR 10/adamantine. In addition, they no longer need to eat, sleep, or breathe, become immune to fatigue, exhaustion, and nonlethal damage, and no longer age.

+++ ##4B0092|Font-Bound Wonders##
The conduit with the imbuement font has access to the following wonders:

++++ Evoke Essence (Veilweaving sphere or evocation spell)*
The conduit can exhaust an item as a swift action to gain a number of points of temporary essence equal to their operative modifier (minimum 2). These points of temporary essence last until the item recovers its magical charges, though you may delay the item regaining its magical charges up to 1 hour per conduit level. Any additional uses of this ability cause essence from previous uses to be lost.

++++ Fantastical Artifice (Fallen Fey sphere or conjuration spell)*
You can create a Fallen Fey sphere Aelfwine, Enchanting Music, or Fairy Dust fey link, which can be augmented with any of the listed talents or the following talents: Bounty of the Fey, Greater Fey Link, or Share Link

++++ Mechanical Ward
The conduit is able to treat any allied construct within 30 feet (as well as any of its equipment) as a technological item they are carrying for the purpose of font abilities.

++++ Impromptu Veilweaving (Veilweaving sphere or conjuration spell)*
When the conduit gains this talent, they choose three veils that can be shaped in any of the following chakra slots: Hands, Feet, Head, Wrists, Shoulders, Headband, Neck, Belt, Chest or Body. The conduit can evoke dweomer as a standard action to instantly and temporarily shape one of the veils chosen with this talent; the conduit must be able to legally shape the veil in its chakra slot, they may bind the shaped veil as part of shaping it if they have the ability to bind veils to the slot it is shaped in, and they cannot shape that veil to an esoteric chakra slot (Blood, Ring, Storm, and so on). If the conduit's level matches or exceeds the minimum level to select a Veilweaving Sphere (bind) talent which could be used to bind the veil they shaped, the conduit may augment the evoked dweomer to gain the ability to bind the veil to that slot for as long as the veil is shaped.

The veil shaped using this talent persists until the conduit regains uses of evoke dweomer, upon which it is immediately unshaped. The conduit can have a maximum of one veil shaped using this ability at one time; once they reach this maximum, they cannot use this talent.
