Rather than having a single repertoire of techniques, the armiger has a unique repertoire of techniques for each of their customized weapons. Any feat or effect which increases the number of techniques the armiger may have in their repertoire applies to each of the armiger's repertoires. In addition, the armiger may draw or stow a technique script as part of the same action to draw or stow a customized weapon.

*Source: BGA*
