---
id: conduit-chain-hack
name: "Chain Hack"
type: class-trait
system: might
tags: []
className: conduit
featureId: conduit-wonders

---

Whenever the conduit successfully hacks a magical item, they can spend an immediate action to hack one additional magical item within 30 feet of the original item, plus one additional item for every 6 conduit levels they possess.
