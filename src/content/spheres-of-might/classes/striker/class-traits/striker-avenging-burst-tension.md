When the striker is hit by a melee attack, she may spend 1 point of tension as an immediate action to vent energy on the attacker. The attacker takes 1d4 points of fire and electricity damage per 2 striker levels (Reflex save half, DC 10 + 1/2 the striker's level + her practitioner modifier). The striker may spend 1 additional point of tension to increase the damage dice from 1d4 to 1d6, or 2 additional points of tension to increase them from 1d4 to 1d8.

*Source: LG*
