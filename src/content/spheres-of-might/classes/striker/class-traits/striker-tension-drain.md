As a standard action, the striker may make a melee touch attack against a creature. The target must attempt a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 the striker's level + her practitioner modifier) or become sickened for a number of rounds equal to the striker's level. On a successful touch attack, the striker gains 1 point of tension, or 3 points of tension if the target fails their saving throw.

*Source: LG*
