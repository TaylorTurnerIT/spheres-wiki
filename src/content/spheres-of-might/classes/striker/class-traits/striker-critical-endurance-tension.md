If the striker is subject to a critical hit or instance of precision damage, she may spend 1 tension to gain a 25% chance to negate this additional damage as per the fortification armor special ability. For every additional point of tension spent, this chance increases by 25%, to a maximum of 100%.

*Source: LG*
