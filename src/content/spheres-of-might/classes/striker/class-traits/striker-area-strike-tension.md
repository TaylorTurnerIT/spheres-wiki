The striker may spend 2 tension to have the next natural attack she makes target all creatures in a 15-foot cone or a 25-foot line. For each additional tension you spend to activate this adaptation, the striker may increase the cone's length by 5 feet or increase the line's length by 10 feet. Regardless of how many creatures are hit by this attack, she cannot apply the effects of an attack action to more than one target (as if she were making an attack with a scatter weapon).

*Source: LG*
