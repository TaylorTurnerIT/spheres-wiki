level: 1

---

At 1st level, the battered detective gains the Alchemy sphere as a bonus sphere. If he already possesses the Alchemy sphere, he may instead choose any talent he qualifies for from that sphere instead. In addition, when using Craft (alchemy) to create an alchemical item, an investigator gains a competence bonus equal to his class level on the Craft (alchemy) check. An investigator can use Craft (alchemy) to identify potions as if using detect magic. He must hold the potion for 1 round to make such a check.
