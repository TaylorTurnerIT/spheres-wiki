---
id: beastfriend
name: "Beastfriend"
level: 4
replaces: ["improved empathic link"]
tags: []

---

At 4th level, all creatures of the animal type have a starting disposition toward the beastmaster of no worse than indifferent and will not attack the beastmaster or her allies within close range (25 ft. + 5 ft. per 2 levels) unless commanded to or attacked by the beastmaster or her allies first. She may also improve a creature's disposition by 2 steps when using the Animal Empathy talent by increasing the DC of the check by 10.

This replaces improved empathic link.
