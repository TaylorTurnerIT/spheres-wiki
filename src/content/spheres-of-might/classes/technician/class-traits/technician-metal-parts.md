The invention is built of steel. It has a hardness of 10 instead of 5, and gains a +2 bonus to its natural armor. An invention may gain this improvement a total of three times; if gained twice, the invention is built of mithril, gaining a hardness of 15 and a +4 bonus to its natural armor. If gained three times, the invention is built of adamantine, with a hardness of 20 and a +6 bonus to natural AC.

The invention is twice as expensive to build. If taken twice is costs 5x as much. If taken three times, it costs 10x as much.
