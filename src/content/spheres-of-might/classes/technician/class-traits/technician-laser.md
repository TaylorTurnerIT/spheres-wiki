With pressurized gas and an electrical array, the wielder can fire a short-range laser as a standard action, dealing 1d8 fire damage per technician level to a target. The laser has a range of close range (25 ft. +5 ft. per two levels), and requires a ranged touch attack to hit. This laser may be used 3 times per day. This improvement may be gained multiple times. Each time it is gained, increase the number of daily uses by 3.

**Note:** If the technician possesses mechanical insight, this improvement grants 4 uses instead of 3.
