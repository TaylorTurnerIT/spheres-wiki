---

At 4th level, the nature's blade can choose to not gain a hunter's bond, but may instead expand their combat training by gaining either the Beastmaster or Warleader sphere and one additional talent from the corresponding sphere as bonus talents. If the nature's blade already has one of these spheres, he may instead choose any talent from that sphere for which he qualifies.

This modifies hunter's bond.
