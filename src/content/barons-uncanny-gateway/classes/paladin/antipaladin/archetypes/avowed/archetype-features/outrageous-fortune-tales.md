---

The avowed gains the Fate sphere as a bonus sphere. Whenever she creates a consecration or motif, she may spend a use of divine touch to treat her class level as her caster level for the effect or ignore any one general drawback she possesses for the purpose of using the consecration. Multiple uses of divine touch may be spent on the same effect.

This replaces lay on hands or touch of corruption.
