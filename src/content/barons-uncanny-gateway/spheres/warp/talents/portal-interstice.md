**Prerequisites:** Warp sphere (Extradimensional Room, Mass Teleport [mass], Portal (advanced), Wormhole (space)), caster level 5th.

Whenever you create a portal that leads between two locations, you may create an extradimensional room that both the entry and exit portal connect to (rather than connecting to each other) as part of the same action and without spending additional spell points. This extradimensional room lasts for the duration of the portal.
