**Prerequisites:** Warp Sphere (Manifest Planar Zone (advanced)), caster level 5th

When using the Manifest Planar Zone talent, the area increases to a number of 10-foot cubes equal to your caster level and the duration increases to 1 hour per caster level if you spend a spell point.

If your permanent caster level is at least 10 and you instead spend 2 spell points, the effect has a permanent duration and dispelling attempts affect it as if it were a magic item. Effects that would destroy a magic item (such as Greater Counterspell) can also destroy the planar zone. A planar zone that is made permanent becomes stationary and can no longer move with a creature.
