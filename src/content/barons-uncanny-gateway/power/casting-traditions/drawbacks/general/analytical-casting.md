---
id: analytical-casting
name: "Analytical Casting"
---
Your magic requires that you know the target particularly well. When you create a magic sphere, each of the targets or the targeted area must be within the area of your pathing or an area you have analyzed. Alternatively, all targets must have been successfully analyzed, scouted, or be the subject of your theories. Casting in circumstances where none of these conditions are met requires a successful MSB check (DC 20 + ½ the caster level).
