**Prerequisites:** Warp sphere, caster level 5th.

You may place different security measures on portals you encounter or create. If you are applying any of these effects to a portal you did not create, you must succeed on a magic skill check against the portal. A portal with an unspecified origin should have an MSD of at least 21. Portals created by particularly powerful entities such as gods or demon lords should have MSD between 31 and 51.

**Disguise Portals (requires Illusion sphere):** You may spend a spell point as a standard action or as part of the action to create a portal to alter the appearance of a portal using a glamor that lasts for 1 minute per caster level. This can include effects such as rendering the portal invisible if you possess the Suppression talent. If you possess the Permanent Illusion talent, you may instead spend 3 spell points to place a permanent glamor over the portal.

**Portal Seal (requires Protection sphere):** You may spend a spell point to place restrictions over who can pass through a portal you create. The portal can discern between entering creatures with all the same parameters that a Protection Sphere Glyph can and can permit or deny creatures based on those parameters, and if the portal can lead to multiple locations it may send creatures to different exit points based on different parameters. The knowledge or requirements to pass through a portal to a specific location is often called a "portal key". A portal seal can also be used to completely halt all entry to a portal or to make a portal one-way (allowing entry from one side but not the other). If you place a portal seal on a portal which already possesses a portal seal, the new portal seal replaces the old one for the duration of the new portal key. A portal key lasts for 1 hour per caster level. If you possess the Permanent Ward talent, you may instead spend 3 spell points to place a permanent portal seal over a portal.

**Portal Surveillance (requires Divination Sphere (Scrying)):** You may spend 2 spell points to establish a continual viewing sensor around a portal that allows you to observe any movement through it. This sensor lasts for 1 hour per caster level. If you possess the Greater Scrying talent, you may instead spend 3 spell points to place a permanent sensor over the portal.
