**Prerequisites:** Warp sphere (Unseeing Teleport), caster level 5th.

When you use your teleport and you are one of the creatures teleported, you can cause your teleportation magic to rip through dimensions erratically as a last-ditch escape measure, sending any teleported creatures to a random plane. A creature can be affected by randomized planeshift only if it is currently not on its home plane (although targets can, by chance, end up traveling to their home plane as a result of this effect). Subjects always appear in a location that is not inherently harmful, but the exact destination is otherwise random. The specific plane is determined by rolling on a table of accessible planes, with a sample table given below; the location on the destination plane where the transported creatures arrive is completely subject to the GM's whim. A creature cannot be affected by randomized planeshift more than once every 24 hours.

If you possess the Manifest Planar Zone talent, you may use this talent in conjunction with Randomized Planeshift to make your planar zone emulate a random plane rather than a plane you have selected with Manifest Planar Zone, rolling as normal.
If you possess the Portal talent, you may cause any portals you create to randomly planeshift any creatures who pass through it. If you possess the Portal Security talent, you may cause your portal seal to randomly planeshift creatures who pass through the portal (including making only creatures under certain parameters randomly planeshift).
