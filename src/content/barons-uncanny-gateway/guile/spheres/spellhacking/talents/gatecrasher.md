**Prerequisites:** Spellhacking Sphere

**Target:** A magic portal that connects two or more locations, a magic effect which inhibits dimensional travel, or a magic glyph or similar effect which can discern certain qualities about a creature which passes within its range.
You alter the planar construction of a portal to apply one or more of the following effects.

**Break Seal:** You may spend 1 skill leverage to attempt to automatically dispel an effect that stops dimensional travel (such as a Quantum Lock (ward)).

**Connect Portal:** You may spend 1 skill leverage to reconfigure the portal for the duration of the hack, changing its destination so that entering the portal causes you to exit out of any portal you have passed through within 1 day per rank in an associated skill you possess. This redirection automatically fails if the portal chosen as the endpoint is no longer active.

**Hazardous Travel:** You may apply a (mishap) to a portal, causing the mishap’s effects to occur on both sides of the portal when a target travels through it. If you have at least 5 ranks in an associated skill, you may instead attempt to hack a random item carried by the next creature to pass through the portal.

**Key Bypass:** You may spend 1 skill leverage to hack a glyph or similar magical effect which can detect certain parameters about creatures in its area. If you successfully hack the effect, up to one creature per rank in an associated skill you possess are treated as having whatever traits or passwords you wish for the purpose of the magic effect for the duration of the hack.

**Maintain Passageway:** You may stabilize a portal which could lead to multiple exit locations (including random locations) such that anything that travels through the portal instead arrives at the portal’s most recent destination until the hack ends. In addition, the portal cannot be dismissed or closed until this hack ends.
When determining the DC of the hack attempt, a portal with an unspecified origin should have a caster level of at least 10. Portals created by particularly powerful entities such as gods or demon lords should have caster levels between 20 and 40.
