**Prerequisites:** Navigation sphere (Beyonder).

You may select the following acclimation benefits. Any creature affected by one of the following acclimations gains a fly speed of 30 ft (perfect) in areas of zero gravity or subjective directional gravity. For every 5 ranks you possess in one of your associated skills, this fly speed increases by 10 feet.

**Astral Mastery:** While on the Astral Plane, an acclimated target may use their Intelligence modifier in place of their Strength modifier and their Wisdom modifier in place of their Dexterity modifier for all purposes.

**Dream Mastery:** You gain a +3 bonus on Charisma checks to determine your initial condition when your lucid body enters a dream and when attempting impossible feats in a dreamscape. This bonus increases by +1 for every 5 ranks you possess in one of your associated skills. If someone creates a dream effect that includes you as a target while you are dreaming, You can enter a shared dreamscape as described in the dream council spell without spending a casting of dream or dream council.

**Ethereal Mastery:** While on the Ethereal Plane, an acclimated target may spend a swift action to increase their movement speed by any amount until the end of their turn. For every 5 feet by which they increase their movement speed, the acclimated target suffers 1 point of nonlethal damage which cannot be avoided by nonlethal damage immunity, reduced by damage reduction, and cannot be healed through any means except rest (a full night’s rest heals all nonlethal damage caused by this effect at once).

**Vacuum Mastery:** While in a vacuum such as the vacuum of space, an acclimated target automatically succeeds on Constitution checks to resist the effects of the vacuum and takes no Constitution damage from holding their breath in the vacuum.

**Associated Feat:** Lucid Dreamer
