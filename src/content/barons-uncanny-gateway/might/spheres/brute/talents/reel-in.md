When you perform a drag combat maneuver as a swift, move, standard, or immediate action, you can choose not to move with your target, instead bringing your target closer to you. If you do this, you can’t drag the target any farther than the distance it takes to move it adjacent to you, even if your result was high enough to drag the target farther. As long as you have martial focus, a creature dragged into a square adjacent to you provokes an attack of opportunity from you.
**Associated Feats:** Balor Whip, Improved Balor Whip
