You may choose for your poison to not cause fatigue instantly, but instead to enhance the concentration of those  subjected to it (this talent cannot be used with a contact or inhaled poison). Those who are under the effects of this poison gain a +3 alchemical bonus to their concentration checks, but suffer an equivalent penalty to Perception checks  for 1 minute. At the end of this talent’s duration, the creature must succeed at a Fortitude save or be fatigued for 10 minutes. If you possess at least 10 ranks in Craft (alchemy), this bonus increases by +2. A creature cannot benefit from a concentration aid more than once per hour.
