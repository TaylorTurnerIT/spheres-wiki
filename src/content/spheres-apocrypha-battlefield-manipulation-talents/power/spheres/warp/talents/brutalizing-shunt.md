name: "Brutalizing Shunt"
tier: advanced
tags: []
---

**Prerequisites:** Warp sphere (Create Gap (space) or Unseeing Teleport).

When a creature would be shunted into an already-occupied space by your teleport or the end of your create gap, the target takes 1d6 additional damage for every 5 caster levels you possess from being shunted from an occupied space and becomes staggered for 1 round per caster level unless they succeed at a Fortitude save.
