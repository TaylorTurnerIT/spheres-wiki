**Prerequisites:** Sniper sphere, base attack bonus +15.

You fire your weapon in such a way that it shreds through the hard plate and scales of your enemies. Your deadly shot ignores all armor, natural armor, and shield bonuses the target may have.

**Associated Feat:** Pinpoint Targeting.
