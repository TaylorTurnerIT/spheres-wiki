---
id: "snagging-steel"
name: "Snagging Steel"
tier: "basic"
---

You may use a melee weapon to perform a snag, adding its bonuses to your snag's attack and causing the snag to deal damage equal to the weapon's enhancement bonus.

You may take this talent a second time, in which case the snag deals damage equal to your Strength modifier + 1/2 your base attack bonus + the weapon's enhancement bonus.
