**Prerequisites:** Alteration sphere (Vermin Transformation (transformation)) or web bestial trait; poison bestial trait or racial poison ability.

**Benefits:** Any target which becomes entangled by your web is exposed to the poison granted by your bestial trait, shapeshift, or other features that grant you a poison. They are exposed an additional time whenever they fail a check to escape your web.
