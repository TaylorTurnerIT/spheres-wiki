---
name: "Soul Consumption"
tier: advanced
tags: []
---

**Prerequisites:** Death sphere (Create Soul Gem (advanced)), Life sphere (Revitalize), caster level 5th.

When you use Revitalize, you may destroy a soul gem in your possession as part of creating the effect. This increases the fast healing from Revitalize to an amount equal to your caster level for a number of rounds equal to half the CR of the soul trapped within the gem, rounded down (minimum 0).

If you possess the Resurrection talent, you may use a soul gem containing a creature's soul in place of the creature's body to resurrect them.
