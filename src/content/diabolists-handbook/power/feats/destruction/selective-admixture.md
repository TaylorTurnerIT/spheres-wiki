---
id: "selective-admixture"
name: "Selective Admixture"
sphere: destruction
tags: []
tier: feat
summary: "Apply an Admixture effect to creatures omitted by Selective Blast instead of creatures damaged by the blast."
---

Amidst ruin, you have learned to support those whom you favor.

**Prerequisites:** Destruction sphere (Admixture, Selective Blast), any one Admixture feat.

**Benefit:** When you omit creatures from being affected by your blast using Selective Blast, you may choose to have the effect of any Admixture on the blast affect only the omitted creatures rather than affecting damaged creatures.
