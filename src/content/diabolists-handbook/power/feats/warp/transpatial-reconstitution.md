name: Transpatial Reconstitution
sphere: warp
dualSphere: life
tier: feat
---

Your form in this particular world is one of many. Material from beyond can replace any damaged components.

**Prerequisites:** Life sphere, Warp sphere.

**Benefit:** When teleporting a creature, you may increase the casting time by one step or spend a spell point to target the teleported creature with a cure, invigorate, or restore effect, paying additional costs for the effect as normal. If you have both the Mass Healing and Mass Teleport talents, you may target multiple teleported creatures with the Life sphere effect used.
