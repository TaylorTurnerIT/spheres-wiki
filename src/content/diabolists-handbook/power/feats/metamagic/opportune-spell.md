tags:
  - metamagic
tier: feat
summary: "Replace an attack of opportunity with an immediate touch or strike-compatible magic sphere effect."
---
Incredible power waits to strike any who would cross you.

**Prerequisite:** Magic skill bonus +7.

**Benefit:** When you would make an attack of opportunity, as an immediate action, you may use any magic sphere effect in conjunction with a strike talent or with a range of touch in place of the attack of opportunity. You cannot use this feat with an effect whose casting time is longer than 1 full-round action.

**Cost:** +4 spell points
