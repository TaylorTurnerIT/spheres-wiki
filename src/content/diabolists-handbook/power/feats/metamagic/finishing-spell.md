name: Finishing Spell
tags:
  - metamagic
tier: feat
---
Your magic is not troubled by the endeavors of the weak.

**Prerequisite:** Magic skill bonus +10.

**Benefit:** Whenever a finishing spell targets a creature whose remaining hit points are less than 4 x the caster level of the effect, that creature takes a -2 penalty to saving throws against the effect. This penalty increases to -4 if the creature's remaining hit points are less than 2 x the caster level of the effect.

**Cost:** +2 spell points
