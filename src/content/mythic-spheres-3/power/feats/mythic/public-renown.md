---
id: public-renown
name: Public Renown
tags:
  - mythic
tier: feat

summary: "Spend mythic power to immediately regain influence over a creature when it would otherwise end due to conditions or expenditure."
---

When your influence over a creature would end due to the creature gaining a condition or you expending influence, you may spend a use of mythic power to immediately regain influence over the creature.
