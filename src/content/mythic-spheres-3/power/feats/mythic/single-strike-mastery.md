name: Single Strike Mastery
tags:
  - mythic
tier: feat

summary: "Spend mythic power to further increase the sneak attack damage dice from single strike mastery by up to two sizes (max d12)."
---

When you increase the damage dice from sneak attack using single strike mastery, you may spend a use of mythic power to increase the size of the dice by two additional sizes (to a maximum of d12).
