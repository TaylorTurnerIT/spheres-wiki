---
id: insinuating-incantation
name: Insinuating Incantation
tags:
  - mythic
tier: feat
---

When you use Insinuating Incantation, if you spend a use of mythic power, you do not need to spend 3 additional spell points when using the quip or may use the quip as a free action rather than a swift action. You may spend 2 uses of mythic power to gain both benefits.
