name: Advanced Tactics
tags:
  - mythic
tier: feat

summary: "You may roll your mythic surge die plus half your tier instead of 1d6 when changing tactics, and can do so after learning the consequences of your roll."
---

When you spend skill leverage to change tactics, you may choose to roll your mythic surge die rather than 1d6 and gain a bonus to the roll equal to half your mythic tier. You may spend skill leverage to change tactics after you learn the consequences of your roll, allowing you to change the result.
