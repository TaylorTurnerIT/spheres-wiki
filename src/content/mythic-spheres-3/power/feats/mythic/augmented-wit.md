---
id: augmented-wit
name: Augmented Wit
tags:
  - mythic
tier: feat
---

When you successfully outwit a creature by tricking it when it has a disorientation condition, by embarrassing it when it has the impressed condition, or when using a special outwit option granted by an ability you possess, you may spend a use of mythic power instead of the cost to outwit, allowing you to outwit the creature one additional time. A creature cannot be outwitted by this ability more than once per round.
