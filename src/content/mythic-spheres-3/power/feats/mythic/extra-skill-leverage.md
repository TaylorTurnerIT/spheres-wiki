---
id: extra-skill-leverage
name: Extra Skill Leverage
tags:
  - mythic
tier: feat

summary: "Increase your maximum skill leverage by your tier and expend mythic power when gaining skill leverage to receive additional uses."
---

Your maximum skill leverage increases by your mythic tier. In addition, when you gain skill leverage you may spend a use of mythic power to gain a number of uses of skill leverage equal to your mythic tier.
