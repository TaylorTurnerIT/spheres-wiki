name: "Divine Relationships"
system: power
type: talent
sphere: divination
tier: basic

You can determine whether or not two or more creatures have any sort of relationship. The amount of information gleaned depends on how long you study a particular area or set of subjects.

**1st round:** Whether or not the subjects share any sort of interpersonal relationship (be it a friendship, romance, or blood relation).

**2nd round:** Number of related subjects and their general relationships to one another. The types of relationship divined by this talent are generally divided between blood, friendship, professional, familial, and romantic, although other terminology may be utilized based on setting at GM discretion. For example, divining a couple would reveal a romantic relationship while divining a parent and their biological child would reveal both a blood and familial relationship.

**3rd round:** Precise relationship between subjects in the area. In the above examples, this divination would clarify that the couple are committed lovers and that the family is parent and child. A target's Will save prevents you from determining its exact relationship with another creature, and only one subject needs to succeed at this save to prevent you from determining its relationship with another creature. (So, for instance, if two brothers are in the area and one succeeds at his Will save, you could tell that they are siblings, but could not distinguish whether they are full- or half-siblings.) 

Although this talent can discern blood relations and personal relationships between people, it cannot divine legal associations and would have no way of determining constructs such as marriage, parental rights, or the legitimacy of children.
