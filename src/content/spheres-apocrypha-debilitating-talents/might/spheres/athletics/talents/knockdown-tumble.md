---
name: "Knockdown Tumble"
tier: "basic"
tags:
  - "motion"
---

When you successfully make an Acrobatics check to avoid provoking an attack of opportunity from an opponent when you move through its threatened area or its space, you can expend your martial focus to try to knock them down. The opponent must succeed on a Reflex save or be knocked prone. **Associated Feat:** Tangle Feet.
