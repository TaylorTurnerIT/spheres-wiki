Scattered among ancient tomes and strange relics are archives of an arcane tradition known as sin magic, a dangerous tradition which allows a wizard to unleash their unbridled vice upon the world. Sin magic comes in seven varieties, each tied to a deadly sin that serves as a foundation for their unique magic.

* **Wrath:** Draining Casting, Madness Mantra<br>
* **Lust:** Emotional Casting, Mental Focus<br>
* **Pride:** Center Of Power, Consciousness Linked<br>
* **Greed:** Expensive Locus, Focus Casting or Galvanized Casting<br>
* **Envy:** Coy Caster, Unsettling Casting<br>
* **Sloth:** Charged Spells, Painful Magic (replaces Prepared Caster)<br>
* **Gluttony:** Addictive Casting<br>
* **Wrath:** Deathful Magic<br>
* **Lust:** Draw Magic<br>
* **Pride:** Metamagic Expert<br>
* **Greed:** Empowered Abilities<br>
* **Envy:** Overwhelming Power<br>
* **Sloth:** Easy Focus<br>
* **Gluttony:** Overcharge
