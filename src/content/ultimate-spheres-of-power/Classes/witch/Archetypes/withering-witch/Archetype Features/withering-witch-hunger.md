The withering witch may corrupt a sphere ability or hex that persists over an area, such as a darkness of the Dark sphere or a totem of the War sphere. The result is that the zone has a small amount of intelligence - just enough so that it can feel hunger. Whenever a creature with Hit Dice equal to or greater than half the caster level of the withering witch fails a saving throw or is reduced to 0 or fewer hit points against the hungry area, the zone grows by 5 ft.
