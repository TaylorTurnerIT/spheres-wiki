: The void conduit's avatar's mirror of the master ability does not "mirror" the effects of any (form) talents borrowed this way, as the avatar already benefits from this (form) talent. If the void conduit selects a (form) talent that would assign a specific ability score or alter saving throw progression (such as the Amorphous Creature (form) talent or the Skill Companion (form) talent), do not adjust the void conduit's ability scores or saving throws.
