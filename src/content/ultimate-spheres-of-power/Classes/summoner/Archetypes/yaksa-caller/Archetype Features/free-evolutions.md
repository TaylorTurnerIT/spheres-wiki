*Essence Pool:* Starting at first level the yaksa gains an essence pool equal to the caller's class level. This replaces the standard eidolon's evolution pool.

*Veilweaving:* At 1st level and every 3 levels thereafter (4th, 7th, 10th, etc.), the yaksa increases his veils shaped by 1 (to a maximum of 7 at 19th level) and gains the ability to shape one veil chosen from the daevic veil list. At 3rd level and every 3 levels thereafter the yaksa gains the ability to bind veils directly to his chakra, unlocking potent new abilities. He gains these binds in the following order: Feet, Hands, Wrists, Shoulders, Belt, and Neck.

This ability modifies the standard eidolon.
