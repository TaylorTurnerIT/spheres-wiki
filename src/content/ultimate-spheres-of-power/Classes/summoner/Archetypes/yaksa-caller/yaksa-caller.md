Yaksa callers learn to bind lesser daeva, called yaksa, to their will... Or perhaps the other way around. Whether serving as high priest and god to primitive tribes or dominating the battlefield with a group of trusted companions, the caller and his yaksa leave an indelible mark upon the pages of history.
