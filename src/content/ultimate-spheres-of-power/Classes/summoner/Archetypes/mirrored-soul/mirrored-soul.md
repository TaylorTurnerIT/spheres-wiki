Sometimes in their eternal journey, souls become fragmented in their passage from one realm of existence to the next. Even more rarely, these fragmented shards find each other at a later leg in their journey. Such beings sometimes arise as mirrored souls, two beings who were once one, now too different to completely rejoin into a single entity. Only a lifetime spent realigning their bodies and minds can make whole what was rent asunder, and so these mirrored souls strive tirelessly to master themselves, so that one day their tattered fragments may rejoin into a unified whole. Such a joining must surely lead to a being of unrivaled strength and skill, fueled by a level of self-knowledge few beings could ever comprehend.
