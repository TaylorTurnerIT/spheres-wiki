id: conjoined-combatant
name: "Conjoined Combatant"
level: 4
replaces: ["shield ally and greater shield ally"]
tags: []
---

At 4th level, whenever the mirrored soul is within his soul eidolon's threatened area, he receives a +2 insight bonus to his attack rolls and a +1 dodge bonus to his Armor Class. At 12th level, the insight bonus to attack rolls increases to +4 and the dodge bonus to Armor class increases to +2.

This replaces shield ally and greater shield ally.
