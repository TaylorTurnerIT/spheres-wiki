Kinetic powers manifest themselves not only from unusual sources, but in many unique ways. In the case of some kineticists, their personal histories factor into the form their abilities take; this is the case with soundweavers. Almost invariably, a soundweaver descends from a long lineage of minstrels and bards, and often began life training to become one before fate had other plans for them.
