id: dragoon-leap
name: "Dragoon Leap"
level: 2
tags: ["leap"]
---

At 2nd level, a kinetic lancer gains the air's leap utility wild talent, regardless of their element. They also ignore their base land speed for determining if their jumping distance exceeds their base land speed while using the Kinetic Leap feat, allowing them to leap as far as their Acrobatics check would allow. If the kinetic lancer already possesses air's leap, they can select another utility wild talent for which they qualify.

This ability replaces the 2nd level utility wild talent.
