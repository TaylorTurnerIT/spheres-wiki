---
id: mindscape-master
name: "Mindscape Master"
level: 20
tags: []
---

At 20th level, a true psychic can maintain 2 permanent mindscapes and reduces the burn cost of all infusions used inside of a mindscape by 1, as well as receiving a +2 to attack rolls, damage rolls, and save DCs for their mind wild talents while inside of a mindscape.

This ability replaces omnikinesis.
