Some assume that the power of a kineticist is theirs and theirs alone, but there are those who share this power with a magical creature, the two forming a symbiotic relationship. In such pairings, a familiar is a vital part of a kineticist's personal growth, as the two advance in power as a single entity. The familiar's powers help their master understand the darker arts of their powers, granting insight into how to harness the power of hexes. The kineticist provides the innate power that fuels their familiar's growth, the two perfectly synchronized with each other in all situations.
