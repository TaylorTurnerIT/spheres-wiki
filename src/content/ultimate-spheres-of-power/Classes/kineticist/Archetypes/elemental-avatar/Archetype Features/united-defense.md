---
id: united-defense
name: "United Defense"
level: 9
tags: []
---

At 9th level for every 2 points of burn an elemental avatar accepts to increase the effect of one of their elemental defenses, they may select a second elemental defense, treating it as though they had accepted 1 point of burn for the purposes of its effects.

This ability replaces metakinesis (maximize).
