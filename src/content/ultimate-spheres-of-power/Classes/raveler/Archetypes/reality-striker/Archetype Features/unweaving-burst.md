The reality striker fires a burst of unweaving energy as a standard action that provokes attacks of opportunity, making a ranged touch attack against a single creature or unattended object within Medium range (100 feet + 10 feet per level). The struck target takes damage equal to 1d6 + the higher of reality striker's casting ability modifier or practitioner modifier. This damage increases by a further 1d6 at 5th level, and every 3 levels thereafter. Only the first d6 of damage is multiplied on a critical hit, and this damage ignores the struck targets damage reduction and resistances. This damage is not halved when dealing damage to objects, but is still affected by an object's hardness.

At 8th level, and again at 15th level, when the reality striker uses this ability they may fire an additional burst of energy. The second burst fired in this way suffers a -5 penalty to its attack roll, and the third ray fired suffers a -10 penalty to its attack roll.
