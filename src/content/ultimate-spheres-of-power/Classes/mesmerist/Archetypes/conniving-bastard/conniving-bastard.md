His mind is his first weapon. He looks deeply into others, tricking them, influencing them, and outright controlling them. Then the knives come out.

This archetype requires sphere mesmerist or champion mesmerist. The mass charmer class feature may be replaced as if it were mental potency, just as rule minds can be replaced as the class feature of the same name.
