The device can be primed with a sphere ability, and then activated anytime within the next hour, either with a timer or by a mental command from the combat engineer (a free action). The device may also be used as a dead man's switch, automatically activating the ability inside when the engineer loses consciousness. The device can be carried by the engineer, or by someone else, or be placed somewhere, and can be used as part of an attack action.
