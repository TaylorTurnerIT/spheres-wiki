The essentialist can use natural ingredients to make poisons. As a standard action, he may expend an essence to create an injury poison. This poison requires a Fortitude save equal to 10 + 1/2 the essentialist's class level + his casting ability modifier, and it has a frequency of once per round for 5 rounds. On a failed save, the afflicted takes 1 point of Constitution damage, +1 additional point every 4 levels after the first to a maximum of 5 points at 17th level. The poison loses potency 24 hours after creation, but can be renewed for another 24 hours by spending another essence as a standard action. In addition, when he crafts a poison, he may expend one essence to lower the raw material cost of a poison by 25 gp, +5 gp for every class level he possesses, to a minimum of 0 gp. Poisons created this way lose their effectiveness in 24 hours.
