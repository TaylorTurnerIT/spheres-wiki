At 3rd level, the adaptive gunner adds one of the following skills to his class skill list: Knowledge (arcana), Knowledge (dungeoneering), Knowledge (nature), Knowledge (planes), or Knowledge (religion). At 6th level and every three adaptive gunner levels thereafter, he gains a +1 bonus to this skill.
