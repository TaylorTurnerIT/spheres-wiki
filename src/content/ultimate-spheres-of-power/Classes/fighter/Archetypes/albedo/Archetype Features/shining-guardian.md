level: 1

tags: []
---

At 1st level, the albedo gains the ability to shape the Perihelion Pauldrons veil with a veilweaver level equal to her class level, increases her total veils shaped by 1, and gains a pool of essence equal to 1/3 her class level (minimum 1). Shaping this veil requires 1 hour of quiet contemplation. At 8th level, and again at 14th level, the maximum essence capacity of of her Perihelion Pauldrons increases by 1. At 8th level, she gains the ability to bind veils to her Shoulders slot.

This replaces the bonus feats gained at 1st and 8th level.
