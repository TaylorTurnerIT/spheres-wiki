At 4th level, a foe reaper forms a close bond with his hunting companions. This functions as a standard ranger's hunter's bond class feature, except the foe reaper must choose to form a bond with his allies. In addition to the usual benefits of his hunter's bond, the foe reaper and his allies gain the benefits of the Ability Share† feat for the bond's duration.

This ability alters hunter's bond.
