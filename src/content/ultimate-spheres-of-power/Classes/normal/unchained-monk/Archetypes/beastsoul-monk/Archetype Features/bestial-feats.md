At 1st level, the beastsoul monk gains the Transformation [http://spheresofpower.wikidot.com/feats feat] as a bonus feat in place of the bonus feat granted at first level. At second level, the beastsoul monk adds Hybrid Transformation and Improved Transformation to her list of bonus feats. The beastsoul monk may take the Transformation feat more than once, selecting a different form each time.

This modifies monk bonus feats.
