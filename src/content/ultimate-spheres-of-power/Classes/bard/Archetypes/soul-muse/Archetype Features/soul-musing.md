---
id: soul-musing
name: "Soul Musing"
level: 2
tags: []
---

At 2nd level, a soul muse can choose one type of Perform skill. He can make checks with his selected Perform skill in place of binder level checks when attempting to seal a pact with a spirit that belongs to the constellation he selected with the tunneled musing class feature.

This ability replaces the bard's first versatile performance.
