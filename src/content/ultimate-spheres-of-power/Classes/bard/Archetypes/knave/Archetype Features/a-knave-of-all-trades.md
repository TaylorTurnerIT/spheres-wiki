The knave knows more than he lets on, and can prepare a few tricks even seasoned wizards don't know. At 2nd level, whenever the knave rests and regains spell points, he may choose a sphere he possesses. He may use his class level as his caster level with that sphere. This stacks with other caster level sources normally.

This replaces well-versed.
