name: "Blindfolded Fighting"
level: 1
replaces: ["the mount class feature"]
tags: []
---

At first level the blind swordsman gains Blind-Fight as a bonus feat. In addition, he can spend one use of his resolve as an immediate action to gain the benefits of the Blindfolded Oracle Divination (sense) talent with an effective caster level equal to his class level.

This replaces the mount class feature.
