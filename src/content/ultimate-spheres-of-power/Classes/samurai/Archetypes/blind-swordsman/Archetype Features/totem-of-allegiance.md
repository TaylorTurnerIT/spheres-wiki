id: totem-of-allegiance
name: "Totem of Allegiance"
level: 14
replaces: ["the greater banner class feature"]
tags: []
---

At 14th level, the blind swordsman can spend one use of his resolve as a move action to present an emblem (such as a banner or insignia) granting the benefits of the War sphere's Totem of Allegiance talent with an effective caster level equal to his class level to those who can see it.

This replaces the greater banner class feature.
