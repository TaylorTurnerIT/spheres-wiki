id: improved-blindfolded-fighting
name: "Improved Blindfolded Fighting"
level: 9
replaces: ["the greater resolve class feature"]
tags: []
---

At 9th level, the blind swordsman gains Improved Blind-Fight as a bonus feat, even if he does not meet the feat's prerequisites. In addition, he can spend one use of his resolve as a standard action to gain the benefits of the Ghost Sight Divination (sense) talent with an effective caster level equal to his class level.

This replaces the greater resolve class feature.
