---
id: sealed-augmentation
name: "Sealed Augmentation"
level: 5
tags: []
---

Beginning at 5th level, when a seal-etched pactmaker makes a good pact with a spirit that she possesses a binding tattoo of, she can select one of her pact augmentations and increase the bonus that it provides by +1. At 11th level, she can either increase the bonus that one pact augmentation provides by +2 or increase the bonus that two pact augmentations provide by +1. At 17th level, she can either increase the bonus that one pact augmentation provides by +3, increase the bonus that three pact augmentations pro- vide by +1, or increase the bonus that one pact augmentation provides by +2 and another by +1.

This ability replaces the pactmaker's 5th-level binder secret.
