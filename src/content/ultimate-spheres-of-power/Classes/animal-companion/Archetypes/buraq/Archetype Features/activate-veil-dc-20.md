---
id: activate-veil-dc-20
name: "Activate Veil (DC 20)"
level: 1
tags: []
---

The animal uses a supernatural, spell-like ability, or extraordinary ability associated with one of its veils (such as the breath weapon granted by Gorget of the Wyrm or the area blast of Ditchdigger's Armlets). You may choose which veil the animal activates as part of the command.
