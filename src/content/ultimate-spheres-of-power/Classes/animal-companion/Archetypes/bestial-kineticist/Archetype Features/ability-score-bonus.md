level: 1
tags: []
---

Choose Strength or Dexterity; once this choice is made it cannot be changed. The bestial kineticist receives a +1 bonus to the ability score chosen and to their Constitution score per 3 druid levels their master possesses (minimum 0).

This ability replaces Str/Dex bonus.
