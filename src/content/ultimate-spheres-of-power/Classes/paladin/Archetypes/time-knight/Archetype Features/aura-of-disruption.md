---
id: aura-of-disruption
name: "Aura of Disruption"
level: 14
replaces: ["aura of faith"]
tags: []
---

At 14th level, a time knight's attacks deliver a jolt of disruptive chronal energy that makes it difficult for creatures to cast spells or use spell-like abilities. Any creature struck by the time knight who attempts to cast a spell or use a spell-like ability before the start of the time knight's next turn must succeed at a concentration check (DC 15 + 1/2 the time knight's class level) or have the spell fail, losing any spell points, spell slots, and the action taken to cast the spell. Any attack action made by an ally within 10 ft. of the time knight also benefits from this effect.

This replaces aura of faith.
