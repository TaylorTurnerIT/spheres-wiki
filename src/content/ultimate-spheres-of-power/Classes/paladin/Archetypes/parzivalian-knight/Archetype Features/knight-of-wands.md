At 3rd level the Parzivalian knight gains a special consecration. This has the same effect as all the auras a paladin of her level would normally have, but as a consecration it is not always active, normally has a radius of 20 ft., etc. She must concentrate to maintain effect, but may spend a spell point to allow the consecration to continue for 10 minutes per paladin level. The Parzivalian knight may use this consecration in conjunction with talents such as Sanctify Object, in which case she gains the special bonus (such as immunity to fear) only while in the area of the consecration.

This ability replaces aura of courage, aura of resolve, aura of justice, aura of faith, and aura of righteousness.
