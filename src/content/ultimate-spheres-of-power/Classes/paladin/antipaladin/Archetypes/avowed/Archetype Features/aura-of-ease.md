---
id: aura-of-ease
name: "Aura of Ease"
level: 3
tags: []
---

At 3rd level, the avowed emits a calming, cooperative aura with nature around her, allowing all allies within 10 feet of her to move through any non-magical difficult terrain at their normal speed. This ability functions only while the avowed is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

At 8th level, this aura also allows allies to move through magical difficult terrain at their normal speed.
