level: 1
tags: ["bleed"]
---

As a full-round action, the worldsoul incarnate may spend 14 vitality points to summon forth ghostly spirits that echo her physical form, each of which makes a single attack before disappearing. The worldsoul incarnate may make a single melee attack against every enemy within 20 ft she can see. These attacks are at her full BAB.
