id: death-company-member
name: "Death Company Member"
level: 3
tags: []
---

At 3rd level, a suicide soldier gains Societal Obedience as a bonus feat, ignoring its prerequisites. She must choose the Company of the Dead as her chosen organization. At 6th level and every 3 levels thereafter, she gains a +1 bonus on activity checks to remain a member of the Company of the Dead, as well as on skill checks made to influence members of the Company of the Dead who are at least indifferent towards her. This ability replaces trap sense.
