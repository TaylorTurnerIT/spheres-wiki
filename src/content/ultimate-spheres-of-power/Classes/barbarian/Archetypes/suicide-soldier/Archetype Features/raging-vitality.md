name: "Raging Vitality"
level: 2
tags: []
---

At 2nd level, a suicide soldier gains Diehard and Raging Vitality as bonus feats, ignoring their prerequisites. In addition, she counts the Raging Vitality feat as the Endurance feat for the purpose of meeting the prerequisites of feats that list Diehard as a prerequisite. This ability replaces uncanny dodge and the rage power gained at 2nd level.
