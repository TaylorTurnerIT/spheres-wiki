The vigilante gains a +10 ft. bonus to his land movement speed.

  - Additionally, at 4th level, the vigilante gains a 10% chance to ignore critical hits and precision damage. This increases by 10% every 4 levels thereafter, to a maximum of 50% at 20th level. This stacks with similar benefits, such as that provided by the fortification armor special quality.
