---
id: expanded-infusion
name: "Expanded Infusion"
level: 1
tags: []
---

Select one substance infusion the surge fist monk possesses. By spending 1 point from their ki pool plus the infusion's burn cost as a swift action, they can apply its effects to all unarmed strikes and surge fists they use until the beginning of their next turn. A surge fist monk can select this ki power multiple times, choosing a different substance infusion each time.
