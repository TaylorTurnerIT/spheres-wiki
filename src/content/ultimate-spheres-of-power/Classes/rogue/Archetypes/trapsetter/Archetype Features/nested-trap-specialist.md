name: "Nested Trap Specialist"
level: 4
tags: []
---

At 4th level, a trapsetter is skilled at creating traps hidden inside of other traps. If a trapsetter creates a trap in the same location as another trap, one of the two traps of the trapsetter's choice is more difficult to disarm and notice—the DCs increase by 4 for the chosen trap's Disable Device check and Perception check.

This ability replaces uncanny dodge.
