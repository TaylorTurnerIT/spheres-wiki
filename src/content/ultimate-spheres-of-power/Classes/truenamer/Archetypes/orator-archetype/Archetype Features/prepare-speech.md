id: prepare-speech
name: "Prepare Speech"
level: 7
tags: []
---

Starting at 7th level, an orator has learned how to choose his words for maximum effect. If he takes an hour to prepare a speech on a particular topic, he adds his Intelligence modifier to his Bluff or Diplomacy skill check made when delivering the speech.

This ability replaces the inflection gained at 18th level.
