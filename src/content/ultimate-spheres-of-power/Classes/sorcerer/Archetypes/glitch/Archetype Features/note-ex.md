This ability is fundamentally bizarre and, though it would normally be listed as a supernatural or spell-like ability it is listed as an extraordinary (Ex) ability due to the fact that it circumvents the normal rules of "magic" and would thusly function in an antimagic field and not be impeded by things like spell resistance.
