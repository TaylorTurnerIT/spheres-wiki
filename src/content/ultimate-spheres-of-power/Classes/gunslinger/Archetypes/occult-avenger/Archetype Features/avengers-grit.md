id: avengers-grit
name: "Avenger's Grit"
level: 1
tags: []
---

Each day, an occult avenger gains a number of grit points equal to her Wisdom modifier. Unlike other gunslingers, an occult avenger gains grit only from scoring a critical hit or killing blow against a creature that qualifies as the favored enemy of one of her bound spirits.

This ability alters the grit class feature.
