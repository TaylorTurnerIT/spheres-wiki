id: spiritual-conversion
name: "Spiritual Conversion"
level: 7
tags: []
---

At 7th level, a pactsworn magus can use his bound spirit to spontaneously cast any spell that he has already prepared. By expending his bound spirit's major granted ability for 5 rounds as a swift action, the pactsworn magus can "lose" any prepared magus spell that is not a cantrip in order to cast any other magus spell of the same spell level or lower that he has already prepared. For example, a pactsworn magus who prepared shocking grasp and mage armor can expend his bound spirit's major granted ability as a swift action in order to "lose" mage armor in order to convert the spell into shocking grasp.

This ability replaces knowledge pool.
