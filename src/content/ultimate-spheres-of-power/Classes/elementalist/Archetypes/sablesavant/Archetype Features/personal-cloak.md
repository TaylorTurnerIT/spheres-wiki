---
id: personal-cloak
name: "Personal Cloak"
level: 1
tags: []
---

The sablesavant gains the ability to maintain a single *cloak* as a constant sphere effect; this *cloak* may only be granted (meld) options the sablesavant possesses. The sablesavant must pay any additional spell point costs for the *cloak* or its options as appropriate. The sablesavant may use their *cloak* sphere ability to select different options for this *cloak*, replacing the previously chosen options (instead of creating a new *cloak* effect).
