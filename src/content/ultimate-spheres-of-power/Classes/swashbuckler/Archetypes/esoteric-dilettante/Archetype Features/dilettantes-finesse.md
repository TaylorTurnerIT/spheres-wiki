---
id: dilettantes-finesse
name: "Dilettante's Finesse"
level: 1
tags: []
---

At 1st level, an esoteric dilettante gains the benefits of Weapon Finesse feat with light or one-handed piercing melee weapons and with all melee touch attacks that she makes. Additionally, the esoteric dilettante treats all light or one-handed melee weapons that she gains from the granted abilities of a spirit that she is currently bound to as a one-handed piercing melee weapon for all swashbuckler class abilities that require such a weapon.

This ability replaces swashbuckler's finesse.
