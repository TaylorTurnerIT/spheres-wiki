tags: []

---

When you use your Serendipity consecration, in addition to bestowing a luck bonus upon your allies, all enemies within the area of effect also suffer a penalty to their own attack rolls, skill checks, ability checks, and saving throws equal to the bonus you grant to your allies. When used this way, serendipity is considered a curse effect on your enemies.
