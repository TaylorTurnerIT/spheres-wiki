**Prerequisites:** Fate sphere (Bound Consecration), caster level 5th.

When you use a consecration that remains in place using Bound Consecration, you may spend three spell points to allow the effect to continue for 2 hours per caster level without concentration. You may take this talent a second time at 10th caster level, which allows you to spend three spell points to make the effect permanent.
