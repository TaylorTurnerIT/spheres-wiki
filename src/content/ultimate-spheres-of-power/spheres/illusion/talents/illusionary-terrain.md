---
id: illusionary-terrain
name: "Illusionary Terrain"
system: power
type: talent
sphere: illusion
tier: basic
tags: []

---

As a trick, you may designate a single cube (no larger than your maximum illusion size) as difficult terrain for one round per caster level. A Will save to disbelieve is allowed by all who enter the cube. On a successful save they treat the area as normal. This difficult terrain applies to all travel through the area regardless of type of movement.

You may treat the area of your figments as difficult terrain regardless of the type of movement for those who fail to disbelieve.
