type: talent
sphere: fallen-fey

tags: []
---

**Prerequisites:** 8th caster or higher.

As a standard action, you may spend a spell point to make a melee touch attack against a creature and hurl it into Faerie. This provokes an attack of opportunity. A successful Will save negates this ability. Once within Faerie, if the target creature wishes to return to where it previously was, it must travel 1d12 miles to reach a fairy circle and find a way through, arriving within 1d6 miles of its former location. This is a teleportation effect.
