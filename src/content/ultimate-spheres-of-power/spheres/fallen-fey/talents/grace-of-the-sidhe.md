You may activate the following abilities as fey-blessings:

**Evasion:** You gain the evasion ability. When you succeed at a Reflex saving throw against an attack that normally deals half damage on a successful save, it instead takes no damage. This ability does not function when wearing medium or heavier armor.

**Supernatural Speed:** Whenever you move at least 20 feet, you gain 20% miss chance against ranged attacks until the start of your next turn. If you spend an additional spell point, you instead gain this benefit against all attacks.
