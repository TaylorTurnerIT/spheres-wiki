**Prerequisites:** Fallen Fey sphere, caster level 10th.

Any humanoid within 30 feet of you that looks at you must succeed on a Fortitude save or be blinded for 1 minute per caster level. A creature that succeeds on this save is immune to your use of this ability for 24 hours. You may suppress or resume this ability as a free action. Activating this fey-blessing costs an additional spell point.
