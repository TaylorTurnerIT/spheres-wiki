You may activate the following abilities as fey-blessings:

**Animal Blessing:** All friendly creatures of the animal type within 30 feet of you gain a +1 luck bonus to all saving throws. This bonus increases by +1 for every 5 caster levels you possess.

**Plant Blessing:** All friendly creatures with the plant type within 30 feet of you gain a +1 luck bonus to all saving throws. This bonus increases by +1 for every 5 caster levels you possess.

**Vermin Blessing:** All friendly creatures with the vermin type within 30 feet of you gain a +1 luck bonus to all saving throws. This bonus increases by +1 for every 5 caster levels you possess.
