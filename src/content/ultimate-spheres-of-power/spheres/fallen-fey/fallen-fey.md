### Note: Flavor and Faerie
The Fallen Fey sphere is unusual in Spheres of Power because it has a much stronger flavor/theme when compared to other, largely-generic spheres. However, if the fey focus of this sphere does not appeal to you, you can plausibly change it to represent almost any other group by renaming talents and changing the 'image' of each one. -SoP Wiki Admin

---

In its original publication in Player's Guide to Skybourne, the Fallen Fey sphere was limited to elves (and, with GM permission, other fey-related races). While a GM is well within their rights to limit this sphere in a similar fashion depending upon the role of fey within their setting, no such limitation is included here.

### Fallen Fey Talent Types

Some talents are marked (fey-blessing). These talents grant additional fey-blessed effects.
