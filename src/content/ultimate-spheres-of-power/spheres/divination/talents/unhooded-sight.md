---
id: unhooded-sight
name: "Unhooded Sight"
system: power
type: talent
sphere: divination
tier: basic
tags: ["sense"]

---

You may spend a spell point to gain a sense that grants you a bonus equal to 1/2 your caster level (minimum 1) to all Will saves, Perception checks, or magic skill checks made to disbelieve an illusion.
