tags: ["combat"]

---

**Prerequisites:** Destruction sphere (Energy Sphere (blast shape)).

**Benefit:** Whenever you successfully deal damage to a target with your energy sphere, increase its duration by 1 round (whether or not you have spent a spell point; it not, then it can persist after you end concentration). You can only extend the sphere's duration by up to a number of rounds equal to your Destruction caster level.
