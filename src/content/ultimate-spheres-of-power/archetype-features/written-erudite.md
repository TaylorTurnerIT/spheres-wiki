Starting at 5th level, a truescribe's mastery of the written word makes her resistant to abilities that rely upon it. She gets a +1 bonus to saving throws made against spells and abilities with a writing motif, such as sepia snake sigil, symbol of pain (and all like symbol spells), and explosive runes. This bonus to saving throws increases to +2 at 11th level and +3 at 17th level.

This ability replaces linguistic familiarity.
