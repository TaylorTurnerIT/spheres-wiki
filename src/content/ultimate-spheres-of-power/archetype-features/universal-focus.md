An elemental avatar treats air, earth, fire, and water as their primary element, selecting a simple blast from each one as well as gaining their basic manipulations. An elemental avatar is unable to gain a composite blast except from the elemental fusion class feature.

This ability alters elemental focus and replaces the infusion gained at 1st level.
