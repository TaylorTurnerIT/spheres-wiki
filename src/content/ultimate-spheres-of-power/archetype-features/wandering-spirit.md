At 4th level, when the soul with many faces forms a bond with a wandering spirit, she may choose a second sphere to be a spirit sphere. This must be a sphere associated either with her wandering spirit or with her normal spirit. She gains this sphere as a bonus magical talent (or a talent from it if she possesses it already), and uses her class level as her caster level with that sphere. This stacks with other caster level sources normally.

The soul with many faces does not gain spells from her wandering spirit.

This modifies wandering spirit.
