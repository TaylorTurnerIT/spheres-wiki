The ability to close on a deal before the other party has time to figure out that they are being cheated is the bread and butter of a snake oil salesman. She adds half her level to all Appraise checks, as well as any Bluff checks made to convince someone to buy her products or to trust her (minimum +1).

This ability replaces trapfinding.
