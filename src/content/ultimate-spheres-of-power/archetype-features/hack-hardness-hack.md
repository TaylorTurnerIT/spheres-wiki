As a swift action that provokes an attack of opportunity a glitch may change the hardness of an unattended, non-magical object less than 10x10x10 ft. within 30 feet. This may be done to pieces of a contiguous structural material (walls, dirt, doors, etc). The hardness of the material is divided by 2 or multiplied by 2 to a minimum of 1 or a maximum of 20 (as per adamantine). This has no effect on the HP per inch of the material.
*Recoil:* x3 the initial hardness of the material
