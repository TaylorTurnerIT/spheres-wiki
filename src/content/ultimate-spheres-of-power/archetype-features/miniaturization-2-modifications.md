The device takes the form of a large piece of ammunition, such as an arrow or crossbow bolt. This works similar to Fuse, and the sphere effect appears wherever the ammunition ends after an attack is made with it or it becomes attached to a creature hit (if the sphere ability can be attached to creatures).
