An evoker minstrel uses their Intelligence modifier in place of their Charisma to determine what spells they can learn and cast, as well as spells per day, DCs, and any effects of spells they cast from the bard spell list. They also receive 4 skill ranks plus their Intelligence modifier when gaining a level.

This ability alters the bard's skill ranks per level and key ability score.
