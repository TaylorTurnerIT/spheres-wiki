At 15th level a fusion kineticist can select wild talents from their sub element as though it was the same level as their main element. They also gain 1 infusion or utility wild talent for which they qualify. In addition, they can also accept 1 burn to use two utility wild talents with the same standard action as long as those utility wild talents are from two different elements and 2 levels lower than the highest utility wild talent the fusion kineticist can access.

This ability replaces the 15th-level expanded element.
