At any level in which the kenza would gain a bonus feat (1st, 2nd, 6th, 10th and so on), she may instead gain the ability to learn spells from one of the following cleric domains: air, earth, ire, plant, or water. This only gives him access to the spells, he must still learn the spells normally, and does not gain access to the domain or subdomain's associated powers.
