At 1st level, the apex predator gains the Scout sphere as a bonus sphere, or a talent from that sphere if she possesses it already. When using her scout ability, she may substitute a Survival check for the appropriate Knowledge check at a -5 penalty. At 8th level, she no longer takes a -5 penalty.

This replaces tracking and swift tracking.
