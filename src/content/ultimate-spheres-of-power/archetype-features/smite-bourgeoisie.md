A comrade's smite evil targets creatures who would count as invested or as a patriot with regard to the comrade's detect nobility ability rather than against evil creatures. Creatures who fall into this category but are a member of a socialist government are not valid targets. If the target of smite bourgeoisie is noted to be greedy (such as dragons, dwarves, etc) or is of an evil alignment, the bonus to damage on the first successful attack increases to 2 points of damage per level the comrade possesses. Regardless of the target, smite bourgeoisie attacks automatically bypass any DR the creature might possess.

This ability modifies smite evil.
