At 11th level, a comrade may expend a use of his smite bourgeoisie ability as a swift action to grant all allies within 30 feet of him a morale bonus on attack and damage rolls equal to 1/3rd his comrade level against any creature who would be a valid target for his smite bourgeoisie class feature for a number of rounds equal to his Charisma modifier. Creatures with levels in the commoner class gain this bonus while within 100 feet of an allied comrade, regardless of whether or not he expends a use of his smite bourgeoisie ability.

This ability replaces aura of justice.
