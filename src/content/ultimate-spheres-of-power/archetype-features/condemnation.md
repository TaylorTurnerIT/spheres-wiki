At 3rd level, the grand purifier gains the ability to channel divine power into her attacks. As an attack action, he may spend a spell point or expend her martial focus to make an attack that has an additional affect on her target. The target may make a saving throw determined by the ability to negate the effect. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + 1/2 the grand purifier's level + the grand purifier's Charisma modifier.
