At 1st level, a pactsworn hunter gains a loyal animal that serves as her spirit companion. A hunter may choose any Animal, Magical Beast, or Vermin that can serve as a druid's animal companion or vermin companion or as a wizard's familiar to be her spirit companion. The spirit companion gains all of the benefits of being the pactsworn hunter's animal companion, but it calculates its base Intelligence score as if it were a familiar, using the pactsworn hunter's level as her effective wizard level to determine the spirit companion's base Intelligence score.

This ability alters animal companion.
