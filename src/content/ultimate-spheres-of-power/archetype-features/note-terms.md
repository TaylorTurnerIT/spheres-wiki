In this class entry we refer to two things: the master familiar and the wizard-thrall. The master familiar is the creature who is being played directly and fills the role of a party member. The wizard-thrall is a humanoid creature who follows all the normal rules for character creation but is actually the master familiar's pet/slave/thrall (as described in the familiar class feature). A "familiar" refers to the class feature by that name and the "master familiar" is the name of this class.
