The reality striker infuses their weapon with energy that unweaves that which it strikes. As a standard action, the reality striker makes a single attack with a weapon they are wielding, dealing an additional 1d6 points of damage on a successful hit. This damage increases by a further 1d6 at 5th level, and every 3 levels thereafter. This damage is not multiplied on a critical hit, and ignores the struck target's damage reduction and resistances. This additional damage is not halved when dealing damage to objects, but is still affected by an object's hardness. 

At 8th level, and again at 15th level, when the reality striker uses this ability they may make an additional attack using their infused weapon. The second attack made in this way suffers a -5 penalty to its attack roll, and the third attack made in this way  suffers a -10 penalty to its attack roll.

This replaces the raveler's bonus talents gained at levels 2, 8, 14, and 20. A martial raveler and casting raveler can also select this archetype, replacing their bonus combat talents or bonus magic talents gained at levels 2, 8, 14, and 20 instead.
