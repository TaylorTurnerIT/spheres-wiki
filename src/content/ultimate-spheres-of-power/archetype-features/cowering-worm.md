While taking a total defense action the bonus to the coward's AC is improved by an additional +2 (this stacks with the benefits of having 3 ranks in Acrobatics). In addition, a coward who fights defensively takes only a -2 penalty on attack rolls but retains the +2 to his AC until the start of his next turn.
