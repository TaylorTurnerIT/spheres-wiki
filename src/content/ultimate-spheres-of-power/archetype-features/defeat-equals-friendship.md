name: "Defeat Equals Friendship"
type: archetype-feature
system: power
archetypeId: pacifist
level: 20
tags: []
---

At 20th level, when the pacifist confirms a critical hit he may elect not to deal damage and instead make a Diplomacy check with a bonus equal to his weapon's critical multiplier (DC 30 + creature's Charisma modifier). If successful then the creature's attitude shifts to indifferent, or to friendly if the pacifist beats the DC by 5 or more.

This ability replaces weapon mastery.
