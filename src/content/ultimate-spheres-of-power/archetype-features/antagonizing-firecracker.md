type: archetype-feature
system: power
archetypeId: harpy
level: 8
tags: []
---

With a word a creature affected by this insult is compelled to attack with furious rage. They gain the confused condition and benefit from a rage spell for a number of rounds equal to the harpy's Charisma modifier. At 8th level, the duration increases by 3 rounds.
