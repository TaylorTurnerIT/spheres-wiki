Time thieves who reach 20th level have stolen so much time that it can no longer touch them. The time thief no longer suffers penalties to her physical ability scores due to age and cannot die due to old age, though she still accrues mental bonuses for aging as normal. She cannot be affected by any magical effect which alters her age unless she chooses to be, and any beneficial time effect cast on the time thief, such as a haste spell or alter time effect, automatically has its duration doubled; this extended duration applies only to the time thief and does not affect the duration of the spell for any other targets. In addition, the vast amounts of stolen time that imbue the time thief's body can help her heal injuries much faster than normal, granting her fast healing 5.

This replaces master strike.
