At 20th level, the war hero gains his third greater heroic aura, and may retrain any previous totems. When the war hero makes a successful attack against on an enemy with at least half as many Hit Dice as himself, he may activate an aura as an immediate action.

This replaces the bonus feat gained at 20th level.
