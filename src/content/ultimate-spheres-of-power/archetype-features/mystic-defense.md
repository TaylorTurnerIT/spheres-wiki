At 2nd level, an evoker minstrel gains the defense wild talent of the same element of the kinetic blast taken with improved cantikinesis. An evoker minstrel can spend 2 minutes and 2 rounds of bardic performance to treat their defense wild talent as though they had accepted 1 point of burn for the purposes of its effects until they next refresh their bardic performance rounds. After doing this, they can expend an additional 2 rounds as a standard action to increase the effective burn for the effects of their defense wild talent. An evoker bard is treated as having spent burn on a wild talent of that element whenever they use their kinetic blast while using their bardic performance.

This ability replaces well-versed.
