name: "Liberating Smite"
type: archetype-feature
system: power
archetypeId: avowed
level: 6
tags: []
---

At 6th level, whenever the avowed would smite a creature, that creature immediately releases any grapple they may have on a creature. Additionally, the smote creature suffers a penalty to the save DC to any ability that would restrict movement they possess (this includes paralysis, slow effects, entanglement, etc.) equal to her casting ability modifier.
