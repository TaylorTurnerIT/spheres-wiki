At 6th level and every 6 levels thereafter, the celestial knight chooses one constellation other than the one he selected for his champion. He gains the ability to manifest the weapon, armor, or equipment option of that constellation without paying its essence cost. The cavalier must choose a different constellation each time he gains this ability.

This replaces the bonus feats class feature.
