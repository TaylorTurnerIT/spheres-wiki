type: archetype-feature
system: power
archetypeId: eldritch-jailer
level: 1
tags: []
---

When an eldritch jailer seals a pact with a spirit, she doesn't gain any granted abilities that her bound spirit would normally exchange for its vestigial bond ability. In addition, an eldritch jailer can never gain a vestigial bond ability from a spirit that she seals a pact with.
