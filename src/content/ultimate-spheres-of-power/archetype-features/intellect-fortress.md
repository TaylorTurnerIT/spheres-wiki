As a standard action, the ascendant mind may spend a point from her phrenic pool to create a telekinetic bubble surrounding herself and everyone within 10 feet. The bubble is made up of overlapping fields moving in chaotic patterns, blocking line of effect for those who do not understand its mysteries. Whenever an enemy attacks anyone within the bubble, the enemy must succeed at a Perception check with a DC equal to 10 + 1/2 her ascendant mind level + her phrenic modifier or else the attack is negated as the fortress blocks it. Area of effect abilities can not penetrate the fortress, and the fortress blocks incorporeal movement, but not gaze attacks or light-based attacks. The fortress lasts for a number of rounds equal to her class level.
