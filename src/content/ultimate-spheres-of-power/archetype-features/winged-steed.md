At 3rd level the buraq adds the Gorget of the Wyrm veil to his list of veils known and increases his total veils shaped by 1. The buraq is always treated as having a Shoulder chakra to shape this veil in, even if their animal type would normally not have shoulders.

At 9th level, the buraq learns to bind veils to its Shoulder chakra, unlocking their most potent abilities. This replaces the evasion and Multiattack class features; at 15th level when the buraq would normally gain improved evasion, it instead gains evasion.
