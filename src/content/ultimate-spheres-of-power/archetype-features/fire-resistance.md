At 2nd level the pyrotechnician gains fire resistance 5. At 5th level this improves to 10 and to 20 at 8th level. At 14th level all fire damage he takes is nonlethal fire damage.

This replaces all instances of the alchemist's poison resistance class feature as well as his persistent mutagen.
