Thanks to a steady diet of unique and unpalatable foods the folken gains a uniquely strong constitution. He gains a +4 bonus to his Constitution score for the purposes of determining the effectiveness of folken class features (including rounds of rage) and the DC of folken abilities. In addition, he is immune to the stench universal monster rule.
