A surge fist monk can make ki strikes with their surge fist. A surge fist monk must be 8th level to select this ki power.

A surge fist monk cannot select the following ki powers: elemental burst, elemental fury, ki range, qinggong power. When they use the ki blocker ki power, it also increases the burn cost of any wild talent used by the affected creature by 1 for its duration.

This ability replaces the ki power gained at 4th level and alters ki power.
