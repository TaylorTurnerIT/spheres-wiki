At 1st level, the starchild's ever expanding oneness with the world and her love for all her people manifests with a small representation of the love the world feels for her. This creature, known as a dreamchild, is a being of pure joy, freedom, and love. It appears as a child-sized, humanoid creature whose features are obscured by the ever changing mass of swirling psychedelic colors and patterns. This functions like a druid's animal companion (part of the nature bond class feature) though she may only select the following animal companion.

This ability replaces nature bond.
