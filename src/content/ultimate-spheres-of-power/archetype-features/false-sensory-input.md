As a swift action, the ascendant mind implants a thought into a target's mind, making its reality a little more convenient for the ascendant mind. For 1 minute, she receives a circumstance bonus equal to 1/2 her class level on all Bluff, Diplomacy, Disguise, or Stealth checks against that target. This is an enchantment (charm) ability.
