At 2nd level and every 2 levels thereafter, an occult priest selects one domain spell from any of her deity's domains and adds it to the list of domain spells that she can spontaneously cast. The occult priest must be able to prepare and cast a spell in order to select it with this ability. Once a spell has been chosen, it cannot be changed. At 20th level, an occult priest can spontaneously cast any spell from any of her deity's domain spells.
