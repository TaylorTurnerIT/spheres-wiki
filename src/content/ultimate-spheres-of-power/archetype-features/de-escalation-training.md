The pacifist gains Diplomacy in place of Intimate as a class skill. In addition, he no longer takes the usual -4 penalty on rolls to deal nonlethal damage and may deal nonlethal damage with any weapon he can wield. Finally, he gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls made when dealing nonlethal damage. At 4th level and every 4 levels thereafter this bonus increases by +1.

This replaces the pacifist's 1st level bonus feat.
