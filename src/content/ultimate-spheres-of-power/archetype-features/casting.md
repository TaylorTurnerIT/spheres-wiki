At first level a yamabushi may combine spheres and talents to create magical effects. A yamabushi is considered a Low-Caster and uses Wisdom as her casting ability modifier. (Note: All casters gain 2 bonus talents (which may also be used to select spheres) and may select a casting tradition the first time they gain the casting class feature.) This replaces the stunning fist class feature.
