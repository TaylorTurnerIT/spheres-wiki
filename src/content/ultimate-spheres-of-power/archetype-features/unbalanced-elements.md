At 1st level, a fusion kineticist selects two elements for their elemental focus, gaining a simple blast for both. A fusion kineticist must select 1 main element, the other being considered their sub element. A fusion kineticist can only select 1st level talents for their sub element, and does not gain a composite blast even if their choice of simple blasts would qualify them for one.

This alters elemental focus and replaces the 1st-level infusion and basic utility talent normally gained at this level.
