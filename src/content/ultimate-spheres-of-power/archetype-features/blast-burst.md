At 9th level, as a free action an elemental brethren can spend 3 rounds of their elemental assault to increase the damage die of their kinetic blast by 1 step (d4 to d6, d6 to d8, d8 to d10, d10 to d12) for 1 round.

This replaces the infusion gained at this level.
