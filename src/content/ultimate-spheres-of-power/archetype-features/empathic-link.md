The master familiar has an empathic link with his wizard-thrall to a 1 mile distance. The master familiar can communicate empathically with the wizard-thrall, but cannot see through its eyes. Because of the link's limited nature, only general emotions can be shared. The master familiar has the same connection to an item or place that his wizard-thrall does.
