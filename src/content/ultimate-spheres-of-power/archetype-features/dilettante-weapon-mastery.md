At 20th level, when an esoteric threatens a critical hit with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon or a melee touch attack, that critical is automatically confirmed. Furthermore, the critical modifiers of such weapons increase by 1 (x2 becomes x3, and so on).

This ability alters swashbuckler weapon mastery.
