At 7th level, the mysterial gains the ability to empower his veils and other essence receptacles with his ki energy, giving them temporary boosts of power. As a swift action, the mysterial may spend a ki point to treat one available essence receptacle as though it were invested with an additional point of essence for 3 rounds. This ability functions even if the mysterial has already invested the maximum essence capacity of the targeted receptacle, allowing him to temporarily exceed the normal essence capacity limit for his level. Multiple uses of this ability on a single receptacle overlap, but do not stack.

This ability alters the ki pool class feature.
