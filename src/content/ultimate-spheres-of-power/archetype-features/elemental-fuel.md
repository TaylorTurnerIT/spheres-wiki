---

At 3rd level, as a free action an elemental brethren can spend 1 round of their elemental assault to reduce the burn cost of an infusion of up to 3rd level by 1. At 8th level, they can spend 2 rounds of their elemental assault to reduce the burn cost of an infusion of up to 6th level by 1. At 12th level, they can spend 3 rounds of their elemental assault to reduce the burn cost of an infusion of up to 9th level by 1.
