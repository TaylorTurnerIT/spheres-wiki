name: "Collateral Damage"
type: archetype-feature
system: power
archetypeId: energy-roper
level: 1
tags: []
---

When the energy roper successfully uses Kinetic Pin to damage a grappled creature, they also deal their blast's minimum damage to each creature in a direct line between them and the grappled target, allowing a Reflex save to negate it.
