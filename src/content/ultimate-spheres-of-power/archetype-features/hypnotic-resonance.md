At 1st level, the resonant song gains the ability to shape the Hands of the Bard veil, increases her total veils shaped by 1, and gains a pool of essence equal to ¼ her class level (minimum 1). At 3rd level, the resonant song gains the ability to bind veils to her Hands chakra. At 8th and 15th level, she increases the maximum essence capacity of her Hands of the Bard veil by 1. This replaces the fascinate, suggestion, and mass suggestion performances.
