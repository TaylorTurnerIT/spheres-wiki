An occult shaman's spirit animal is inscribed with the seals of every such Knowledge Task that the shaman has completed. A shaman must commune with her spirit animal in order to seal a pact with a spirit. If her spirit animal is slain, the shaman's pact immediately ends as if she had willingly expelled the spirit using Expel Spirit.

This ability alters spirit animal.
