The mirrored soul gains a bonus (form) talent drawn from the Conjuration sphere at 3rd level and every odd level thereafter. This (form) talent must be applied to his soul eidolon.

This replaces summon monster I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VII, VIII, IX, and gate.
