At 1st level, an occult scourer gains the [http://spheresofpower.wikidot.com/pm-inquisitor-inquisitions exorcism inquisition†]. He uses his warpriest level as his effective inquisitor level when determining the effects of this inquisition.

This ability replaces blessings.
