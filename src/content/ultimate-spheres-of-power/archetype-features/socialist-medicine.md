At 3rd level, each ally within 10 feet of the comrade gains a +4 morale bonus on saving throws against all diseases, including supernatural and magical diseases (including mummy rot). This ability functions only while the paladin is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.

This replaces divine health.
