A nascent deity gains their prerogative bonus when submerged at least partially (up to their waist) in water or in an area where water surrounds them (a small island, on a boat, etc). A nascent deity with the water dominion is compelled to swim at every available opportunity and feels "dry" unless properly moisturized.
