The sadhu gains the ability to breathe under water as long as he has at least a single point of balanced ki invested. The sadhu gains base swimming speed of 10 feet per point of balanced ki invested. He does not need to make a Swim check to swim normally. The sadhu gains a +2 insight bonus to Swim checks per point of balanced ki invested. He can always choose to take 10 on these checks, even when threatened or rushed. Provided he can swim in a straight line, the sadhu may use the run action.
