At 4th level, the skald may produce a song that instantly summons stormclouds. While singing, the skald may use his control weather ability to affect both Wind and Precipitation, and the severity level he may affect increases by 1. As soon as he stops singing, the control weather effect ends. The skald may not use this ability in conjunction with any talent that would modify the area of his control weather ability. This replaces song of strength.
