At 7th level, a true psychic gains either telekinetic blast or mind blast (whichever they did not select at 1st level). They can also infuse a telekinetic blast with mental energy, allowing the true psychic to use mind substance infusions with their telekinetic blast (mind substance infusions are still mind-affecting effects).

This ability replaces the expanded element gained at 7th level.
