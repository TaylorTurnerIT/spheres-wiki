The aura grows weeds, vines, underbrush, and other basic plants (except trees) suitable for the Nature sphere plant package, as if the area was affected by the Nature sphere Create Nature talent to create a field of plants. This plantlife only exists as long as the aura persists, becoming lifeless when outside the aura's area.
