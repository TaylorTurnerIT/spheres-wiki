Rancers guilds are loose affiliations so a rancer's "order" class feature more specifically refers to their disposition.

The vast majority of rancers align with the Order of the Shield (protecting the common folk) and less commonly with the Order of the Blue Rose. Evil rancers often favor the Order of the Cockatrice. Occasionally they will be members of the Order of the Blue Rose or Order of the Dragon

They are never members of honor-based or fealty-based orders like the Order of the Lion or Order of the Sword.
