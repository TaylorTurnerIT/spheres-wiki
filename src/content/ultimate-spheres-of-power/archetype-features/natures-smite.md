id: natures-smite
name: "Nature's Smite"
type: archetype-feature
system: power
archetypeId: avowed
level: 6
tags: []
---

At 6th level, whenever the avowed would smite a creature, the target must immediately succeed at a Reflex saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 her avowed level + her casting ability modifier) or immediately become entangled from nearby sources of nature (such as stone or grass) for 1d4 rounds, or until they attempt to break free as a move action by succeeding an Escape Artist or Strength check against the DC.
