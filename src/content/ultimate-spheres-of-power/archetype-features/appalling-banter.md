name: "Appalling Banter"
type: archetype-feature
system: power
archetypeId: harpy
level: 1
tags: []
---

A creature affected by this is compelled to talk back to the harpy at great length - consuming the creature's move or standard action on their next turn. If the harpy is at least 6th level, the creature's hair also falls out.
