At 2nd level, whenever a forgemaster inscribes his runes for the day, he may inscribe copies of some of his equipment runes to other nonmagical items. For each power level he can inscribe (Least, Lesser, etc.), the forgemaster may select a single variant he is currently inscribing. If he does, he inscribes that rune on two items rather than just one. Designate one of these two items as the "rental". A rented rune acts just like a normal rune, but cannot be activated. Rented runes persist until the next time the forgemaster inscribes runes.

This ability replaces practiced scribe and the compound rune slot granted at 10th level.
