At 2nd level, the verminspeaker has gained the service of a particularly strong vermin. The creature must be a mindless vermin that is available to druids as an animal companion. This creature functions as a druid's animal companion, using the verminspeaker's level as her effective druid level, with the exception that the verminspeaker's animal companion does not get the share spells ability.
