archetypeId: ordained-hunter

tags: []

Each time the ordained hunter rolls a natural 1 on a saving throw, she regains 1 kismet point. If an ability or effect would allow the ordained hunter to roll twice or reroll, she regains kismet only if the roll she takes is a natural 1. Rolling a natural 1 on a saving throw against an effect caused by a creature, trap, or effect with a CR less than half her character level or who is not genuinely hostile to her does not restore kismet.
