name: "Curse of the Medium"
type: archetype-feature
system: power
archetypeId: occult-medium
level: 1
tags: []
---

An occult medium always suffers the personality influence of her bound spirit and she gains no benefit from Unyielding Personality or similar effects that suppress a spirit's influences. Additionally, an occult medium cannot suppress her bound spirit's physical sign and takes the penalties of one oracle curse of her choice, but gains none of the benefits.

This ability alters the oracle's curse.
