At 6th level and higher, the negotiator can no longer be flanked. This defense denies a rogue the ability to sneak attack the inquisitor by flanking her, unless the attacker has at least four more rogue levels than the target has negotiator levels. If a character already has uncanny dodge (see above) from another class, the levels from the classes that grant uncanny dodge stack to determine the minimum rogue level required to flank the character.

This ability replaces the inquisitor's second teamwork feat.
