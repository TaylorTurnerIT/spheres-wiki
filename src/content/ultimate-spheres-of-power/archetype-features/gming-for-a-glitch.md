Allowing a player to play as a glitch invests in them a degree of narrative power not normally seen in Pathfinder. A GM should work with their glitch to not upset the normal narrative flow or intention of the game. A glitch's power should never get in the way of the enjoyment of the players or the GM. For example: If the GM has set up a unique encounter he worked hard on and the glitch wants to switch the monster on him, the GM could request that he not but perhaps offer him an alternative.
