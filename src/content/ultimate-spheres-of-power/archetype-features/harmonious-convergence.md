At 20th level, the soundweaver has mastered their ability to command the elements with their music. During a kinetic concert, they can use gather power as a swift action to reduce the cost of a kinetic blast by 1 (to a minimum of 0). This cannot be done on the same turn they use gather power as a move or full-round action.

This ability replaces omnikinesis.

In addition, they gain the following bardic masterpiece:

++++ The Consequence of the Harmonious Convergence
