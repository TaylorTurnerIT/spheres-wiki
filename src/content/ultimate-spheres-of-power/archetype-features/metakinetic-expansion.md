A metakinetic savant can select Expanded Metakinesis starting at 1st level, ignoring its prerequisites. Starting from 5th level, the metakinetic savant can select any metamagic feat for Expanded Metakinesis that increases a spell's effective level by 1 or less in addition to the metamagic feats normally available for Expanded Metakinesis when gaining this feat. They cannot select a metamagic feat through it that they've selected with their metakinetic feats class ability or vice versa, nor can they select any metamagic feat which cannot be selected through their metakinetic feats ability.
