The withering witch may corrupt a sphere ability or hex that causes damage and has no duration beyond instantaneous, and can increase the pain and suffering caused. Those who are damaged by the ability or hex take an additional amount of non-lethal damage equal to twice the withering witch's class level. This happens once, when the corrupted ability or hex takes initial effect. This extra damage can not exceed the damage done by the ability itself.
