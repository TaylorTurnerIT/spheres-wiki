At 10th level, whenever a divine conduit uses a spell-like utility wild talent that affects themselves with a duration longer than 1 round (such as wings of air), they can choose to have it also affect a number of allies equal to their constitution modifier within 30 feet of themselves for 1 round per kineticist level they possess.

This replaces the 10th-level utility wild talent.
