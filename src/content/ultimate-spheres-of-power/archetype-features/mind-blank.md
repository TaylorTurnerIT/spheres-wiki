As a swift action, the ascendant mind may empty her mind of all thought. While in this state, she is still able to function, but can not apply great mental effort, such as by using sphere abilities or attempting skill checks that involve mental abilities. While she is like this, she is effectively mindless, and is invisible to senses that detect intelligent creatures. Scrying also fails to detect her. This state lasts for up to 1 hour per class level, or until she exerts herself mentally. Her psychic nexus still operates normally.
