A verminkeeper can handle her animal companion as a free action, or push it as a move action, even if she doesn't have any ranks in the Handle Animal skill. The verminspeaker gains a +4 circumstance bonus on all wild empathy checks and Handle Animal checks made regarding an animal companion.
