Upon reaching 20th level, whenever the clockstopper is under the effects of an active haste (the spell or the alter time effect), she gains a +4 circumstance bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, and saving throws in addition to the ability's normal effects. The clockstopper also gains these benefits on attacks made against enemies under the effects of slow (the spell or the alter time effect), and to saving throws against spells and abilities created by such enemies.

This replaces true grit.
