Rather than placing an enhancement from the enhancement sphere onto a target, a snake oil salesman can create a magical substance such as a pill, a pinch of powder, or a small vial of liquid. The creature who ingests this substance gains the effects of the enhancement, as if she had the Special Delivery drawback with the same duration and limitations.

A snake oil salesman may apply these magical substances to a weapon as a standard action, as if it were a poison. When used to strike a target, the target must save against the enhancement or suffer its effects. This can affect targets normally immune to poisons, but still benefits from talents, abilities, and feats that affect poisons, such as swift poison or deadly cocktail.
