At 1st level the shadow boxer gains Basic Magical Training as a bonus feat, but must select the Dark sphere. At 2nd level she gains Advanced Magical Training as a bonus feat. She may take Extra Magical Talent as a monk bonus feat, but may only select talents from the Dark sphere when she does so.

This replaces the bonus feats gained at first and second level.
