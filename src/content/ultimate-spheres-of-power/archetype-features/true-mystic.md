At 20th level, the mystic becomes a master of spells and combat. Whenever he uses his advanced spellstrike ability, he does not need to make a concentration check to cast the spell defensively. Whenever the mystic uses advanced spellstrike ability, he can choose to either increase the DC to resist the spell by +2, grant himself a +2 circumstance bonus on any checks made to overcome spell resistance, or grant himself a +2 circumstance bonus on all attack rolls made against the target during his turn, including the attack roll made as part of his advanced spellstrike.

This replaces true magus.
