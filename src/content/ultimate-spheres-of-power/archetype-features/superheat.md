An orthella possessed by a fire spirit superheats the metal of her motorcycle frame and burns anyone who touches her other than her rider. This functions as a supernatural version of the fire shield spell, but it only causes 1 point of fire damage per point of the motorcycle's Strength modifier. This ability is constant, though may be suppressed as a swift action with as little as a verbal command from the rancer.
