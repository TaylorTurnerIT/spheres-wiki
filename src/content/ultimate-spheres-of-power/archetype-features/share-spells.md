name: "SHARE SPELLS"
type: archetype-feature
system: power
archetypeId: master-familiar
level: 1
tags: []
---

The master familiar may cast a spell with a target of "You" on his wizard-thrall (as a touch spell) instead of on himself. A master familiar may cast spells on his wizard-thrall even if the spells do not normally affect creatures of the wizard-thrall's type.
