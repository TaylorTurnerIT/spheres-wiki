At 3rd level, the time knight is immune to all magical aging effects. The time knight and all allies within 10 ft. of her gain a +4 morale bonus on saving throws against time effects; this includes effects created with the Time sphere or any of its talents, time magic spells such as slow, or the shifting breath of a time dragon.

This replaces aura of courage.
