At 7th level, the prophet can use her prophetic binding ability to change which of her deity's cleric domains she has selected with the God Binder feat as a full-round action. A prophet can use this ability once per day 7th level, plus one additional time per day every 6 levels thereafter.

This ability replaces spirit mastery.
