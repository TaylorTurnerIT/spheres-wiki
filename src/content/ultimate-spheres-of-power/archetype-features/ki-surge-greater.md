A surge fist monk spends 1 less point from their ki pool to activate and maintain their improved ki surge. This reduction does not stack with any other effects which reduce the cost of a ki power, nor does it affect the ability's duration. If the activation cost is 0 ki points, the ability is maintained passively, even when the surge fist monk is unconscious, and allows the alterations to one's appearance to be activated or deactivated as a free action. A surge fist monk must have improved ki surge to select this ki power, and can select it up to 3 times: once at 12th level and once more for every 4 surge fist monk levels thereafter.
