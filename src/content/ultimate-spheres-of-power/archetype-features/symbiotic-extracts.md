With a touch the mad experimentalist can impart a single extract he is currently affected by to his symbiotic ooze. An extract imparted in this fashion uses the remaining duration that the alchemist is affected instead of its normal duration. A symbiotic ooze can only benefit from one extract in this fashion at a time. At 9th level the symbiotic ooze can benefit from up to 3 extracts in this fashion.
