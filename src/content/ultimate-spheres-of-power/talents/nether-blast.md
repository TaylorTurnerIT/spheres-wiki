id: nether-blast
name: "Nether Blast"
system: power
type: talent
sphere: destruction
tier: basic
tags: []
---

You may change the damage type of your destructive blast to negative energy. Any creature struck by the attack or that failed their Reflex saving throw if one was required must succeed at a Will saving throw or be shaken for 1 round. A nether blast does not damage undead; instead, the undead must succeed at a Will save or become frightened for 1 round. This bypasses the immunities of the undead type.
