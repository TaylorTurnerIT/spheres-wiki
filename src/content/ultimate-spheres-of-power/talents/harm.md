system: power
type: talent

Whenever the target of this word suffers damage, they suffer an additional amount of untyped damage equal to 1/2 your caster level (minimum 1). This effect lasts as long as you concentrate, but you may always spend a spell point as a free action to allow this word to continue for 1 round per caster level without concentration.
