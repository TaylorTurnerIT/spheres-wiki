name: "Adamant Coils"
level: 7
tags: []

---

At 7th level the snake charmer can spend 1 point from his arcane pool as a swift action to set his light whip dancing and whirling in a protective cyclone. For the next 3 rounds while this ability is active, the snake charmer gains dodge, deflection, and natural armor bonuses to his armor class that are each equal to ½ the amount of essence invested in his light whip.

This ability replaces the medium armor ability.
