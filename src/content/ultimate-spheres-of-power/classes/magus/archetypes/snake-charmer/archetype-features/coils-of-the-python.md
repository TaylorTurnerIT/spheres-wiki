name: "Coils of the Python"
level: 13
replaces: ["the heavy armor ability"]
tags: []

---

At 13th level, the snake charmer gains a +1 insight bonus per point of essence invested in his light whip on all grapple checks made while using his lightwhip, and is no longer considered grappled himself when grappling an opponent with his lightwhip.

This replaces the heavy armor ability.
