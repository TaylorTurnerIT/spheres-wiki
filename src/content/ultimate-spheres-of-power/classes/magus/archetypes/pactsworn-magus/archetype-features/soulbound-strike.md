---
id: soulbound-strike
name: "Soulbound Strike"
level: 2
tags: []

---

At 2nd level, whenever a pactsworn magus casts a spell with a range of "touch" from the magus spell list or uses a granted ability of a spirit that he is bound to with a range of "touch," he can deliver the granted ability through any weapon he is wielding as part of a melee attack, otherwise functioning as spellstrike.

This ability alters spellstrike.
