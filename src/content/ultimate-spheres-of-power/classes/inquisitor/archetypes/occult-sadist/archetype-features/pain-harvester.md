---
id: pain-harvester
name: "Pain Harvester"
level: 17
tags: []

---

At 17th level, whenever a creature fails a saving throw against channel pain or one of the occult sadist's cruelties, it suffers 1 point of ability damage to its Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution score (the occult sadist's choice) in addition to that ability's other effects. The occult sadist cannot deal more than 1 point of ability damage to a creature per turn.

This ability replaces slayer.
