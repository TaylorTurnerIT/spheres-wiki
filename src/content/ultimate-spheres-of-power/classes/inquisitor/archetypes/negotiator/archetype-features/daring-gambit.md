---
id: daring-gambit
name: "Daring Gambit"
level: 15
tags: []

---

At 15th level, as a free action the negotiator may present a false opening to a foe while preparing a devastating counter attack. She may declare herself as flat-footed against one creature she can see. If that creature attacks her, then she may make an attack of opportunity against that creature. This attack resolves first and, if hits, then the negotiator is not considered flat footed against the attack that triggered this ability. The target receives a Sense Motive check (DC 10 + negotiators Bluff bonus) to realize this deception.

This ability replaces the inquisitor's fifth teamwork feat.
