id: pact-augmentation
name: "Pact Augmentation"
level: 4
tags: []
---

At 4th level and every 3 levels thereafter, a pact protector gains the pactmaker's pact augmentation class feature. She uses her inquisitor level as her effective pactmaker level to determine the effects of pact augmentation.

This ability replaces the later iterations of the judgment ability.
