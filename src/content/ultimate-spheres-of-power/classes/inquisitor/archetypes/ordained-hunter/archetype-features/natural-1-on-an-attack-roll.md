id: natural-1-on-an-attack-roll
name: "Natural 1 on an attack roll."
level: 1
tags: []

---

Each time the ordained hunter rolls a natural 1 on an attack roll against a creature she regains one kismet point. If an ability or effect would allow the ordained hunter to roll twice or reroll, she regains kismet only if the roll she takes is a natural 1. Rolling a natural 1 on an attack against a creature with a CR less than half her character level or who is not genuinely hostile to her does not restore kismet.
