tags: []
---

At 2nd level, the nightblazer may choose one skill that uses Dexterity, Wisdom or Charisma, and use Intelligence with that skill instead. They may choose additional skills at 5th, 8th, and 11th level.

This replaces poison lore, poison resistance, and poison immunity.
