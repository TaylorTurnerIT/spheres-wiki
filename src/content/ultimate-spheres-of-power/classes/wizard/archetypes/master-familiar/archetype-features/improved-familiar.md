name: "Improved Familiar"
level: 7
tags: []
---

At 7th level, the master familiar gains Improved Familiar as a bonus feat. He must match the alignment requirement of his new form. He adjusts his statistics according to his new form. This ability replaces the master familiar's ability to speak with animals of its kind at 7th level.
