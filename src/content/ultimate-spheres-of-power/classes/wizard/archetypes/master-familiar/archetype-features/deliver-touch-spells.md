---
id: deliver-touch-spells
name: "DELIVER TOUCH SPELLS"
level: 1
tags: []
---

If the master familiar is 3rd level or higher, his wizard-thrall can deliver touch spells for him. If the master familiar and his wizard-thrall are in contact at the time the familiar casts a touch spell, he can designate his wizard-thrall as the "toucher." The wizard-thrall can then deliver the touch spell just as the master familiar would. As usual, if the master familiar casts another spell before the touch is delivered, the touch spell dissipates.
