---
id: holy-healing
name: "Holy Healing"
level: 2
tags: []

---

At 2nd level, a divine conduit gains the kinetic healing utility wild talent, regardless of their element. A kineticist must accept burn with this utility wild talent; they cannot allow another creature to accept it. They may use this utility wild talent on themselves as a swift action. A divine conduit always treats this healing as though they were using a physical blast, regardless of what simple blast they possess.

This ability replaces the 2nd-level utility wild talent.
