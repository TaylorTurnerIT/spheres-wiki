While some kineticists begin with power over a single element, there are others who embody a storm of multiple elements from the onset of their power. These kineticists wield abilities that can at times seem counter-intuitive to each other, as each element fights for dominance inside of their host. The struggle between nurturing both powers can prove impossible for some, but there are a few who are able to harness both elements with equal skill and prove dominant over both, becoming a master of both in a way that no other kineticist can do.
