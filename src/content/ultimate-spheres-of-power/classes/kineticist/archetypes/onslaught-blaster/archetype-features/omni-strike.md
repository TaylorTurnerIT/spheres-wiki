---
id: omni-strike
name: "Omni Strike"
level: 17
tags: []

---

At 17th level, the onslaught blaster can increase the burn cost of their onslaught blast by 4 to double the number of blasts they create. Each blast the onslaught blaster creates deals +1 additional damage. This ability cannot be used with excessive blaster or extreme blaster.

This ability replaces metakinesis (twice).
