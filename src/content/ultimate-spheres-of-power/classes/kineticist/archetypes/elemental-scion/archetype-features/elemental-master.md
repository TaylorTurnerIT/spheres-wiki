---
id: elemental-master
name: "Elemental Master"
level: 15
tags: []

---

At 15th level, an elemental scion increases the DCs of their infusions and wild talents by an additional +1, as well as increasing the damage of all simple and composite blast by 1 step. In addition, they also gain an additional utility wild talent or infusion.

This ability replaces the expanded element class feature gained at 15th level.
