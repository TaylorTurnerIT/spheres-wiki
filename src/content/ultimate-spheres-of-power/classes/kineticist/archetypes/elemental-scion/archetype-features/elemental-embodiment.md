---
id: elemental-embodiment
name: "Elemental Embodiment"
level: 20
tags: []

---

At 20th level, an elemental scion has reached their peak of power. An elemental scion treats all infusions and wild talents as though they had accepted 1 point of burn or increased the burn cost by 1 for the purpose of their effects. They also gain 1 additional infusion and utility wild talent.

This ability replaces the omnikinesis class feature.
