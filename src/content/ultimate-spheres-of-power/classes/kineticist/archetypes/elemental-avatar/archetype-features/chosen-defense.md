At 2nd level, an elemental avatar can select any elemental defense wild talent from your primary elements. They can select their other elemental defenses with the expanded defense utility wild talent. At 8th level, an elemental avatar gains an additional elemental defense wild talent.

This ability alters the elemental defense class feature.
