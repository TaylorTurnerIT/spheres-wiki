This sculpture gains DR 5/adamantine and the [earth] subtype.
> 
> Your sculpture must be produced entirely from one substance. You can only have one Sculpture Blast at a time, attempting to produce another will cause the first to collapse and fade. This blast deals damage as a simple blast and is only associated with universal infusions.
