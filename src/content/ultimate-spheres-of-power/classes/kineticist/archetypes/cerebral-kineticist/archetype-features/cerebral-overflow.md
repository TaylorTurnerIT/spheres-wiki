level: 1
tags: []

---

A cerebral kineticist can choose to use the power of their element to open new neural pathways, enhancing their already genius level intellect. A cerebral kineticist can choose to give themselves a morale bonus to any mental ability score in place of a size bonus to a physical ability score.

This ability alters the elemental overflow class feature.
