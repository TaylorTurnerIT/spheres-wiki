id: two-handed-specialty
name: "Two-Handed Specialty"
level: 1
tags: []
---

A telekinetic bladeshifter can select two-handed melee weapons for the telekinetic blade ability, and they also gain proficiency with all such weapons except double weapons. They increase the damage they deal with two-handed weapons created from telekinetic blasts by one step. The telekinetic bladeshifter must possess Power Attack to select this specialty.
