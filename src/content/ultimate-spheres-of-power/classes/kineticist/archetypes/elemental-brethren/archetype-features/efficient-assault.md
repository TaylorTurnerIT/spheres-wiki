---
id: efficient-assault
name: "Efficient Assault"
level: 15
tags: []
---

At 15th level, an elemental brethren reduces the amount of rounds of elemental assault they need to spend to use elemental fuel, augmented internal buffer, and blast burst by 1. This cannot reduce the amount of rounds needed to 0. They also gain an infusion or utility wild talent for which they qualify.

This ability replaces the expanded element gained at this level.
