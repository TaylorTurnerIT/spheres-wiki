At 20th level, an elemental brethren can spend 4 rounds of elemental assault to use any kinetic blast wild talent they don't know for 1 round or change any of their wild talents into any other wild talent of the same category for 24 hours. An elemental brethren cannot use or change a wild talent into an element other than an air, earth, fire, water, or universal.
