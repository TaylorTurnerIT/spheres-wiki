name: "Dragoon Frenzy"
level: 8
tags: []

---

At 8th level, a kinetic lancer can make a full-attack with the kinetic blade infusion or any other infusion for which it is a prerequisite while using the dragoon dive class feature. If the kinetic lancer applies the vital blade form infusion to their kinetic blast, all adjacent squares to the target take damage equal to the minimum damage of the kinetic lancer's kinetic blast (Reflex save for half). A kinetic lancer can choose a number of spaces equal to their Constitution modifier; these spaces do not take damage from this ability.

This ability replaces the 8th level utility wild talent.
