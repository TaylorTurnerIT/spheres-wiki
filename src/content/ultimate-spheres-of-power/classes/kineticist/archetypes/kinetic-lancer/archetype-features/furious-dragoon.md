name: "Furious Dragoon"
level: 13
tags: []

---

At 13th level, by accepting 2 points of burn when they use their dragoon dive class feature, they can make an additional attack with their kinetic blade or an infusion for which it is a prerequisite.

If the kinetic lancer kinetic blast has the vital blade form infusion applied to it, they instead double the area of additional damage done by dragoon frenzy.

This ability replaces the 13th level infusion.
