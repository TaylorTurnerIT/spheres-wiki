name: "Imbued Familiar"
level: 3
tags: []
---

At 3rd level a hex kineticist's familiar gains an element, as per the kineticist's elemental focus class ability, but does not gain a simple blast or basic utility wild talent. The hex kineticist can accept 1 burn to imbue their familiar with a simple blast associated with that element, which it can use to make an attack with its kinetic blast that deals half of the damage dice of their master's simple kinetic blast . Accepting burn in this fashion reduces the damage dice of the hex kineticist's kinetic blasts by half rounded up (if a kineticist with a 3d6 kinetic blast used this ability, their familiar would gain a blast that dealt 1d6 damage, while the kineticist would deal 2d6 with their own kinetic blast).

This ability replaces the 3rd-level infusion and the deliver touch spells ability the familiar would normally gain at 3rd level.
