id: greater-elemental-bond-sp-or-su
name: "Greater Elemental Bond (Sp or Su)"
level: 15
tags: []

---

At 15th level, a hex kineticist's familiar gains an additional infusion up to 2nd level and infusion specialization 2.

A hex kineticist may now select major hexes with their kinetic curse ability, gaining a major hex instantly. In addition, they can increase the burn cost of the hex synthesis by 2 to apply a major hex to it.

This ability replaces the 15th-level expanded element.
