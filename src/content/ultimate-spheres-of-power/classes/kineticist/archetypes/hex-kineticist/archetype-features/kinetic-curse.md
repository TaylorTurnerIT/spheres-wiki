id: kinetic-curse
name: "Kinetic Curse"
level: 2
tags: []
---

At 2nd level a hex kineticist gains a hex as the witch class feature. At 6th level and every 4 levels after, a kineticist can select a hex in place of a utility wild talent, treating their kineticist level as their witch level. A kineticist treats their Constitution score as their Intelligence score for the purpose of DCs and effects for hexes.

This ability replaces the 2nd-level wild talent and alters utility wild talents.
