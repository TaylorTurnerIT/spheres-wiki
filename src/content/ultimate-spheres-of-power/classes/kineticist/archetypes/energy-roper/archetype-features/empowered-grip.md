name: "Empowered Grip"
level: 1
tags: []
---

When using their kinetic tendril infusion, the energy roper can choose to increase its burn cost by up to 1 point per 4 energy roper levels they possess. For each point of burn added to the blast's burn cost in this way, they receive a +1 bonus to their CMB to perform combat maneuvers with their kinetic tendril.
