---
id: deadly-tendrils
name: "Deadly Tendrils"
level: 1
tags: []
---

Increase the damage die size of the energy roper's kinetic blast by 1 step. If this would increase its damage die from a d10, it increases to a d12 instead of 2d6s; it cannot be increased higher than a d12 this way. This roper talent can be taken up to three times; its effects stack.
