---
id: advanced-kinetic-pin
name: "Advanced Kinetic Pin"
level: 1
tags: []
---

By accepting 1 additional point of burn as part of a Kinetic Pin used as a standard action, the energy roper can instead attempt to deal ¼ their blast's damage to each creature they are currently grappling. A single combat maneuver roll is made at a -10 penalty and applied against each grappled creature's CMD individually, and the grapple is successfully maintained against all creatures this combat maneuver succeeds against. The energy roper must be 13th level to select this roper talent.
