---
id: natural-grappler
name: "Natural Grappler"
level: 1
tags: []

---

Choose one natural attack the energy roper possesses. They can use the appendage normally used to deliver it to instead use their kinetic blast, even if it is not prehensile (for example, an energy roper with a bite attack could choose it to allow themselves to use a kinetic blast with their mouth). They cannot use this natural attack while maintaining a grapple made with a kinetic blast from that appendage.
