---

At 1st level, the buraq gains the ability to share any one veil it is capable of shaping with its master. The master gains the benefits of any essence the buraq has invested in the veil, but loses the veil and its benefits if he is more than 30 feet from the buraq.

The master regains the benefits of this veil as soon as he returns to within 30 feet of the buraq. This replaces either the share spells or light armor proficiency class feature, depending on which the companion starts with.
