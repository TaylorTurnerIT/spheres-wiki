In addition to the standard animal companion feats, a buraq may select Access Higher Chakra Slot, Access Low Chakra Slot, Access Middle Chakra Slot, Akashic Charge, Enhanced Capacity, Extra Essence, and Shape Veil, treating his master's effective druid level as his character level when determining prerequisites.
