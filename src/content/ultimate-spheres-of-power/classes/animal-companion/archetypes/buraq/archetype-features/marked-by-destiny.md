---

The buraq must choose the Shape Veil feat as his 1st level feat, though he does not need to meet the prerequisites when he gains it in this manner. The buraq must have the appropriate limbs and equipment slot to learn a veil (for example, a snake buraq cannot normally learn or shape a veil that attaches to the Feet, Hands, Shoulder, or Wrist chakra). The buraq always knows how to use any veil it can shape, including allocating essence, and will instinctively shape its known veils after an 8 hour rest.
