---
id: bounding-blast
name: "Bounding Blast"
level: 9
tags: []
---

At 9th level a bestial kineticist can imbue its master with its simple kinetic blast as a standard  action, allowing them to use it once for no burn cost and treating the blast's damage as from a kineticist 2 levels higher than the bestial kineticist's hit dice; this blast must be used by the end of the bestial kineticist's master's next turn or else it is lost, and can include infusions the bestial kineticist possesses. At 16th level the bestial kineticist can perform this action with a composite blast as well. It must be within 60 ft. of its master to do this and still accepts the burn to use this blast as if it'd used it itself.

This ability replaces multiattack.
