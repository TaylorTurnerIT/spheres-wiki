---
id: shared-magic
name: "Shared Magic"
level: 2
replaces: ["precise companion"]
tags: []

---

At 2nd level, as long as their animal companion is within line of sight, the greenrunner may treat them as being in range of any of sphere abilities that target one creature. In addition, whenever the greenrunner uses such an ability on himself, he may spend a spell point as a free action to have it affect his animal companion as well.

This replaces precise companion.
