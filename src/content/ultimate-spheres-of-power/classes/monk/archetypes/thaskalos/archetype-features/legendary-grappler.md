---
id: legendary-grappler
name: "Legendary Grappler"
level: 17
tags: []

---

At 17th level, the thaskalos no longer suffers any circumstance penalties to grapple checks for grappling with three or less opponents simultaneously. In addition, the thaskalos gains an insight bonus to his grapple combat maneuver checks equal to his Wisdom bonus (if any).

This ability replaces tongue of the sun and moon.
