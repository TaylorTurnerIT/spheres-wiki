name: "Great Grappler"
level: 13
tags: []

---

At 13th level, the thaskalos no longer suffers a circumstance penalty to grapple combat maneuver checks for grappling with two opponents simultaneously, but he still suffers a -1 circumstance penalty to grapple combat maneuver checks to grapple three opponents. He can select another another bonus feat from the list of available bonus feats (see above).

This ability replaces diamond soul.
