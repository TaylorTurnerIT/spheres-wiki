name: "Flurry of Blows"
level: 1
tags: []

---

Starting at 1st level, a kensai can make a flurry of blows using any combination of unarmed strikes or attacks with his living ki weapon (see below). He may not make a flurry of blows with any other weapons, including special monk weapons. A kensai's flurry of blows otherwise functions as normal for a monk of his level.
