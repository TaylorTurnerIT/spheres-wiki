---
id: blindsense
name: "Blindsense"
level: 10
tags: ["auxiliary"]
---

At 10th level, the huashan's senses become incredibly acute, giving it blindsense out to a range of 30 feet. This ability allows the huashan to pinpoint the location of creatures that he cannot see without having to make a Perception check, but such creatures still have total concealment from the huashan. Visibility still affects the huashan's movement and he is still denied its Dexterity bonus to Armor Class against attacks from creatures it cannot see.

This ability replaces slow fall 20 feet through slow fall 50 feet.
