At 3rd level, the sadhu starts to awaken the seven major chakras (see below) that control the low of ki throughout his body, gaining the powers of an awakened major chakra (see below). In addition, the sadhu can align the ki between yin, yang, and balanced. The sadhu gains an additional major chakra at 7th level and at every four levels thereafter. 

These abilities replace still mind, wholeness of body, diamond body, quivering palm, and empty body.
