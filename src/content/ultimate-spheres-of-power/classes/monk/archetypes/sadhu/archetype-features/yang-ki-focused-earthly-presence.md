By tapping into this chakra, the sadhu learns to be confident in his body and in his presence amongst others. By investing yang-aligned ki into this chakra, the sadhu gains a +2 insight bonus on Bluff, Diplomacy, Handle Animal, and Perform checks for each point invested.
