name: "Yang Ki Expended – Indomitable Soul"
level: 1
tags: []
---

Through sheer force of will the sadhu can endure the effects of tremendous damage. When he expends his ki focus, the sadhu can choose to take an immediate free action to delay the effect of a single attack, spell, or ability used against him. The damage or effect does not happen until the end of the sadhu's next round's action. The duration of this ability extends by 1 round for each additional point ki expended.
