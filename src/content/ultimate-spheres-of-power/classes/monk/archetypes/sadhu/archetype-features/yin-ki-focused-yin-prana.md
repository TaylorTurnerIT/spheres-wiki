name: "Yin Ki Focused – Yin Prana"
level: 1
tags: []
---

The sadhu uses his animus to decrease the base movement rate by 5 feet and inflict a -1 penalty to initiative for every point of yin-aligned ki invested to those around him. The ability is a continuous burst with a 5 feet radius for every 5 sadhu levels. Targets may attempt a Will saving throw (DC 10 + half sadhu level + Wisdom modifier). Those who fail the saving throw drop in the initiative order by a number equal to the number of yin-aligned ki points invested. Once a creature has successfully saved against this ability, it cannot be affected by this sadhu's yin prana again for 24 hours. If it leaves the range of this ability, its movement, but not initiative, returns to normal.
