The sadhu calls upon the power of ire that clears the way for new life when the old life is destroyed. He can surround his fists, feet, weapons, and ammunition in lame. It is not destructive to the sadhu or his equipment. For each point of ki the sadhu expends, he inflicts +1d6 points of fire damage with any and all of his attacks for 1 round.
