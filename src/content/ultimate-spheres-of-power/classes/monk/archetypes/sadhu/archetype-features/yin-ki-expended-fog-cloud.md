name: "Yin Ki Expended – Fog Cloud"
level: 1
tags: []
---

By expending his ki focus, the sadhu dispserses his yin-aligned ki into a concealing cloud. This ability functions as the spell, fog cloud, except that the duration is a number minutes equal to the number of yin-aligned ki expended.
