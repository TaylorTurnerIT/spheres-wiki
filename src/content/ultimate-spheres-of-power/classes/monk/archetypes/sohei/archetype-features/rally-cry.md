---
id: rally-cry
name: "Rally Cry"
level: 15
tags: []
---

At 15th level, the sohei can rally his allies through a battle cry or stirring speech. All allies within 50 feet radius gain a +2 morale bonus to attack rolls, saving throws, and checks for 1 round per level. The sohei can use this ability once per day.

This ability replaces quivering palm.
