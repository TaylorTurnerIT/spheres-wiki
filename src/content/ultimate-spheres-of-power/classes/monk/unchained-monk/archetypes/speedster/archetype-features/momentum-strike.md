A speedster deals more damage with his unarmed strikes while moving at high speeds, as shown on Table: Momentum Strike. This circumstance bonus to all damage rolls applies to all attacks made with unarmed strike or monk weapons after the speedster moves but before the end of this turn. This additional damage is multiplied on a critical hit.

This replaces the increased unarmed strike damage a monk normally receives.
