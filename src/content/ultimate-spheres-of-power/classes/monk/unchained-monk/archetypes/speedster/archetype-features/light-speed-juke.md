At 4th level a speedster doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity due to movement unless the attacking creature has a Dexterity modifier equal to or greater than 1/2 the speedster's level rounded down (a speedster of 10th level would only provoke attacks from creatures with a Dexterity modifier of +5 or greater).

This replaces slow fall. For the unchained monk this replaces their 4th level ki power.
