name: "Style Technique"
level: 1
tags: []

---

Once an adaptive gunner has reached 2nd level, each time he expends or regains his psionic focus he may change the damage type of his Hand Cannon veil from bludgeoning and piercing to acid, cold, electricty, fire, or sonic damage for a number of rounds equal to his Wisdom modifier. This damage stacks with that from weapon properties like flaming, frost, shocking, etc.
