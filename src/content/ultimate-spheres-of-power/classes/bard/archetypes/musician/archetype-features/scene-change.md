The ability's area is draped in an ever-shifting illusion (as a figment *illusion* from the Illusion sphere) that incorporates all five senses. Enemies treat the ability's area as though it were difficult terrain, and the illusion itself can change under certain circumstances (as per the Programmed Illusion Illusion sphere talent), which, if met while an enemy attempts to interact with it, forces the enemy to roll the Will saving throw to disbelieve the illusion twice, taking the lowest result.
