---

At 12th level, the knave may play a song that extends the effect of his sphere abilities. Whenever he uses a sphere ability, any ally within 60 ft. who can see or hear him is considered within range, and he may spend a spell point when using a sphere ability to affect any number of allies within 60 feet.

This replaces soothing song.
