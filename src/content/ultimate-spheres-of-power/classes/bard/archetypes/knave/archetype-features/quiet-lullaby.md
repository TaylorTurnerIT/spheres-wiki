name: "Quiet Lullaby"
level: 1
replaces: ["distraction"]

---

At 1st level, a knave learns a performance that helps disguise his true intentions. When he uses this form of performance, he may make a Perform check. Any attempt to detect the knave's use of supernatural, spell-like, or sphere abilities by observing him must succeed in a Perception check against the knave's Perform check. This includes the use of the lullaby itself. The effects of a disguised ability are not hidden.

This replaces distraction.
