---
id: tunneled-musing
name: "Tunneled Musing"
level: 1
tags: []

---

At 1st level, a soul muse chooses one constellation. He may only seal pacts with starless spirits and spirits belonging to his chosen constellation. All other constellations are barred to him. Additionally, a soul muse may seal pacts with spirits that are associated with any bardic masterpiece that he knows as if they belonged to his chosen constellation. This ability counts as having the tunneled lore class feature for the purpose of meeting binder secret and feat prerequisites.
