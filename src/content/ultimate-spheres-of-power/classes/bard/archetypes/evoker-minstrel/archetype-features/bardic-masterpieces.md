Starting at 10th level, the evoker minstrel can select the following bardic masterpieces from the soundweaver kineticist archetype: The Gossamer Parapet, Heart of the Planes, Elements Awaken, The Foolish Soul, and Fey From Afar. They do not need the kinetic concert class feature, but must learn them in place of either a feat gained by achieving a higher character level or a spell slot of 4th level or higher gained by achieving a higher evoker minstrel level.
