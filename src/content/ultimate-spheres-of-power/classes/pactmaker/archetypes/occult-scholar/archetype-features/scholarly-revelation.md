---
id: scholarly-revelation
name: "Scholarly Revelation"
level: 3
tags: []

---

At 3rd level and every 3 levels thereafter, an occult scholar select one revelation from the lore oracle mystery. While the occult scholar is bound to a spirit, she gains the benefits of her chosen revelations. An occult scholar cannot select the Extra Revelation feat unless she has levels in a class with the revelation class feature.

This ability replaces pact augmentation.
