This archetype requires sphere summoner or champion summoner.

*A direct conduit to a vestige of some greater power, godly or otherwise. The void conduit is a capable wielder of their otherworldly companion, capable of limited manifestations to create miracles, devastate their foes, and otherwise channel the elseworlds directly to their own space.*

### Avatar
A void conduit gains the Conjuration sphere as a bonus talent and their conjuration companion gains the voidrusher archetype. If the void conduit already possesses the Conjuration sphere, they must select the Extra Companion talent (or purchase back any drawbacks which remove the ability to summon a companion). The Conjuration sphere companion granted by this talent must be given the avatar companion archetype and is referred to by this class as the void conduit's "avatar". The void conduit uses their void conduit level as their caster level when determining the statistics and abilities of their avatar. This stacks normally with caster levels gained from other sources.

The void conduit's avatar is always designated as the void conduit's 'eidolon', and any summoner class features and abilities that reference the void conduit's eidolon refer to the void conduit's avatar.

This alters eidolon.

### Commandment (Su)
The void conduit is connected to their power, and their power responds in kind.

At 1st level, as a swift action, the void conduit may grant their avatar a single move, standard, or swift action, which it must take immediately. The void conduit may only use commandment once every 1d4 rounds.

The void conduit cannot use commandment while the void conduit's avatar is summoned (and not possessing the void conduit), such as temporarily "summoned" with the merge with the void ability, or summoned normally with the manifest the void ability.

At 8th level, the void conduit may spend 1 spell point as a free action to refresh this ability.

At 14th level, commandment may be used once every 1d3 rounds.

### Avatar Mastery (Su)
The void conduit gains a bonus Conjuration (form) talent at 1st level and every odd level thereafter. This (form) talent must be applied to the void conduit's avatar.

Whenever the void conduit rests to regain spell points, the void conduit may choose to learn a new (form) talent in place of one of the selected (form) talent they have learned from this ability. In effect, losing the previously selected (form) talent and exchanging it for a new one.

This replaces summon mastery from the sphere summoner archetype.
