id: greater-share-veil
name: "Greater Share Veil"
level: 18
tags: []
---

At 18th level level the caller may now gain the benefits of any three veils currently shaped by his called yaksa when he uses his share veil ability. The caller no longer needs to end his turn within 30 feet of his yaksa, and may use these abilities so long as he and his called yaksa occupy the same plane. These benefits still end if the yaksa is helpless or unconscious.

This ability replaces greater aspect.
