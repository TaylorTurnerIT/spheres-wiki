---
id: reunion
name: "Reunion"
level: 20
tags: []
---

At 20th level, as a standard action the mirrored soul and her soul eidolon may merge, granting the mirrored soul the benefit of all the (form) talents the eidolon possesses. The soul eidolon counts as not being summoned for the duration of the merger and cannot take actions nor be targeted or affected by any effect. The mirrored soul may end this effect as a free action. While merged, once per round the mirrored soul may make an attack action and cast a sphere ability at the same time, using the longer action time of the two abilities.
