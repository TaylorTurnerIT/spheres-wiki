name: "Mystic Aegis"
type: class-trait
system: power
tags: ["sp"]
className: mageknight
featureId: mystic-combat-su
requires: "Protection sphere"

---

When you create an aegis on yourself, you may use your mageknight class level in place of your caster level. In addition, if you use a (succor) talent to sacrifice an aegis you created that you bear, you may use your class level as your caster level. This stacks normally with caster levels from other sources.
