At level 11, the Swarm Master may take on the form of a swarm, gaining the swarm template for a number of minutes equal to his class level. He gains the movement, space, and swarm damage of his pestilence swarm as well as the benefits of the swarm type. He cannot use any abilities that would require anatomy he does not have in this form, such as veils or speech.

This ability replaces shadow twin.
