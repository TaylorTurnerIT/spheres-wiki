---
id: entombed
name: "Entombed"
level: 1
tags: []
---

The target is entombed in chains of force unless they make a Reflex save. They are entangled and anchored to the ground, unable to move from their square. Breaking free is a move action, requiring either a Strength check or an Escape Artist check against a save DC equal 10 + the grand purifier's class level + her Charisma modifier. A target may also destroy the chains by dealing 3 damage per class level to the grand purifier. This causes the chains to vanish and removes the entangled condition. The chains are completely effective against incorporeal targets, but not against teleportation.
