---
id: spirit-sight
name: "Spirit Sight"
level: 5
replaces: ["detect undead"]
tags: []

---

At 5th level, you gain blindsight to a range of 10 feet. This ability only allows you to detect living creatures and undead creatures. This sight also tells you whether a creature is living or undead. Constructs and other creatures that are neither living nor undead cannot be seen with this ability. The range of this ability increases by 5 ft. at 7th level, and by an additional 5 ft. for every four levels beyond 7th to a maximum of 30 ft. at 19th level.

As a move action, you may double the range of your spirit sense for 1 round.

This replaces detect undead.
