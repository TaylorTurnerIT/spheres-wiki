---
id: projection-favoritism
name: "Projection Favoritism"
level: 4
tags: []

---

Starting at 4th level, whenever the projectionist would learn a new rune by virtue of increasing in level, he may choose to forego learning a new rune. If he does, he instead selects a known projection rune with a Least variant. When inscribed as a Least rune, the selected rune may be activated for free twice per day rather than just once. Starting at 12th level, the projectionist may select a known projection rune with a Lesser variant instead. When inscribed as a Lesser rune, the selected rune may be activated for free twice per day rather than just once.

This ability replaces modify runes.
