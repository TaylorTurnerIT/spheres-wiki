name: "Prepared Adventurer"
level: 2
tags: []
---

At 2nd level, a campaigner gains The Adventuring Soldier as a bonus feat. He does not need to meet the prerequisites of this feat. In addition, at 4th level and every two campaigner levels thereafter, the number of temporary inscription points granted by the The Adventuring Soldier feat increases by +1.

This ability replaces practiced scribe.
