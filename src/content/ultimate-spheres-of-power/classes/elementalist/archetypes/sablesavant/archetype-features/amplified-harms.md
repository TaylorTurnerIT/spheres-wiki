: Whenever the sablesavant damages a target within a magical darkness they created (such as a Dark sphere *gloom* or other effect), the sablesavant deals additional damage equal to 1/2 their sablesavant level. This additional damage is treated as part of the initial damaging effect and is not treated as a separate source (i.e. a 6th level sablesavant deals 14 fire damage with a *destructive blast* against a target within a *gloom*; the sablesavant would deal +3 damage, treating the total damage as 17).
