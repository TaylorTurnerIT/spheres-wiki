At 3rd level, and every odd level thereafter, the sablesavant selects a sablesavant gift, a unique effect which augments their Dark sphere, Destruction sphere, or other personal capacity. A full list of sablesavant gifts is included below.

This replaces favored element, elemental defense, and elemental movement.
