The sablesavant gains a fly speed with maneuverability (good) equal to 1/2 their base land speed and can always choose to fall slowly (this otherwise functions as a constant *[https:*www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=feather%20fall feather fall]// effect). In an area of bright light, this fly speed is reduced by 20 feet (minimum 10 feet).
