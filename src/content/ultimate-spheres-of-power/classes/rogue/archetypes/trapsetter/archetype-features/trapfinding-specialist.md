id: trapfinding-specialist
name: "Trapfinding Specialist"
level: 1
tags: []

---

A trapsetter rogue adds 1/2 his level to Craft (traps) skill checks and to his Perception skill checks made to locate traps and Disable Device skill checks, but cannot use Disable Device at 1st level to disarm magic traps. A trapsetter rogue can buy back the ability to disarm magic traps if he wishes by spending a rogue talent on this ability.

This ability alters trapfinding.
