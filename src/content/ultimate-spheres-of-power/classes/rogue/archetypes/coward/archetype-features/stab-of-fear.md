---
id: stab-of-fear
name: "STAB OF FEAR"
level: 1
tags: []

---

When a coward successfully delivers a sneak attack against an opponent, that creature must immediately make a Will save or suffer the effects of the coward's fearmonger class feature. In addition, a coward who successfully delivers a sneak attack may elect to automatically raise or lower his own level of fear by 1. This occurs before his enemy makes its Will save.
