---
id: watch-out
name: "WATCH OUT!"
level: 1
tags: []

---

If a coward successfully makes a Reflex save that triggers his evasion class feature, all allies that can see and hear him receive a +4 bonus on that same Reflex save. Providing this bonus typically involves the coward shouting at the top of his lungs and waving his arms around. A coward with this talent can elect not to provide this bonus if he wishes.
