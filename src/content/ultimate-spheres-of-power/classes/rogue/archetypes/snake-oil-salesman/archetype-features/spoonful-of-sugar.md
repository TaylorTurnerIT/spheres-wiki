id: spoonful-of-sugar
name: "Spoonful of Sugar"
level: 1
tags: []

---

Whenever a snake oil salesman uses an enhancement and chooses to create pills, she may coat the pill in a contact or ingestion poison as part of the same action without the risk of poisoning herself. If she does so, any attempts to magically detect the poison must pass a successful magic skill check or fail automatically.

This ability modifies rogue talents.
