The planar custodian gains a bonus language depending upon their primary element: air gains Auran, earth gains Terran, fire gains Ignan, water gains Aquan, and wood gains Sylvan. This is in addition to Druidic and any other bonus languages they gain through their race, Intelligence modifier, or ranks in Linguistics.
