---
id: instantaneous-defenses
name: "Instantaneous Defenses"
level: 15
replaces: ["the dizzying defense deed"]
tags: []
---

At 15th level, the hasted blade gains Improved Haste as a bonus talent; if she already knows this talent she may instead gain any other talent she qualifies for from the Time sphere. In addition, whenever the hasted blade uses haste on herself, she increases the dodge bonus to AC and Reflex saving throws granted by Improved Haste to +4; this increase only affects the hasted blade, even if that casting of haste would normally affect multiple targets (such as when using Group Time).

This replaces the dizzying defense deed.
