name: "Dilettante Weapon Training"
level: 5
tags: []

---

At 5th level, an esoteric dilettante gains a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with light or one-handed weapon piercing melee weapons and with any melee touch attacks that she makes. While wielding such a weapon, she gains the benefit of the Improved Critical feat. These attack and damage bonuses increase by 1 for every 4 levels beyond 5th (to a maximum of +4 at 17th level).

This ability alters swashbuckler weapon training.
