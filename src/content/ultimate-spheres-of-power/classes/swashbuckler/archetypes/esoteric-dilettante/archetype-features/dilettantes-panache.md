---
id: dilettantes-panache
name: "Dilettante's Panache"
level: 1
tags: []
---

Each day, an esoteric dilettante gains a number of panache points equal to her Charisma modifier. Unlike other swashbucklers, an esoteric dilettante gains panache only from scoring a critical hit or killing blow against a creature that qualifies as the favored enemy of one of her bound spirits.

This ability alters the panache class feature.
