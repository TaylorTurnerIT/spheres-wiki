The following abilities replace foretell and perfect knowledge.

**Weave the Threads (Su)**
At 8th level, each day when you rest to regain spell points, you gain a +3 insight bonus that can be used on any one die roll before the result is revealed. Every four incanter levels thereafter, this ability may be used one additional time per day and the insight bonus is increased by 1. You may spend 2 uses of this ability to apply it to a die roll after the result is revealed.

**Perpetual Foresight (Su)**
At 20th, anytime you roll a d20, you may spend an immediate action to roll a second time and choose either result. You may choose to use this ability after you see the result.

*Source: 3PP*
