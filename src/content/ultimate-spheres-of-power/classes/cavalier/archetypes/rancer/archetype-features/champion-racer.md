---

At 17th level, if the rancer's orthella is a lighting orthella, it increases its land speed again, for a total of x4 movement speed (240 for a typical lightning orthella). If it is a fire orthella, its Strength score improves by 4 (for a total of 26 including its 4th-level advancement).

This ability replaces master tactician.
