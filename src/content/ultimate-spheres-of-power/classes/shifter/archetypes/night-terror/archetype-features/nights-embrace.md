---
id: nights-embrace
name: "Night's Embrace"
level: 1
alters: ["quick transformation"]
tags: []

---

At 1st level, while the night terror is within a darkened area, the night terror reduces the sphere ability casting time of their Alteration sphere *shapeshift* or Dark sphere *cloak* to target themself (and only themself) by 1 step (minimum swift action).

Additionally, the night terror reduces the action cost to maintain concentration on a *shapeshift* or *cloak* placed on themselves (and only themselves) by 1 step (minimum swift action).

This alters quick transformation.
