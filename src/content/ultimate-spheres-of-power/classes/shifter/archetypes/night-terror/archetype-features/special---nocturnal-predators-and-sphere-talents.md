name: "Special - Nocturnal Predators and Sphere Talents"
level: 1
tags: []
---

If a nocturnal predator would gain sphere talents, the nocturnal predator gains the base sphere (or the selected talent, if it is already granted or possesses that base talent). Nocturnal predators cannot be granted magic sphere talents unless the night terror possesses the Living Darkness Mastery advanced talent; if granted a magic sphere talent, a nocturnal predator gains 1 spell point when summoned and is treated as a low caster.
