The harpy can command, giving her the power to give a single magical command to the target of her insult. This action can be something that would be against their normal alignment and interests, but not something that would result in certain death. They can be compelled to attack allies, consume items, etc. This is most appropriately done by challenging their masculinity, insinuating that you could do better, etc.
