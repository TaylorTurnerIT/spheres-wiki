---
id: belittling-barrage
name: "Belittling Barrage"
level: 1
tags: []

---

The harpy savages their target by berating them with a hailstorm of insults, insinuations, and "harsh truths". They take a penalty on all attack rolls and skill checks equal to 1/4th her harpy level (minimum -1) for a number of rounds equal to her Charisma modifier. If the harpy is at least 6th level, she also has the option to have the creature reduced in size as per reduce person for the same duration.
