Oh no you didn't! In fact, you won't be. This insult may be used as an immediate action when an opponent tries to make an attack against the harpy or one of her allies. If the creature fails with a Will save they have a choice. Either forgo the declared attack action, wasting it or take 1d8 + twice the harpy's level in non-lethal damage. The damage is resolved before the creature makes their attack.
