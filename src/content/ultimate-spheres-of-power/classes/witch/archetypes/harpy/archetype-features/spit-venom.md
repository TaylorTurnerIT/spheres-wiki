---
id: spit-venom
name: "Spit Venom"
level: 1
tags: ["combat", "mythic"]

---

A creature affected by this insult is poisoned by a specific injury poison of the harpy's choice whose value is equal to or less than 100 gp per harpy level.

In addition, the harpy may select one of the following hexes in place of an insult. Any instance where the witch uses her Intelligence, a harpy uses her Charisma modifier for these hexes:

- Cackle
- Charm
- Discord
- Evil Eye
- Tongues
