name: "Lag (Hack)"
level: 1
tags: []
---

As a full-round action that provokes an attack of opportunity a glitch can remove a creature from reality until the start of the creature's next turn. The creature is removed from time and space, existing ahead of time and only reappearing in the normal flow of time once its next turn has started. To the creature they simply skip forward in time a few seconds. However this removes them from play, allowing foes to not make attacks of opportunity, allies to avoid what would normally be a deadly attack, etc.
*Recoil:* 1/2 CR of the creature
*Required Level:* 10th
