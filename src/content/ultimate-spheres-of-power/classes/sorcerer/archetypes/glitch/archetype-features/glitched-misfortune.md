id: glitched-misfortune
name: "Glitched Misfortune"
level: 1
tags: []

---

Reduce the number the glitch has rolled on any dice by 1/2 the amount of recoil spent. The new amount rolled is considered as what was naturally rolled (if it is reduced to 1 it is considered a critical failure for example).
*Recoil:* 2 per number reduced by.
