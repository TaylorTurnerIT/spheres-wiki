id: glitched-object
name: "Glitched Object"
level: 1
tags: []
---

When a glitch draws a weapon it gains the broken condition (regardless of if it is magical or not) upon its first successful hit, causing it to miss retroactively. This can also be used to cause a piece of defensive equipment (such as armor or a shield) to gain the broken condition right before it is struck (dropping the AC of the glitch). This cannot effect magic equipment but can instead render it completely non-magical for 1d6 rounds.
*Recoil:* 1/2 CR of glitch
