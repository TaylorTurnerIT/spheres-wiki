---
id: glitched-social-order
name: "Glitched Social Order"
level: 1
tags: []
---

The next town, hamlet, or city the party comes across has its starting attitude moved 2 towards hostile (to a maximum of hostile) towards the party. The amount of recoil this costs is dependent on the size of the population. This lasts for 3d4 days and starts when the party enters the town.
*Recoil:* 2 per 10,000 people (1 for 5,000 or less)
