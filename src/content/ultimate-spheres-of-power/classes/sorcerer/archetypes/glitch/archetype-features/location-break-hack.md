name: "Location Break (Hack)"
level: 1
tags: []
---

As a move action that does not provoke an attack of opportunity, a glitch may swap places with another enemy creature within 30 feet of them. The glitch may only swap places with a creature of it's own size category.
*Recoil:* 3 if willing, CR of the creature if unwilling
*Required Level:* 4th
