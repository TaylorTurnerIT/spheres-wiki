name: "Magical Manipulation (Hack)"
level: 1
tags: []
---

As a swift action that provokes an attack of opportunity a glitch may allow a prepared spellcaster within 30 feet of them to re-prepare their remaining spells. This may only be used once per day on a given spellcaster.
*Recoil:* 1/2 caster level of prepared spellcaster (minimum 4)
*Required Level:* 4th
