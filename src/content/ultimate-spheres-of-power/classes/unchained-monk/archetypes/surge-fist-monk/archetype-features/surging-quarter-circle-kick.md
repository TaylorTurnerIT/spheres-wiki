A surge fist can be used with one's feet. A surge fist performed in this manner does not require open hands to use, but has its range reduced to 5 ft. and cannot have extended range surge fist or extreme range surge fist applied to it. Style strikes involving kicks can be used with a surge fist made this way, but not those involving fists.
