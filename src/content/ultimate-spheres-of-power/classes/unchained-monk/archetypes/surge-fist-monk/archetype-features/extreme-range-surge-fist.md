---
id: extreme-range-surge-fist
name: "Extreme Range Surge Fist"
level: 1
tags: []
---

By spending 2 points from their ki pool as a swift action, a surge fist monk can increase the range of surge fists they use this turn to 480 ft. A surge fist monk must have the extended range surge fist ki power and be 6th level to select this ki power.
