---
id: ki-infusion-specialization
name: "Ki Infusion Specialization"
level: 1
tags: []

---

Whenever the surge fist monk applies one or more infusions to their surge fist, reduce the combined ki point cost of those infusions by 1 (to a minimum ki point cost of 0). This ki power can be selected up to 3 times; its effects stack. A surge fist monk must be 8th level to select this ki power, and select it a second and third time at 12th and 16th level respectively.
