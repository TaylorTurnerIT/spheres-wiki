---
id: unseen-seer
name: "Unseen Seer"
level: 4
replaces: ["debilitating injury"]
tags: []
---

At 4th level, any divination effect (other than sense talents and abilities) reveals nothing about a treasure seeker or any of the treasure seeker's gear, unless the origin of the effect succeeds on an MSB check against the treasure seeker's MSD + 5. This replaces debilitating injury.
