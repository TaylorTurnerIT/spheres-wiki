Rather than trusting only her wits and guts, a lucky bastard relies on Lady Luck's blessing in combat. Starting at 2nd level, she gains a pool of kismet: a fluctuating measure of her ability to perform amazing actions in combat. At the start of each day, she gains a number of kismet points equal to her Charisma modifier (minimum 1). Her kismet goes up or down throughout the day, but usually cannot go higher than her Charisma modifier (minimum 1), though feats and magic items can affect this maximum.
