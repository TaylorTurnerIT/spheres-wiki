The hedgewitch can magically adjust her personal space in slight ways, allowing her to wear one additional magic item without it interfering with the other ones. She gains an untyped item slot that can hold any item of any slot, such as a second headband, a second pair of gloves, or third ring. This extra magic item can be stolen, sundered, or removed just like any other item.
