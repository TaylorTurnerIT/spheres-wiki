The vigilante is treated as both undead and his usual creature type for the purpose of determining what spells and effects may target him. He is damaged by negative energy and healed by positive energy while in his vigilante identity, and gains a +2 racial bonus to saving throws against disease, mind-affecting effects, death effects, and paralysis.
