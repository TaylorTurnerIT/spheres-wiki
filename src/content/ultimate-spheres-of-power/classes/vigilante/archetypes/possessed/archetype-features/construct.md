The vigilante is treated as both a construct and his usual creature type for the purpose of what spells and effects may target him. The vigilante does not need to breathe while in vigilante form, and gains a +2 racial bonus to saving throws against disease, mind-affecting effects, poison, and effects that cause either exhaustion or fatigue.
