---
id: concerted-attack
name: "Concerted Attack"
level: 1
replaces: ["the stunning appearance class feature"]
tags: []

---

From 17th level on, whenever the vigilante confirms a critical hit against an opponent, each ally adjacent to that opponent may make a single melee attack against it as an immediate action.

This replaces the stunning appearance class feature.
