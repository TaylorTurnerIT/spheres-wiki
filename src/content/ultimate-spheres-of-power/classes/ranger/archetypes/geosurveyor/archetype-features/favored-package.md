id: favored-package
name: "Favored Package"
level: 3
replaces: ["the hunter’s bond class feature"]
tags: []

---

At 3rd level, a geosurveyor chooses one of the packages from the Nature sphere and gains a +2 bonus to their caster level with that package. At 9th level, this bonus improves by an additional +2, and they may choose another package to gain a +2 bonus with. At 15th level, all previously chosen packages increase their caster level by another +2, and the geosurveyor may choose a third package to gain a +2 bonus. These bonuses stack with other class features with the same name.

This replaces the hunter's bond class feature.
