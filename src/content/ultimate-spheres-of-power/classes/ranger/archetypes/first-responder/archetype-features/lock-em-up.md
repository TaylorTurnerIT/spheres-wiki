---
id: lock-em-up
name: "Lock 'Em Up"
level: 1
tags: []

---

The first responder may apply manacles of an appropriate size to a nongrappled enemy as a full round action. The first responder makes an attack roll at his full BAB against an adjacent enemy and if the attack hits the manacles are applied. In addition, when using a lasso, a police officer can slide the knot to reuse it as a free action rather than a standard.
