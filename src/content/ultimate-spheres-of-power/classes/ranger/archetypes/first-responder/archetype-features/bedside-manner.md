---
id: bedside-manner
name: "Bedside Manner"
level: 8
replaces: ["wild empathy and swift tracker"]
tags: []

---

A first responder can attempt a Diplomacy check (DC 10 + creature's Will save) against a creature within 30 feet of him. If successful the creature receives the effect of a calm emotions spell. At 8th level he may make a single roll and effect all creatures within 30 feet of it. This replaces wild empathy and swift tracker.
