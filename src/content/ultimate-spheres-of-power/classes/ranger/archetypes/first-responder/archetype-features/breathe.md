---
id: breathe
name: "Breathe!"
level: 1
tags: []
---

As a swift action, the first responder can heal a creature for his Wisdom modifier + his level in hit points by touching a creature. He may only do this a number of times per day equal to his Wisdom modifier. He may take this multiple times and each times he takes it he adds 3 to the number of times per day he can use it.
