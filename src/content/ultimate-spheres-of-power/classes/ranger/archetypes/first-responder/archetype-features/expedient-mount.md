id: expedient-mount
name: "Expedient Mount"
level: 4
tags: []
---

At 4th level, the first responder gains a mount that can quickly deliver him to where he is most needed. This ability functions like the cavalier's mount ability, except that the first responder's effective cavalier level is equal to his ranger level -3 and that the mount gains a +20 foot bonus to its base movement speed.
