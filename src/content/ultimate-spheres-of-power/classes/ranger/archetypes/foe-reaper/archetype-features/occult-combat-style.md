id: occult-combat-style
name: "Occult Combat Style"
level: 2
tags: []
---

At 2nd level and every four levels thereafter, foe reaper gains a pact feat as a bonus feat. Alternatively, the foe reaper can select a binder secret in place of a pact feat. He must meet the feat or binder secret's prerequisites, if any, and his ranger levels stack with any pactmaker levels he possesses for the purpose of meeting the prerequisites for binder secrets.

This ability replaces all combat style feats.
