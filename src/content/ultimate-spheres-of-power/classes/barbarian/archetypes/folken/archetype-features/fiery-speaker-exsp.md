name: "Fiery Speaker (Ex/Sp)"
level: 1
tags: []

---

While speaking in his native tongue, a folken can boil the blood and ignite the flame of passion in the hearts of men. While speaking in his native tongue to those who understand his language he may make a Diplomacy check (DC 20) as a swift action to grant an ally who shares his language the benefits of the *rage* spell for a number of rounds equal to 1 + the number of rounds he exceeded the DC by. Doing this consumes 2 rounds of rage.
