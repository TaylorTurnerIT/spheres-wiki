level: 3
replaces: ["trap sense"]
tags: []

---

At 3rd level, a folken gains a +1 bonus on all Knowledge checks pertaining to his homeland and an equal morale bonus on critical confirmation rolls with one weapon native or iconic to his culture/homeland. These bonuses increase by +1 every 3 levels thereafter (6th, 9th, 12th, 15th, and 18th level).

This replaces trap sense.
