Starting at 2nd level, a folken gains an oddity from the list below. He gains another folken oddity for every two levels of folken attained after 2nd level. Unless otherwise noted, a folken cannot select an individual oddity more than once.

He may select a rage power from the following list in place of a folken talent: auspicious mark, boasting taunt, brawler, come and get me, crippling blow, deadly accuracy, disruptive, eater of magic, flesh wound, ghost rager, good for what ails you, hurling (lesser), hurling , hurling (greater), hurling charge, increased damage reduction, inspire ferocity, intimidating glare, lethal accuracy, liquid courage, mighty swing, moment of clarity, powerful blow, quick reflexes, raging climber, raging leaper, raging swimmer, reckless abandon, roaring drunk, rolling dodge, roused anger, smasher, spell sunder, spellbreaker, staggering drunk, strength surge, sunder enchantment, superstition, surprise accuracy, swift foot, unexpected strike, witch hunter.

In addition to the normal requirements of using a rage power, if it uses a weapon (example: when making extra attacks of opportunity while raging via the quick reflexes rage power) the folken must also be using the weapon specified in his stranger in a strange land class feature.
