id: totem-pact
name: "Totem Pact"
level: 5
tags: []

---

At 5th level, the totem pactmaster forges a pact with an animal of the same type as his totem. This ability functions as a druid's animal companion, using the barbarian's level –4 as her effective druid level.

This ability replaces uncanny dodge and improved uncanny dodge.
