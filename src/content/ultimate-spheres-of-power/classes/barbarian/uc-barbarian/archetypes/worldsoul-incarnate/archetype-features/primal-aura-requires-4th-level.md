name: "Primal Aura (requires 4th Level)"
level: 1
tags: []

---

While she is enraptured, she may give off an aura of positive energy that causes her and all living and undead creatures within 20 ft. to glow slightly, giving them a penalty on Stealth checks equal to her class level and negating all bonuses usually bestowed by invisibility, blink effects, darkness, or similar effects. While the aura is in effect, she gains a +5 circumstance bonus to Intimidate checks, and sheds bright light to a distance of 20 ft. around her, and normal light another 20 ft. beyond that. The light harms creatures vulnerable to natural sunlight as if it were natural sunlight.
