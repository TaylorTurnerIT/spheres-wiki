tags: []
---

While enraptured, the worldsoul incarnate may channel primal energy to create powerful supernatural effects. Whenever she spends a free action to enter or maintain rapture, she gains a number of vitality points equal to her class level. She may spend vitality points to achieve different effects. At the end of her turn, she loses all unspent vitality points.
