id: punctuated-fury
name: "Punctuated Fury"
level: 19
replaces: ["armor mastery"]
tags: []

---

At 19th level, an impressor may seamlessly blend martial prowess and emotional technique. When making a full round attack, the impressor may also use an emotion power (the attacks and power may be directed against different targets). This bonus extends even to attacks modified to channel emotional powers (such as the Elicit Strike or Impressionistic Strike feats).

This replaces armor mastery.
