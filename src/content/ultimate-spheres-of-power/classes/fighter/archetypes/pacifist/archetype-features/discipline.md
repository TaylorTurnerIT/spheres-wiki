id: discipline
name: "Discipline"
level: 2
tags: []

---

Starting at 2nd level, the pacifist gains a +1 bonus on saving throws against effects that would force him to take action (or inaction) against his will (such as a charm person or dominate person spell). This bonus increases by +1 for every four levels beyond 2nd. If subject to an ability that dictates his actions with no save he applies this bonus as a penalty to any d20 roll the creature must make to activate that ability (such as the Intimidate roll required to use the Antagonize feat).

This ability replaces bravery.
