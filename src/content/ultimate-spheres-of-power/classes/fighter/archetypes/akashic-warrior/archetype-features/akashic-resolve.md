---
id: akashic-resolve
name: "Akashic Resolve"
level: 2
replaces: ["bravery"]
tags: []
---

At 2nd level an akashic warrior can fortify his stubborn resolve and determination with essence. For each point of essence invested into this ability he gains a +1 bonus to saves vs. fear and enchantment (compulsion) effects. This replaces bravery.
