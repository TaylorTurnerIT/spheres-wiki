---
id: steel-legend
name: "Steel Legend"
level: 20
tags: []

---

At 20th level, a spirit-wielder's awakened weapons are considered part of their body for the purposes of resurrection. In addition, the spirit-wielder cannot be disarmed of their awakened weapons, and may take 10 on an attack roll made with it once per round.
