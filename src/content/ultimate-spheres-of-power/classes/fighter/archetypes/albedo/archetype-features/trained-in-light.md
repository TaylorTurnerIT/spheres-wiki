The albedo gains 4 skill points + a number of skill points equal to her Intelligence modifier at each level, instead of the normal 2 skill points + Intelligence modifier at each level. Furthermore, Knowledge (arcana), Knowledge (geography), Knowledge (planes), Perception, and Spellcraft are all class skills for her.
