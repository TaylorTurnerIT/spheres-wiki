name: Bonus Feats
level: 1
tags: []
---

A warbinder may select combat feats and pact feats as bonus feats. In addition, he may select a binder secret in place of a bonus feat. The warbinder must meet the feat or binder secret's prerequisites, if any.

This ability alters the fighter's bonus feats.
