From 10th level on, the fracture may expend one of his attacks of opportunity for the round as an immediate action to allow himself or an ally within 30 ft. to reroll a to reroll a single failed Strength- or Dexterity-based skill check. The fracture must choose whether or not to use this ability as soon as he knows the result of the failed roll.

This replaces the bonus feat normally gained at 10th level.
