A pyrotechnician's bombs are extraordinary, rather than supernatural. He may make these bombs at any time with ten minutes worth of work per bomb and the expenditure of 20gp worth of gunpowder per d6 points of damage that the bomb deals. These are treated as exotic weapons with which he is proficient. These bombs remain useable for up to 1 day per 4 pyrotechnician levels (minimum 1) and other characters may use them during this time.

This modifies the alchemist's bombs class feature
