At 6th level, the pyrotechnician may snag a thrown splash weapon out of the air with casual ease. This functions as the Deflect Arrows feat and Snatch Arrows feat, save that it only works with splash weapons. If the pyrotechnician has these feats already he may deflect one additional ranged attack per round.

This replaces the alchemist's swift poisoning class feature.
