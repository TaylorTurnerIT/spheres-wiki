id: quick-worker
name: "Quick Worker"
level: 18
tags: []

---

This ability functions as the alchemist's swift alchemy class feature but only functions with explosives and fireworks, other than the ones created with his bomb class feature. If the explosive deals damage it deals an additional 2d6 points of damage. If the explosive relies on a save DC the DC becomes 10 + ½ pyrotechnician level + his Intelligence modifier. At 18th level this functions as instant alchemy and the explosive deals an extra 4d6 points of damage.

This modifies the alchemist's swift and instant alchemy class features.
