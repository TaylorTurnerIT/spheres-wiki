A short delay occurs between the casting the sphere ability and the sphere effect's creation, giving the combat engineer just enough time to throw the device. They may make an attack with the device as part of the casting, and the sphere effect appears at whatever location the combat engineer throws the device. If the device hits a creature, the sphere effect is created at the location they are standing and remains there. The device is considered a thrown weapon with a range increment of 10 ft.
