At 1st level, the hemetic philosopher gains the Blood sphere as a bonus magic talent, or a talent from that sphere if he already possesses it, and treats his class level as his caster level for this sphere. These stack normally with caster levels gained from other classes.

This replaces the throw anything class feature and bombs.
