When the nocturnus uses a whisper of night and ends a shroud, that shroud lingers for 2 rounds. It cannot be used for a whisper of night again, but continues to apply its penalties for this duration. Placing a new shroud on a target with a persisting shroud ends the persisting shroud immediately.
