At 20th level, a comrade becomes an icon of the people - able to become a lightning rod for political change. His DR increases to 10/evil. Whenever he uses smite bourgeoisie and successfully strikes a creature targeted by that ability, 50% of all currency held by the creature is taken and given directly to the comrade (as per his code, this must later be distributed to his party members). In addition, whenever he channels positive energy or uses lay on hands to heal a creature, he heals the maximum possible amount.

This ability replaces holy champion.
