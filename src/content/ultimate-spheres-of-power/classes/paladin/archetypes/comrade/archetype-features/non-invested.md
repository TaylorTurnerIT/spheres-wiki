This is the lowest level of governmental employees. They are typically minor functionaries (scribes, messengers, etc), town guards or non-officers in the military (unless they believe wholeheartedly in the ideals of their government), servants, and the like who are simply employed by the government or nobility but have no real vested interest in them.
