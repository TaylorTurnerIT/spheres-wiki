These are people high up on the ladder who run their government or have a hand in it. This group of people include those with a noble title, high ranking clergy members (theocracy), knights, generals, any sort of career politician, filthy rich businessman, etc. When determining if a creature is simply "invested" or a "patriot" ask yourself, "does the creature have an active hand in running the government?". If so, then they are a patriot.

This ability replaces detect evil.
