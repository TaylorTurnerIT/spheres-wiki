These are people who hold a position in their own right but are not members of higher rungs of the political ladder. They believe in the government or at least support it. This typically includes most officers of the military. In a theology this might be acolytes or devotees to the faith. In a capitalistic society it might be a entrepreneur, mid-level government bureaucrat, banker, etc. In a monarchy it might be a minor lord, a squire, etc. In a tribal society it might be a high ranking warrior, elder members of the society without a ruling stake, etc.
