At 4th level the Parzivalian knight gains a motif talent as a bonus talent. Whenever she rests to regain spell points, she may change this talent to any other motif. When she uses this motif on herself, the spell point cost is reduced by 1 (to a minimum of 0). This ability replaces channel energy.
