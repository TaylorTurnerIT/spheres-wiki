id: spellpurging-smite
name: "Spellpurging Smite"
level: 6
tags: []

---

At 6th level, whenever the avowed would smite a creature, the avowed can attempt to dispel a magic item or existing magical effect that the target possesses or is benefiting from, functioning as the Counterspell feat. The avowed can spend a spell point to choose an additional object or effect on the target, plus an additional object or effect for every 10 avowed levels she possesses.
