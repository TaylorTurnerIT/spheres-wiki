---
id: shared-narrative
name: "Shared Narrative"
level: 6
tags: []

---

At 6th level, the avowed may discharge a motif affecting her as an immediate action and have the discharged motif affect an ally within the area of one of her consecrations as if the ally had discharged the motif themselves. For example, when an ally is struck by a manufactured weapon, the avowed may discharge The Tower to destroy the weapon.
