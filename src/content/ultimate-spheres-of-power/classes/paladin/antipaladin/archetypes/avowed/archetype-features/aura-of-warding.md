---
id: aura-of-warding
name: "Aura of Warding"
level: 8
tags: []

---

At 8th level, the avowed radiates a subtle protective aura, granting all allies within 10 feet of her a +2 morale bonus to saving throws against magical effects. This bonus is doubled against effects that originate from creatures that the avowed has an active smite on.

If the avowed shares the ability to smite via aura of justice or vengeance, or a similar ability, their allies gain this same benefit against creatures they smite. This ability functions only while the avowed is conscious, not if she is unconscious or dead.
