At 20th level, whenever a creature casts a spell or sphere effect on an enemy the avowed can attempt to counterspell that effect as the Counterspell feat, except it does not cost a spell point and she may do this as a free action that can be taken outside of her turn.

### Pledge of Condemnation [SM—]
Putting aside all scruples to focus exclusively on the destruction of their enemy, those under a pledge of condemnation forsake the improvement or protection of the self in favor of raw offensive prowess.
