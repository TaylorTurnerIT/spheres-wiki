---
id: aura-of-unity
name: "Aura of Unity"
level: 8
tags: []

---

At 8th level, all allies within 10 feet of the avowed are immune to the confused condition, in addition to being immune to the effects of harmful spell, spell-like, sphere, or supernatural abilities that originate from other allies (even if they are outside this aura), as long as the effect are subject to spell resistance and cover an area.
