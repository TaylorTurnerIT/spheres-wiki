---
id: biofeedback
name: "Biofeedback"
level: 1
tags: []

---

As a standard action, the ascendant mind may reprogram the mind of a willing creature to ignore pain and injury. The creature receives a number of temporary hit points equal to twice the ascendant mind's class level, heals an equal amount of nonlethal damage, and any condition that currently affecting them has its remaining duration shortened by 2 rounds.
