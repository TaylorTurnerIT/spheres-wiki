---
id: ego-whip
name: "Ego Whip"
level: 1
tags: []
---

As a standard action, the ascendant mind may lash out with a tendril of ectoplasmic matter at an enemy. She makes a ranged touch attack against a creature within 30 feet. If successful, the creature receives a -2 penalty to its Will saving throws for 1 minute. This penalty does not stack with itself.
