name: "Id Insinuation"
level: 1
tags: []

---

As a standard action, the ascendant mind may envelop a target within 60 feet in a telepathic static that subtly influences it. The target must succeed at a save or it immediately make an attack on a target of the ascendant mind's choice other than itself. This is an enchantment (compulsion) ability.
