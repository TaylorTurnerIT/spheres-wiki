---
id: psychic-crush
name: "Psychic Crush"
level: 1
tags: []

---

As a standard action, the ascendant mind can reach out with her mind to another mind within 30 feet, and crush it. The target must succeed at a save or be dazed for 1 round. If successful, it is staggered for 1 round instead. If a creature that fails a saving throw against this ability has a current hit point total less than the ascendant mind's current hit point total, it is overwhelmed by her life force, and immediately drop to -1 hit point. It is unconscious, but stable.
