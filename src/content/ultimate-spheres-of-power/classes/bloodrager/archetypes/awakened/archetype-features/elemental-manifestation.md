name: "Elemental Manifestation"
level: 4
tags: []

---

At 4th level, an awakened can use basic utility wild talents even when not bloodraging. In addition, they gain access to the kineticist's gather charge class feature during their bloodrage, and can affect their own kinetic blasts with any bloodrager spell cast during a bloodrage that normally affects manufactured weapons until their bloodrage ends (or for the spell's normal duration, whichever is shorter).

This ability replaces eschew materials.
