---
id: infusion-specialization
name: "Infusion Specialization"
level: 7
tags: []
---

At 7th level, whenever an awakened uses one or more infusions with a kinetic blast, they reduce the combined burn cost of the infusions by 1. This cannot reduce the total cost of the infusions below 0. They reduce the burn cost by an additional 1 at 10th level and every 3 levels thereafter.

This ability replaces damage reduction.
