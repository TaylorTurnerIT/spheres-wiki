---
id: famine-aura
name: "Famine Aura"
level: 3
tags: []
---

At 3rd level, while the wendigo is in his bloodrage any living creature that begins their turn within 30 ft. or moves into this area must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 the wendigo's bloodrager level + his Charisma modifier) or be fatigued for one round per 3 class levels. Whether a creature succeeds or fails on this saving throw, they are immune to that particular wendigo's aura of famine for 24 hours. At 12th level any creature that fails its save becomes exhausted instead of fatigued and any creature that succeeds on its saving throw is fatigued for one round. At the beginning of each day, the wendigo may choose a number of creatures up to his Constitution modifier or his Charisma modifier, whichever is greater, to be immune to this aura. Any creatures bound to the wendigo or those he chooses to be immune, such as familiars or animal companions, are similarly immune.

For each creature that fails a saving throw against this ability, the duration of the wendigo's fatigue after his bloodrage ends decreases by 1.

This ability replaces all bloodline feats.
