---
id: essence-gambit
name: "Essence Gambit"
level: 1
alters: ["the warlord’s gambit feature normally gained at 1st level"]
tags: []

---

In addition to the standard effects of any gambit he performs, the veiled lord also gains 1 point of temporary essence each time he successfully performs a gambit. This essence lasts for a number of rounds equal to 1/2 the veiled lord's class level + his Charisma modifier. The veiled lord cannot have more than ½ his total character level in temporary essence gained via this ability at one time. If the veiled lord fails a gambit while benefiting from temporary essence, his rake penalty includes the loss of 1 point of temporary essence.

This alters the warlord's gambit feature normally gained at 1st level.
