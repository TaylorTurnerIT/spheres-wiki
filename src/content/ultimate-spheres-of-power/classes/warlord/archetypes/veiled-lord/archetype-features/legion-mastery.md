---

At 17th level, whenever the veiled lord uses his veiled legion ability he may share the effects of all three veils gained via his veiled legion ability with all affected allies. If the veiled lord has the ability to bind any of the veils shared via this ability, allies affected by his veiled legion ability also gain the benefits of the bind.

This replaces the master warleader ability normally gained at 17th level.
