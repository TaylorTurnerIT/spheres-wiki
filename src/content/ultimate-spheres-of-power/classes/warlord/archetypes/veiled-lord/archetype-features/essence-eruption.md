name: "Essence Eruption"
level: 10
replaces: ["the bonus feat normally gained at 10th level"]
tags: []
---

Starting at 10th level, whenever the veiled lord or an ally currently benefiting from his veiled legion ability scores a critical hit with a martial maneuver, he gains 3 points of temporary essence. This temporary essence stacks with that granted by his essence gambit ability.

This replaces the bonus feat normally gained at 10th level.
