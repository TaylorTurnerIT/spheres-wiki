id: fast-time-reload
name: "Fast-Time Reload"
level: 3
replaces: ["the pistol-whip deed"]
tags: []

---

Starting at 3rd level, as long as the clockstopper is under the effects of a haste spell or alter time effect, she may reload a one-handed firearm as a free action or a two-handed firearm as a swift action.

This replaces the pistol-whip deed.
