---
id: two-wand-fighting
name: "Two-Wand Fighting"
level: 11
tags: []

---

At 11th level a wandslinger may activate two wands as a full-round action, one in each hand, as long as their combined caster levels (before wand training) do not exceed his class level.

This ability replaces lightning reload.
