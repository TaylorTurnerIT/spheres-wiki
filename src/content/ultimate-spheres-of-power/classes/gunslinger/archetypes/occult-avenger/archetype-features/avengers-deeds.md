An occult avenger doesn't gain deeds like other gunslingers. At 1st level, 3rd level, 7th level, 11th level, 15th level, and 19th level, the occult avenger selects one deed that is available to a gunslinger of her level or lower. She gains the selected deed but doesn't gain any other deeds that she would normally be entitled to based upon her gunslinger level.

This ability alters deeds.
