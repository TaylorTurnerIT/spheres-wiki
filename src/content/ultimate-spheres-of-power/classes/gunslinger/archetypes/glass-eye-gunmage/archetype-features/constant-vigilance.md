id: constant-vigilance
name: "Constant Vigilance"
level: 3
replaces: ["any one deed gained at 3rd level"]
tags: []

---

At 3rd level, the glass-eye gunmage is never caught off guard. As long as he has at least 1 grit, he ignores penalties to Perception checks for being distracted or asleep. In addition, he can spend 1 grit point at the beginning of a battle (even during a surprise round) to not be treated as flat-footed before he has had a chance to act. This replaces any one deed gained at 3rd level.
