---

At 4th level and every four levels thereafter, the glass-eye gunmage gains a single (lens) talent of his choice from the Light sphere. For the purposes of this talent his glass-eye gunmage levels are treated as casting class levels when determining MSB and MSD. He is treated as having a caster level equal to his glass-eye gunmage level and uses the ability that determines his grit points for his casting ability modifier (usually Wisdom). This stacks normally with caster levels gained from other sources. If a specific use of this talent would require one or more spell points, he may spend an equal number of grit points instead. This ability replaces the gunslinger's bonus feats.
