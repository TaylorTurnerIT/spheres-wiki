---
id: inevitable-strike
name: "Inevitable Strike"
level: 3
replaces: ["psychic strike"]
tags: []

---

Beginning at 3rd level, the epoch-walker can charge his mind blade with temporal energy as a move action. This effect grants the soulknife a circumstance bonus on his next attack roll equal to half his class level (minimum 1). When the soulknife successfully damages an opponent with this attack, or by touching an ally as a standard action, he creates a link to them that lasts for 1 day per class level and allows him know their current location as long as they are on the same plane and within 1 mile per class level the soulknife possesses. As long as the bond created by inevitable strike is in effect and he can use it to sense the target's location, the soulknife can target the affected creature with alter time effects regardless of range, line of sight, or line of effect. The soulknife can only have a total number of inevitable strike bonds active equal to his Wisdom modifier (minimum 1); if the soulknife creates an inevitable strike bond against a creature that would increase his total number of bonds beyond this limitation, he must choose a bond to dismiss. The soulknife may dismiss an inevitable strike bond as a free action.

This replaces psychic strike.
