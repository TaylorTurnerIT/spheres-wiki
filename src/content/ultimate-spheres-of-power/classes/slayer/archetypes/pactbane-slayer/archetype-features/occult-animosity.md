---
id: occult-animosity
name: "Occult Animosity"
level: 2
tags: []
---

At 2nd level, the slayer adds half his level on Knowledge skill checks when making skill checks to identify the abilities and weaknesses of occult creatures and creatures with a binder level. If he successfully identifies such an opponent, he can make that opponent his studied target as an immediate action.

This ability replaces the slayer talent gained at 2nd level.
