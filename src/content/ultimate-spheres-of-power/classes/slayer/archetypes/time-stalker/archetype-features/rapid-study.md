id: rapid-study
name: "Rapid Study"
level: 1
tags: []

---

From 1st level on, a time stalker may choose to take 1 point of Constitution damage when using his studied target ability to process information at a supernaturally rapid pace, reducing the time need to use studied target by one step (move to swift, swift to free, immediate to a free action that can be taken even when it is not his turn).
