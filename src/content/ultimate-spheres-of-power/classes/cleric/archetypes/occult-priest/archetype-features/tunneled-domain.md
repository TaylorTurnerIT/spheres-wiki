At 1st level, an occult priest chooses one constellation that is associated with her domain, as listed below. She may only seal pacts with starless spirits and spirits belonging to her chosen constellation. All other constellations are barred to her. This ability counts as having the tunneled lore class feature for the purpose of meeting binder secret and feat prerequisites.

The constellations (and their associated cleric domains) are detailed below. An occult priest who chooses the Occult domain may select any constellation as her chosen constellation.
