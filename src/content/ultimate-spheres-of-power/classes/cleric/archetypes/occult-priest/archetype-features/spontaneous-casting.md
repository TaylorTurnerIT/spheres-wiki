---
id: spontaneous-casting
name: "Spontaneous Casting"
level: 1
tags: []

---

An occult priest can channel stored spell energy into a domain spell that she did not prepare ahead of time. The occult priest can "lose" any prepared spell that is not an orison in order to cast a spell that is on her domain's domain spell list. This ability replaces one of the cleric's domains and alters spontaneous casting.
