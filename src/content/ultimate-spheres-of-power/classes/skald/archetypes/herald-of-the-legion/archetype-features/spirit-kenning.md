---
id: spirit-kenning
name: "Spirit Kenning"
level: 5
tags: []

---

At 5th level, a herald of the legion can attempt to use stories of the Legion's prowess to seal a pact with a spirit whose Knowledge Tasks he has not completed. Once per day, a skald can choose any one spirit whose level is equal to his maximum spirit level or lower and attempt to seal a pact with it. For each of the spirit's four Knowledge Tasks that he has not completed, he increases the spirit's binding DC by 5.

At 11th level, a herald of the legion can use this ability twice per day. At 17th level, he can use this ability three times per day.

This ability replaces spell kenning.
