---

At 5th level, a storm herald is learned in the magic of other casters, and can expand his magic in untested ways. By increasing the casting time by 1 step, the storm herald can add any one magic talent to his list of talents known for the purposes of a single use of a sphere ability. The storm herald can choose a new base sphere with this ability. The storm herald can use this ability once per day at 5th level, twice per day at 11th level, and 3 times per day at 17th level.

This replaces spell kenning.
