The following abilities replace forewarned and foretell.

**Moldable Talent (Su)**
At 3rd level, you gain a magic talent as a bonus talent. Whenever you rest to regain spell points, you may change this moldable talent, losing the previous ones and gaining a new sphere or talent in its place. Only the moldable talent can be changed in this way; magic talents gained in any other manner are not eligible to be changed in this fashion.

**Instant Recall (Su)**
At 8th level, you may divine as a non-action, gaining information on any subjects you can perceive as if you had spent a full minute observing each of them. You may use this ability once per day at 8th level and an additional time every 6 levels thereafter.

*Source: 3PP*
