---
+ Class Equipment
The following magical items are especially appropriate for hedgewitches.

++++ Amateur's Reference [TS:WAT]
**Aura** faint Universal; **CL** 4th
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These well-read books come in a variety of shapes and styles, from clay tablets to thin sheets of advanced technology. A creature with both the paths class feature and the secret class feature can study an amateur's reference for one hour to gain the path benefit (but not the path power) of a single hedgewitch path, chosen when the reference manual is written. If the option would normally grant a choice, that choice must also be made when this manual is written and cannot be changed. The knowledge from this reference lasts until the next time the reader regains their spell points normally. A creature cannot benefit from more than one amateur's reference at a time.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Apparatus, Mana sphere, creator must have the hedgewitch path benefit being used; **Cost** 7,500 gp

++++ Battle Censer [TS:WAT]
**Aura** moderate Destruction; **CL** 10th
**Slot** none; **Price** 28,316 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This +1 blast vessel cold iron censer hums with eldritch energies when its glowing head is spun around. Treat the censer as a light flail for all mechanical purposes. When wielded by a creature with a hedgewitch path benefit, the battle censer gains one additional weapon special ability, as detailed on the following table, chosen from among the paths corresponding to the path benefit the creature has:

If a creature wielding the battle censer has more than one hedgewitch path benefit, they may change which path the item gains a bonus special ability from by spending 10 minutes meditating with this weapon. A hedgewitch can improve the enhancement bonus on this weapon through crafting, to a maximum of +5, by spending the difference in crafting time and materials. They do not need any crafting feats to do this. A battle censer has a total of +3 in enhancements when first crafted.

**Construction Requirements**
Smith Magical Weapons And Armor, any three base magical spheres, creator must have the hedgewitch paths ability; **Cost** 14,158 gp

++++ Book Of Secrets [TS:WAT]
**Aura** faint Mana; **CL** 5th
**Slot** none; **Price** 15,000 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This thin book contains details of how to manipulate magical energies and mundane substances in useful ways. A character with the hedgewitch secrets class feature can study this manual to gain one additional hedgewitch secret until the next time they regain spell points normally. The secret granted must be chosen when this item is made. If the option would normally grant choices, that choice must also be made when this manual is written and cannot be changed. A creature cannot benefit from more than one book of secrets at a time. This book cannot grant access to a path secret if the user does not possess the appropriate path or path benefit, and it cannot grant the amateur hedgewitch secret.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Apparatus, Mana sphere, creator must know the secret added to this item; **Cost** 7,500 gp
