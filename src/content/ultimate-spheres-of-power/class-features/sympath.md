+ Class Equipment
The following magical items are especially appropriate for eliciters.

#### Ardent Ring [TS:WAT]
**Aura** moderate Mind; **CL** 8th
**Slot** ring; **Price** 10,000 gp (lesser) or 24,000 gp (greater); **Weight** 1/2 lbs.

These elaborate rings come in a variety of styles, each of which helps the wearer constantly feel a particular emotion. When worn by a creature with the emotions class feature, a lesser ardent ring grants them access to a single minor emotion power, chosen when the ring is made. At 5th level, the creature also gains the lesser emotion power of the same emotion as the minor power. A greater ardent ring functions the same as the lesser ring, but at 8th and 11th levels, the wearer also gains the greater and master emotion powers of the same emotion as the minor power, respectively. A creature cannot benefit from more than one ardent ring at a time, and must wear one for 24 hours before accessing its powers.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Apparatus, Mind sphere (Expanded Charm, Powerful Charm), creator must have the emotions ability, creator must have the emotion powers being added to the ring; **Cost** 5,000 gp (lesser) or 12,000 gp (greater)

#### Hypnotizing Pendant [TS:WAT]
**Aura** moderate Mind; **CL** 10th
**Slot** none; **Price** 25,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This slender golden pendant is eye-catching and easy to follow as it moves. Three times per day, a character with the hypnotism class feature can swing this pendant in one hand to activate their hypnotism as a swift action instead of a standard action. A character can only use one hypnotizing pendant per day.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Marvelous Item, Mind sphere (Subtlety), creator must have the hypnotism ability; **Cost** 12,500 gp

#### Snap Out Of It [TS:WAT]
**Aura** faint Mind; **CL** 5th
**Slot** hands; **Price** 16,000 gp; **Weight** 1/10 lbs.

These silk gloves produce a surprisingly loud sound when the user snaps their fingers. As an immediate action, a creature with the liberate hypnotism ability can snap their fingers and spend one use of their hypnotism ability to attempt to free one ally within 30 feet from one mind-affecting ability, even if that ability is not harmful. This otherwise functions as the liberate hypnotism.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Apparatus, Mind sphere (Mind Shield (charm)), creator must have the liberate hypnotism ability; **Cost** 8,000 gp
