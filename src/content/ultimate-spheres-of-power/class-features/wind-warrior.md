+ Class Equipment
The following magical items are especially appropriate for elementalists.

++++ Elemental-Attuning Pendant [TS:WAT]
**Aura** moderate Destruction and Protection; **CL** 7th
**Slot** neck; **Price** 22,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

This golden pendant has four gems on it. When worn by a creature with the elemental defense ability, that creature may spend an immediate action to reduce the energy resistance of three of their elemental defense's types to 0, and increase the remaining type of energy resistance by the amount reduced. This never becomes immunity. This change lasts for one full round, after which the enhanced energy resistance returns to its normal amount and the other energy resistances become 5 less than they were before the user activated this pendant. This change lasts until the pendant is used again or until 1 hour passes; if the hour passes, then the wearer's elemental defense ability returns to its normal amounts for each energy resistance.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Marvelous Item, Destruction sphere, Protection sphere (Energy Resistance (aegis, ward)), creator must have the elemental defense ability; **Cost** 11,000 gp

++++ Energy-Weaving Gauntlets [TS:WAT]
**Aura** moderate Destruction; **CL** 8th
**Slot** hands; **Price** 16,000 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lbs.

These fine cloth gloves are decorated with runes of power. When a character with the favored element class feature uses a destructive blast with their favored element, they may replace any effect except changing the damage dice with the non-dice effects of another blast type they have that is also associated with their favored elements, although they must pay any additional spell point costs (if necessary). For example, an elementalist with the acid and fire favored elements could replace the penalty on Fortitude saves caused by the Blistering Blast (blast type) talent with the lingering acidic damage from the Acid Blast (blast type) talent. If the wearer of these gloves is using the Admixture talent, they may replace one or both additional effects, subject to the limitations described above.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Apparatus, Destruction sphere (Admixture), creator must have the favored element ability; **Cost** 8,000 gp

++++ Nature-Walking Boots [TS:WAT]
**Aura** faint Nature and Protection; **CL** 5th
**Slot** feet; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 2 lbs.

These boots are rugged and durable, suitable for use in all types of different environments. While wearing these, a creature with the elemental movement class feature gains 5 additional energy resistance against the opposite of their form of movement while using that form of movement. Swimming improves fire resistance, flying improves acid resistance, burrowing improves electricity resistance, and land movement improves cold resistance.

**Construction Requirements**
Craft Apparatus, Nature sphere, Protection sphere (Energy Resistance (aegis, ward)), creator must have the elemental movement ability; **Cost** 4,000 gp
