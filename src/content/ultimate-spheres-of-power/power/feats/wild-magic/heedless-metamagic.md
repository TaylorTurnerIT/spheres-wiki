id: heedless-metamagic
name: Heedless Metamagic
tags:
  - wild-magic
tier: feat
---
**Prerequisites:** 1 or more metamagic feats.

**Benefit:** When applying metamagic feats to a spell or sphere effect, you ignore the casting time increase from using a metamagic feat by increasing your wild magic chance by 50% per metamagic feat whose casting time increase you ignore.
