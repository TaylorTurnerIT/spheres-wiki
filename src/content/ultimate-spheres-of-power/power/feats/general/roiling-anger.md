*Our anger is a glorious wave which rolls and churns like the ocean's tide.*

**Prerequisites:** Ki pool class feature, rage class feature.

**Benefit:** Whenever you spend one or more ki points while raging, you may extend the duration of any rage-like effect you are under by 1 round (such as the rage spell or the Disciple of Chaos's practiced anger ability) or maintain your barbarian rage without expending a round of rage at the start of your next turn.

Your ki pool increases by 1. If your rage ability is measured in rounds per day, you can rage for 3 additional rounds per day.

**Note:** The barbarian's rage normally prevents abilities that require "patience or concentration". Ki powers do not technically require either although some GMs might limit a barbarian from using their ki powers during a rage; if so, the Roiling Anger feat allows you to use ki powers while raging if you could not already.
