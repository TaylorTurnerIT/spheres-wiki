*The universe said to slow down, and stop. But your feet pressed on, faster and more determined.*

**Prerequisites:** Base attack bonus +3 or monk level 3rd, nonlawful.

**Benefit:** You ignore the effects of difficult terrain while moving. Whenever you take a move action to move and move through difficult terrain, you increase each of your movement speeds by +10 until the end of your next turn (and may benefit from the increased movement speed immediately).

Whenever you make an attack action, a target struck by your attack must make a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or treat all movement they make as moving through difficult terrain for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting effect. A creature that successfully saves against this effect cannot be affected for 24 hours.

**Special:** If you are a martial practitioner and can maintain martial focus (using the *Spheres of Might* system), you may use your practitioner modifier instead of your Wisdom modifier when determining the effects of Hubris Style.
