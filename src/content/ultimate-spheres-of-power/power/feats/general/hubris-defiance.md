*To defy and reject the orders of the universe and carve your own way, as the first water created the rivers and then the canyons. Your steps march forward, putting down those that would tell you to turn aside.*

**Prerequisites:** Hubris Style, base attack bonus +5 or monk level 5th, nonlawful.

**Benefit:** Whenever you would make a saving throw against a compulsion effect or any effect that would attempt to dictate how you act (such as confusion, a charm person or command spell, the Fate sphere Stricture consecration, etc.), you may roll twice and take the better result on your saving throw. If the effect would not already offer a saving throw, you are granted a saving throw to negate its effects.

If you succeeded on a saving throw against a compulsion effect or effect that would attempt to dictate how you act, you roll twice and take the better result on all attack rolls, skill checks, and ability checks made against the source of that effect for 1 round.
