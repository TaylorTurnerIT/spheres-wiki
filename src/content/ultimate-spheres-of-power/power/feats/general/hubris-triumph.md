*Once all that would stop you, slow you down, or tell you to move aside have been surpassed, your achievement is where you stand and to stand proud. Now you show others.*

**Prerequisites:** Hubris Style, Hubris Defiance, base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th, nonlawful.

**Benefit:** Creatures which succeed on their saving throw against Hubris Style's attack action are no longer immune to its effects for 24 hours. Whenever you use Hubris Style's attack action, instead of having the target treat all movement they make as difficult terrain, you may apply one of the following conditions instead:

- For 1 round, the target does not treat any other creature as its ally, cannot benefit from teamwork feats or flanking bonuses with other creatures, and must attempt saving throws against all effects that allow for one (except their own).
- For 1 round, all damage the target would deal is nonlethal; this has no effects on damage dealt to creatures that cannot take nonlethal damage (such as constructs or undead).
- For 1 round, the target cannot move further away from you, or any creature it treats as an enemy, without succeeding on an additional Will saving throw.

Each of these effects are mind-affecting effects and a successful Will saving throw negates their effects.

In addition, you gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls, damage rolls, skill checks, and ability checks against any creature which failed its saving throw against your Hubris Style attack action or created an effect which you made a successful saving throw against that Hubris Defiance protects against.
