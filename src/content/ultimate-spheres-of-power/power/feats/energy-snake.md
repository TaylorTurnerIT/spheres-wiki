---
name: "Energy Snake"
sphere: destruction
tags: ["combat"]
tier: feat
---

**Prerequisites:** Destruction sphere (Energy Sphere (blast shape)).

**Benefit:** When using the Energy Sphere blast shape, you may spend 1 spell point to form the energy sphere into a serpent, which grows with every target it damages. Whenever the energy sphere successfully deals damage to a target, its size increases by 1 5-foot square. When moving the energy sphere each round, its squares must be arranged contiguously in an arrangement that follows the movement of the 'head' of the serpent.
