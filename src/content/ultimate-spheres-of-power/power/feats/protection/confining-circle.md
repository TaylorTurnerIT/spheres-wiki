name: "Confining Circle"
sphere: protection
tags: []
tier: feat

summary: "Invert a Repel ward to allow creatures of the chosen alignment to enter freely while requiring a Will save to leave."
---
**Prerequisites:** Protection sphere (Repel Chaos/Evil/Good/Law (ward)).

**Benefit:** When you create a Repel ward, you may invert it, so that creatures of the repelled alignment can enter freely, but must attempt a Will saving throw to leave the warded area.
