**Prerequisites:** Heal 1 rank, Life sphere.

**Benefit:** Whenever you treat deadly wounds, you may use restore on the target. This costs one less spell point than normal. Even if you may use treat deadly wounds on a target multiple times in a day, this ability may only be used on a target once per day.
