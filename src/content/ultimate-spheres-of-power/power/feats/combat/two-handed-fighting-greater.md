**Prerequisite:** Str 19, Power Attack, Two-Handed Fighting, Improved Two-Handed Fighting, base attack bonus +11. 

**Benefit:** When using the Two-Handed Fighting feat, increase your Strength bonus on damage rolls by an additional 1/2-times your Strength bonus on your first attack made each round (normally 3-times your Strength bonus). 

In addition, whenever you make a sweeping strike, you may choose up to 3 spaces. When dealing damage with a sweeping strike, you now also add bonus damage from class features, feats, spells, and other effects to the damage dealt (but not bonus damage dice, such as from a weapon with the flaming special weapon ability). 

**Example:** Chandou the Greatspear has the weapon training class feature and the favored enemy class feature. When making a sweeping strike, Chandou would add his weapon training bonus to the damage dealt with a sweeping strike and would add his favored enemy bonus as bonus damage against creatures which his favored enemy class feature would apply against.
