sphere: illusion
tags:
  - combat

summary: "Suppress two decoys to use the Mage Feint talent as a swift action, or dismiss Weird Defense to use it without an action."
---

**Prerequisites:** Illusion sphere (Decoy (glamer), Mage Feint), base attack bonus +3.

**Benefit:** You may suppress two decoys surrounding you until the start of your next turn to use the Mage Feint talent as a swift action.

**Special:** If you possess the Weird Defense (Combat) feat and have its effect active, you can dismiss the effect instead of suppressing two decoys to use Mage Feint talent; using Mage Feint in this fashion does not require any additional actions.
