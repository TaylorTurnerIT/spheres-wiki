---
id: "long-step"
name: "Long Step"
tier: basic
tags: ["fey-blessing"]
---

For the duration of the fey-link, you can teleport up to 10 feet as a move action. This movement does not provoke an attack of opportunity and you must have line of sight to your destination.

This distance increases by 5 feet at 4th caster level and every 2 caster levels thereafter. If you possess Tree Meld, while melded to a tree, you may move to another tree within close range as a move action or medium range as a standard action. You may only move to trees in this fashion once per 1d4 rounds.
