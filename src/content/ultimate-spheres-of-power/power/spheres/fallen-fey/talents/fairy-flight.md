---
id: "fairy-flight"
name: "Fairy Flight"
tier: basic
tags: ["fey-blessing"]
---

You sprout wings (which can appear as insectile, feathered, or made of leaves at the caster's choice); you take no damage when falling and may glide, moving with a speed of 30 feet with maneuverability (poor), but falling 1 feet for every 5 feet traveled. At 5th caster level, you gain a fly speed of 30 feet with maneuverability (poor). This improves by 5 feet and one maneuverability category (to a maximum of perfect) for each 5 additional caster levels. This is a polymorph effect.
