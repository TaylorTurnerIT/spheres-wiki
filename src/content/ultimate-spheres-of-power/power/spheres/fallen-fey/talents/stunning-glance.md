---
id: "stunning-glance"
name: "Stunning Glance"
tier: basic
tags: ["fey-blessing"]
---

You gain the ability to, as a standard action, gaze at a target within 30 feet which must succeed at a Fortitude save or be stunned for one round. Once a creature fails a save vs. this ability, it is immune to your use of it for 24 hours.

(This talent has been modified from the original version found in Player's Guide to Skybourne.)
