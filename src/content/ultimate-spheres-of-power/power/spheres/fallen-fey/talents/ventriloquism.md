---
id: "ventriloquism"
name: "Ventriloquism"
tier: basic
tags: ["fey-blessing", "utility"]
---

As a free action once per round, you can make your voice (or any sound that you can normally make vocally) seem to issue from any location within medium range, or long range if you spend an additional spell point when activating this fey-blessing.

You can speak in any language you know. Any sonic-based abilities granted by this sphere (such as those granted by Beckoning Call and Enchanting Music) can be used through this effect, counting the chosen location as your location for the purpose of determining area of effect. Anyone who hears the sound and rolls a successful Will save recognizes it as illusory (but still hears it, though may choose not to be affected by any abilities used as part of it).
