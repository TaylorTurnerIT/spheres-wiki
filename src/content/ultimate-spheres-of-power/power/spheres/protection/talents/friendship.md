---
name: "Friendship"
tier: basic
tags: ["aegis"]
---

You may create an aegis that gives a creature special protection from the spell, spell-like, sphere and supernatural abilities of his allies. Whenever one of that creature's allies uses a spell, spell-like, or sphere ability that is subject to spell resistance and covers an area, or a supernatural ability that covers an area, allies with this aegis and their equipment are treated as if they were immune. This aegis does not block beneficial effects from a creature's allies.
