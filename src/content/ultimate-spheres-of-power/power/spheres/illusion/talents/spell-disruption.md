name: "Spell Disruption"
tier: advanced
tags: []

**Prerequisites:** Illusion sphere (Blur (glamer), Manipulate Aura (sensory, magic)), caster level 5th.

You may spend an additional spell point when using Blur to have it also cause spell failure chance equal to its miss chance. This spell failure applies to all spells, sphere effects, spell-like and supernatural abilities that go to, from, or whose line of sight or effect cross the illusion.

**Author's Note:** This talent's spell failure chance only applies to an effect if the granted miss chance would apply under similar circumstances. For both a trick and distorting figment, this spell failure chance applies to effects normally. For a distorting glamer, this spell failure chance only applies against effects made against the target (e.g. single target effects).
