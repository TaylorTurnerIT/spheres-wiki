---
id: wild-magic
name: Wild Magic / Variant Wild Magic
---
Your magic is not entirely stable and can result in a variety of unwanted effects. You have a base wild magic chance of 10% (whenever you cast a spell or magical sphere ability, you have a 10% chance of activating a wild magic event). When you activate a wild magic event, roll as if you had activated a rod of wonder, then add that effect to those you produce with your magic. GMs may always use their own wild magic tables (such as the [Wild Magic](@power:article:about-advanced-magic#wild-magic) tables on this wiki) to determine the nature of a wild magic event.
