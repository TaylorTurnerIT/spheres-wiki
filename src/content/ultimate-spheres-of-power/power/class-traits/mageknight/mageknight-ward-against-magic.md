---
name: "Ward Against Magic"
tags: ["spell-like"]
featureId: mystic-combat-su
requires: "Protection sphere, Resist Magic class feature"
---

You may create a ward that duplicates the effects of your resist magic class feature for all within. You may also create an aegis that duplicates the effects of your resist magic class feature for any creature that bears it. The bonus from your resist magic ability can not stack with itself this way.
