name: "Honed Shadowblade"

requires: "Shadowblade"
---

Objects created with your Shadowstuff Armament feat act as if you had the Surreinforcement feat. Treat your mageknight levels as Illusion caster levels for calculating its effects for your shadowstuff armaments. Once per round as a free action you may cause a creature damaged by one of your weapons created or enhanced by your shadowblade mystic combat to suffer a -1 circumstance penalty to all Perception checks and Will saves against illusions for one minute. This penalty stacks with itself up to 5 times.
