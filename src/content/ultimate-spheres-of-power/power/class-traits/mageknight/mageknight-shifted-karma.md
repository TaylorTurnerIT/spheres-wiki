---
id: "mageknight-shifted-karma"
name: "Shifted Karma"
tags: ["supernatural"]
featureId: mystic-combat-su
requires: "Marked class feature"
---

Whenever you hit an enemy you have marked with a melee weapon or natural weapon, you may give the target a -4 penalty to attack and AC against creatures other than yourself. This penalty lasts until the beginning of your next turn, or you may spend a spellpoint to make it last 1 minute. This ability ends immediately if the mark ends, or if the target successfully hits you with a melee attack.
