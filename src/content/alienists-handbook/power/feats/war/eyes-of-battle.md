name: Eyes Of Battle
sphere: war
dualSphere: divination
tier: feat
summary: "Reduce the casting time of divine talents targeting spaces or creatures within your totem by one step."
---
**Prerequisites:** Divination sphere, War sphere

**Benefit:** Whenever you would use a (divine) talent that targets a space or creature within the area of one of your totems, you may reduce the casting time by 1 step, to a minimum of a swift action.
