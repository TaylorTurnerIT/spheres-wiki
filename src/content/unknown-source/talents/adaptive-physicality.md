**Source:** Unknown (drs)


You may add the following traits to your forms:

**Appealing Visage:** The target gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy and Perform checks, in addition to checks made as an impressive display of skill. This bonus increases by 1 for every 4 caster levels you possess. Additionally, any creature that the target impresses has the numerical effects of the impressed condition increased by +2.

**Assimilate Tool:** Choose a body part that the creature possesses, and a tool or similar object that they are holding (this excludes weapons). The object melds into the limb itself, conferring its benefits without having to hold the object, such as granting an arm the benefits of a crowbar, a quill into a finger, or a pair of swim fins to feet. This does not allow the creature to ignore the weight or required hands that the tool may require. An assimilated tool cannot be disarmed, but can be damaged and sundered as normal.
If the object is specifically used with an organ (such as a magnifying glass or spyglass being usable with an eye), you can instead meld the object into that organ for the duration. You may grant this trait multiple times.

**Hyperexpressive Biology:** The target's body becomes hypersensitive to their own emotions, and changes to reflect them (usually by their skin becoming hued with another color). This reduces the DC of Sense Motive checks (except the DC to feint them) made against the target (including opposed checks) by 4; this increases to 8 for Sense Motive checks made to sense the emotion of the creature.
