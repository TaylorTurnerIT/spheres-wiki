**Source:** Unknown (rw-hb)


Great diviners often learn a number of wide-reaching but imprecise tools which can map entire regions and battlefields, grant precise directions, and learn about the terrain.

**Prerequisites:** Divination sphere, caster level 5th.

You gain a number of new (divine) abilities. The abilities granted by this talent are improved if you possess certain alternate divinations. These additional options are noted as expanded options. If you possess the Divination sphere Greater Divine talent, the range of the sphere effects granted by this advanced talent increase by 1 step (1 mile to 10 miles per caster level, 10 miles to 100 miles per caster level).
